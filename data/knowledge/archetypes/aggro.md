# Aggro

## Overview

Aggro decks aim to win through combat damage as quickly as possible. While the 40-life format and multiplayer nature of Commander makes traditional aggro challenging, explosive starts and strategic threat deployment can overwhelm opponents before they establish defenses.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Deploy efficient threats early, apply pressure consistently, and close games before opponents stabilize. Accept that you'll likely eliminate one player at a time rather than the whole table simultaneously.

**Key Elements:**
- Low mana curve (average CMC 2.5-3.0)
- High creature density
- Haste enablers for immediate pressure
- Damage multipliers and extra combat steps
- Targeted removal for blockers
- Card advantage that doesn't sacrifice tempo

## Building Tips

1. **Curve matters more than other archetypes** — You need 1-2-3 drop sequences
2. **Include 8-10 haste sources** — Speed is everything
3. **Run efficient removal** — [[Swords to Plowshares]], [[Path to Exile]], [[Chaos Warp]]
4. **Plan for card disadvantage** — Aggro naturally runs out of gas
5. **Consider damage doublers carefully** — They help opponents too
6. **Pack some board wipe protection** — [[Flawless Maneuver]], [[Teferi's Protection]]

## Common Pitfalls

- **Overextending into board wipes** — Hold back some threats
- **Targeting wrong player first** — Attack the combo player, not the life gainer
- **Ignoring card draw** — Pure aggro runs out of cards
- **Too many expensive finishers** — You need early pressure
- **No protection** — One Wrath effect shouldn't end your game

## Synergy Partners

Aggro pairs well with:
- **Tokens** — Go-wide overwhelms blockers
- **Equipment** — Voltron backup plan
- **Extra combats** — More damage faster
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice aggressive creatures for value

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Slow combo decks
- Greedy mana bases
- Decks without early interaction

**Weak against:**
- Board wipe heavy decks
- Pillowfort strategies
- Lifegain decks
- Multiple control players

**Bracket 1-2:** Very viable, games are shorter
**Bracket 3-4:** Challenging, need card advantage plan
**cEDH:** Rare but exists (fast Najeela, Winota)
