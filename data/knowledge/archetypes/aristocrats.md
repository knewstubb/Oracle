# Aristocrats

## Overview

Aristocrats is a sacrifice-based strategy that extracts value from creatures dying. Named after Cartel Aristocrat, the archetype turns expendable creatures into card advantage, mana, damage, and board control. Every death fuels the engine.

## Core Strategy

The goal is to create a self-sustaining loop:
1. Generate expendable creatures (tokens or recursive creatures)
2. Sacrifice them through free sacrifice outlets
3. Trigger payoffs that drain opponents or generate advantage
4. Repeat until opponents are dead

## The Three-Legged Stool

Aristocrats decks require balance between three essential components. Missing any one leg causes the deck to stumble.

### 1. Sacrifice Outlets

Cards that let you sacrifice creatures at low or no cost.

**Free Sacrifice Outlets** (most valuable)
- Viscera Seer — Scry 1
- Carrion Feeder — +1/+1 counter
- Phyrexian Altar — Produces mana
- Ashnod's Altar — Produces colorless mana
- Altar of Dementia — Mills opponents
- Goblin Bombardment — Deals 1 damage
- Spawning Pit — Makes tokens

**Paid Outlets** (still useful)
- Priest of Forgotten Gods — 2 creatures, but draws and drains
- Diabolic Intent — Tutor effect
- Greater Good — Draw based on power

### 2. Sacrifice Fodder

Creatures you don't mind losing repeatedly.

**Token Generators**
- Bitterblossom — Repeatable 1/1 Faeries
- Ophiomancer — Snake each upkeep if you don't have one
- Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia — Decayed Zombie each end step
- Adeline, Resplendent Cathar — Token per opponent attacked

**Recursive Creatures**
- Gravecrawler — Returns from graveyard for B (needs Zombie)
- Reassembling Skeleton — Returns for 1B
- Bloodghast — Returns on landfall
- Nether Traitor — Returns when another creature dies

**Death Replacement**
- Hallowed Spiritkeeper — Leaves behind Spirit tokens
- Doomed Dissenter — Dies into a 2/2 Zombie

### 3. Death Payoffs

Cards that reward you when creatures die.

**Drain Effects** (primary win condition)
- Blood Artist — Lose 1, gain 1 per creature death
- Zulaport Cutthroat — Opponents lose 1 per your creature
- Cruel Celebrant — Same as Zulaport
- Bastion of Remembrance — Zulaport on an enchantment
- Meathook Massacre — Scalable wipe plus drain engine

**Card Advantage**
- Grim Haruspex — Draw when nontoken creature dies
- Midnight Reaper — Draw and lose 1 life
- Skullclamp — Equip to 1-toughness, draw 2 when it dies
- Species Specialist — Choose type, draw when that type dies

**Mana Generation**
- Pitiless Plunderer — Treasure when creature dies
- Black Market — Charge counters, produce black mana

**Token Generation**
- Pawn of Ulamog — 0/1 Eldrazi Spawn when nontoken dies
- Sifter of Skulls — Same as Pawn
- Open the Graves — 2/2 Zombie when nontoken creature dies

## Key Synergies

### Infinite Loops
Aristocrats often wins through infinite sacrifice loops:

**Gravecrawler + Phyrexian Altar + Zombie**
- Sac Gravecrawler to Altar for B
- Cast Gravecrawler from graveyard for B
- Repeat infinitely with any death trigger

**Karmic Guide + Reveillark + Sac Outlet**
- Sac Karmic Guide
- Sac Reveillark, return Karmic Guide + small creature
- Karmic Guide returns Reveillark
- Repeat infinitely

**Persist Creatures + +1/+1 Counter Source**
- Creature with persist dies, returns with -1/-1 counter
- Counter source (Vizier of Remedies, Grumgully) cancels counter
- Sac again, repeat infinitely

### Non-Infinite Value
Even without going infinite, aristocrats generates overwhelming advantage through repeated triggers. A board with Zulaport + Blood Artist + sac outlet creates 4 life swing per death.

## Building Tips

1. **Balance the stool** — Count sac outlets, fodder, and payoffs separately
2. **Prioritize free outlets** — Paying mana to sacrifice slows you down
3. **Run redundancy** — Multiple Blood Artist effects ensure one sticks
4. **Include protection** — Your payoffs will be targeted
5. **Don't forget removal** — Graveyard hate shuts you down; answer it

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too many payoffs, not enough fodder** — Nothing to sacrifice
- **Relying on expensive outlets** — Can't sacrifice when mana-tight
- **No way to rebuild** — After a board wipe, need ways to restart
- **Forgetting "nontoken" restrictions** — Some payoffs only trigger for real creatures

## Synergy Partners

Aristocrats pairs naturally with:
- **Tokens** — More bodies to sacrifice
- **Reanimator** — Recur creatures for repeated value
- **Tribal (Zombies, Vampires)** — Creature type synergies
- **+1/+1 Counters** — Persist combos, death trigger synergy

## Meta Considerations

- **Power Level**: Scales well from casual to competitive
- **Speed**: Mid-game deck; needs setup before exploding
- **Weakness**: Graveyard hate, Torpor Orb effects, exile-based removal
- **Strength**: Resilient to creature removal (death triggers still happen)
