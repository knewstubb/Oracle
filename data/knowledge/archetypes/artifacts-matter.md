# Artifacts Matter

## Overview

Artifacts-matter strategies leverage artifact synergies beyond just mana rocks — affinity cost reduction, metalcraft thresholds, artifact sacrifice, and artifact ETB triggers. These decks blur the line between artifact-heavy goodstuff and dedicated combo/value engines.

## Core Strategy

Artifacts-matter operates on critical mass:
1. **Artifact density** — High artifact count for synergies
2. **Cost reduction** — Affinity and similar effects
3. **Payoffs** — Effects that scale with artifacts
4. **Recursion** — Bring back artifacts from graveyard

### Artifact Types

Different artifact types enable different strategies:
- **Mana rocks** — Acceleration
- **Equipment** — Creature enhancement
- **Creatures** — Bodies with artifact synergies
- **Tokens** — Thopters, servos, treasures
- **Utility** — Diverse effects

## Building Tips

1. **High artifact count** — 30+ artifacts for consistent synergies
2. **Include mana rocks** — Natural artifact density
3. **Artifact creatures** — Bodies that count as artifacts
4. **Recursion package** — Artifacts will get destroyed
5. **Interaction** — Artifact-based removal options

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too few artifacts** — Synergies need critical mass
2. **Ignoring recursion** — Artifacts get destroyed; bring them back
3. **No win condition** — Artifact value needs a closer
4. **Vulnerable to [[Vandalblast]]** — Artifact hate is common
5. **All expensive artifacts** — Need low-cost artifacts for affinity

## Synergy Partners

Artifacts-matter pairs well with:
- **Treasure** — Treasure tokens are artifacts
- **Equipment** — Equipment are artifacts
- **Sacrifice** — Artifact death triggers
- **Storm** — Cheap artifacts fuel storm
- **Affinity** — Cost reduction synergies

## Meta Considerations

- **Artifact hate** — [[Vandalblast]], [[Collector Ouphe]] are devastating
- **Stony Silence effects** — Shut down activated abilities
- **Graveyard hate** — Hurts recursion strategies
- **Board wipes** — Affect artifact creatures
- **Mass removal** — [[Austere Command]] can target artifacts

## Affinity Strategies

Affinity reduces costs based on artifact count:
- [[Thoughtcast]] — Draw for cheap
- [[Frogmite]] — Free 2/2
- [[Sojourner's Companion]] — Free 4/4
- [[Myr Enforcer]] — Free 4/4

### Maximizing Affinity
- Low-cost artifacts count
- Artifact lands count (be careful of sweepers)
- Treasure/thopter/servo tokens count
- [[Urza's Saga]] makes constructs + fetches 0-1 cost artifacts
