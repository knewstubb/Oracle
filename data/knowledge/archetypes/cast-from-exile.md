# Cast from Exile

## Overview

Cast-from-exile decks exploit the growing number of cards that allow casting spells from exile. By generating value from impulse draw, cascade effects, and exile-matters payoffs, these decks turn a temporary zone into a powerful resource.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Exile isn't just for removal—it's a second hand. Generate consistent value by exiling cards from libraries (yours and opponents') and casting them for additional triggers and card advantage.

**Key Elements:**
- Impulse draw (exile top of library, may cast)
- Cascade and discover effects
- "Play from exile" payoffs
- Treasure generation for off-color costs
- Cards that exile and cast from opponents

## Building Tips

1. **Include treasure generation** — Need mana for off-color spells
2. **Run enough payoffs** — Impulse draw alone isn't enough value
3. **Mind the timing** — Impulse cards have "until end of turn" limits
4. **Don't overload on cascade** — Too random can backfire
5. **Include real card draw too** — Impulse isn't the same as drawing

## Common Pitfalls

- **Missing exile timing windows** — "Until your next end step" matters
- **No mana for exiled cards** — Can't cast what you can't afford
- **Too few payoffs** — Need rewards for the extra steps
- **Cascade into wraths** — Stack your deck properly for cascade
- **Overvaluing opponents' cards** — Your deck should still function

## Synergy Partners

Cast-from-exile pairs well with:
- **Treasure** — Fixes mana for any exiled card
- **Spellslinger** — Many exile effects are instants/sorceries
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice the tokens you create

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Midrange decks (card advantage)
- Creature decks (value engine)

**Weak against:**
- Fast combo (too slow)
- Hand attack (less to exile)

**Bracket 1-2:** Good value engine
**Bracket 3-4:** Prosper is high-tier casual
**cEDH:** Not competitive
