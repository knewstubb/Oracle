# Combo

## Overview

Combo decks aim to assemble specific card combinations that win the game immediately or create insurmountable advantages. In Commander, combo serves as a way to definitively end games that might otherwise stall indefinitely.

## Core Strategy

Combo in Commander requires:
1. **Redundancy** — Multiple paths to win
2. **Protection** — Ways to force combos through interaction
3. **Speed vs resilience trade-off** — Fast combos are fragile; slow combos are interruptible
4. **Tutors** — Finding combo pieces consistently

### Combo Types

- **Infinite** — Unbounded loops (infinite mana, damage, turns)
- **Deterministic** — Guaranteed wins (Thassa's Oracle + empty library)
- **Synergy** — Not infinite but overwhelming (storm, token explosions)

## Building Tips

1. **Multiple combo lines** — Don't rely on single combo; include backups
2. **Overlap pieces** — Cards that work in multiple combos increase consistency
3. **Low curve for Ad Nauseam** — If running Ad Naus, keep average CMC under 2
4. **Interaction balance** — Include some answers, not just combo pieces
5. **Know your speed** — Understand when your deck can win and mulligan accordingly

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Glass cannon** — No backup when combo is disrupted
2. **Telegraphing** — Making combo obvious before going for it
3. **Wrong protection timing** — Using protection too early or not holding it
4. **Greedy keeps** — Keeping hands with combo pieces but no protection
5. **Social contract violations** — Playing high-power combo at casual tables

## Synergy Partners

Combo pairs with:
- **Control shell** — Protect combo with counterspells
- **Stax elements** — Slow opponents while assembling
- **Reanimator** — Recur combo creatures
- **Tutors** — Essential for consistency

## Meta Considerations

- **Interaction density** — More interaction = harder to combo
- **Speed of format** — Fast metas require fast combos
- **Graveyard hate** — Affects recursion-based combos
- **Rule 0** — Infinite combos may not be welcome at all tables
- **Combo awareness** — Experienced players know to hold interaction

## Pod Discussion

Before playing combo, consider:
- Does your group allow infinite combos?
- What's the power level of the table?
- Are wins through combo considered fun by your group?
- Is there a house rule about combo (like infinite combos allowed but not before turn 10)?
