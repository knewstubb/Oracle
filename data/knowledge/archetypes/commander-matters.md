# Commander Matters

## Overview

Commander Matters decks are built around cards that specifically reference the commander as a game object — not just playing a powerful commander, but mechanically interacting with the command zone, commander tax, or "your commander" as a rules concept.

## What Counts as "Commander Matters"

The distinction is subtle but important:

- **Every deck**: Uses a commander that's good for the strategy
- **Commander Matters**: Runs cards that only make sense because they reference "your commander" mechanically

### Examples of Commander Matters Cards

**Command Zone Interaction**
- [[Command Beacon]] — Put your commander into your hand from the command zone
- [[Netherborn Altar]] — Return your commander to hand, bypassing tax
- [[Command Tower]] — Produces mana in your commander's colors

**Free Spells (Commander Control)**
- [[Fierce Guardianship]] — Free counterspell if you control your commander
- [[Deflecting Swat]] — Free redirect if you control your commander
- [[Deadly Rollick]] — Free exile if you control your commander

**Commander Clones**
- [[Sakashima of a Thousand Faces]] — Clone your commander, ignore legend rule
- [[Spark Double]] — Copy your commander with an extra counter
- [[Irenicus's Vile Duplication]] — Flying clone of your commander

**Commander Scaling**
- [[Jeska's Will]] — Scales with your commander's mana value
- [[Commander's Plate]] — Protection from non-identity colors
- [[Blackblade Reforged]] — Power/toughness based on lands

**Cast Triggers**
- [[Command Tower]] — Just kidding, but commanders like [[Kalamax, the Stormsire]] or [[Rionya, Fire Dancer]] care about casting spells

## Why Build Commander Matters

- **Maximize your commander**: If your commander is central to your game plan, lean into it
- **Efficiency**: Free spells and cost reducers are powerful
- **Resilience**: Command zone cheats help with tax accumulation
- **Identity**: Your deck does something unique based on who's in the command zone

## Synergies

- **Voltron**: Commander damage strategies naturally want commander-focused cards
- **Combo commanders**: Keeping your key piece accessible matters
- **High-tax commanders**: If your commander dies often, zone cheats are valuable

## Building Commander Matters

### Commander Selection

Look for commanders that:
- You want to cast repeatedly (value each cast)
- Have high mana value (Jeska's Will gets better)
- Are central to your win condition (worth protecting)

### Card Selection

Prioritize cards that:
1. Reference "your commander" in rules text
2. Care about the command zone
3. Scale with commander mana value
4. Become free/cheaper with commander in play

### Watch Out For

- **Dead cards**: If your commander is tucked or you can't cast it, these cards are blanks
- **Over-investment**: Don't run bad cards just because they say "commander"
- **Diminishing returns**: You only have one commander; too many references dilute your deck

## Common Commanders

Commanders that reward the "commander matters" approach:

- [[Reyhan, Last of the Abzan]] — Counters move when commander dies
- [[Kalamax, the Stormsire]] — Copies spells, wants to stay on board
- [[Rionya, Fire Dancer]] — Creates copies based on spells cast
- [[Prossh, Skyraider of Kher]] — Creates tokens on cast from command zone
