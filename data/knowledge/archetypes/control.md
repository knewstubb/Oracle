# Control

## Overview

Control decks aim to manage the game through removal, counterspells, and card advantage until they can deploy a game-winning threat. In multiplayer Commander, this requires adapting traditional 1v1 control principles to handle three opponents while managing resources efficiently.

## Core Strategy

Control in Commander operates differently than 60-card formats:
- Cannot 1-for-1 trade with three opponents indefinitely
- Must leverage multiplayer dynamics (let threats fight each other)
- Board wipes become essential over spot removal
- Card advantage must be overwhelming to keep up

### The Control Gameplan

1. **Early game** — Develop mana, deploy card draw engines
2. **Mid game** — Answer must-deal-with threats, wipe boards as needed
3. **Late game** — Establish overwhelming advantage and close

### Counter Magic Philosophy

In multiplayer, you cannot counter everything. Counter:
- Cards that will kill you
- Cards that enable infinite combos
- Cards that stop you from winning
- Cards that no one else can answer

Let lesser threats resolve and be dealt with by others.

## Building Tips

1. **Threat assessment** — Learn to identify real threats vs scary-looking creatures
2. **Political play** — Let opponents answer each other when possible
3. **Patience** — Don't counter everything; save for critical moments
4. **Win condition focus** — Have a clear plan to close games
5. **Draw engines first** — Establish card advantage before disruption

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Counter everything** — Running out of gas trying to stop all threats
2. **No win condition** — Controlling the game with no way to close
3. **Making enemies** — Political cost of constantly countering one player
4. **Slow clock** — Games drag when you can't finish
5. **Tap-out vulnerability** — Leaving shields down at wrong time

## Synergy Partners

Control pairs well with:
- **Combo finishers** — [[Thassa's Oracle]] + [[Demonic Consultation]]
- **Stax pieces** — Light stax to slow opponents
- **Reanimator** — Use opponents' creatures against them
- **Superfriends** — Planeswalkers as card advantage
- **Blink** — ETB value with protective elements

## Meta Considerations

- **Fast aggro** — Control struggles against three aggressive decks
- **Combo density** — More combo = more counters needed
- **Counterspell wars** — Blue-heavy metas require uncounterable spells
- **Creature-light metas** — Board wipes become dead cards
