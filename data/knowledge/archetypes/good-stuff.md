# Good Stuff

## Overview

Good Stuff decks prioritize raw card quality over synergy. Instead of building around a specific theme or your commander's abilities, you fill the deck with the most efficient, powerful cards available in your color identity.

## Philosophy

- **Card quality over synergy**: Each card should be strong on its own
- **Flexible game plan**: Adapt to the board state rather than executing a specific strategy
- **Commander as value engine**: The commander provides card advantage or removal, not a build-around effect
- **Threat density**: Every draw should advance your position

## Why Play Good Stuff?

- **Resilience**: No single piece is critical; losing one card doesn't collapse your strategy
- **Flexibility**: You can pivot between aggression and control based on the table
- **Simplicity**: Decisions are about resource management, not complex synergy chains
- **Power floor**: Even mediocre draws are functional

## Building Good Stuff

### Commander Selection

Look for commanders that:
- Generate card advantage ([[Thrasios, Triton Hero]], [[Tymna the Weaver]])
- Provide removal or interaction ([[Korvold, Fae-Cursed King]], [[Chulane, Teller of Tales]])
- Have low opportunity cost (partners, cheap commanders)

Avoid commanders that demand specific build-arounds.

### Card Selection Criteria

1. **Efficiency**: Low mana cost for high impact
2. **Standalone power**: Works without other pieces
3. **Flexibility**: Modal cards, cards with multiple uses
4. **Universal answers**: Removal that hits multiple card types

### Staples by Category

**Ramp**: [[Sol Ring]], [[Arcane Signet]], [[Nature's Lore]], [[Three Visits]]

**Card Draw**: [[Rhystic Study]], [[Mystic Remora]], [[Sylvan Library]], [[Necropotence]]

**Removal**: [[Swords to Plowshares]], [[Path to Exile]], [[Beast Within]], [[Generous Gift]]

**Board Wipes**: [[Wrath of God]], [[Cyclonic Rift]], [[Toxic Deluge]]

**Win Conditions**: [[Craterhoof Behemoth]], [[Thassa's Oracle]], efficient beaters

## Weaknesses

- **Predictable**: Opponents know what to expect from staples
- **Vulnerable to hate**: Cards like [[Collector Ouphe]] or [[Null Rod]] hit many staples
- **Less exciting**: Some players find optimized decks less interesting
- **Meta-dependent**: "Best cards" vary by playgroup power level

## Good Stuff vs. Synergy

Good Stuff isn't anti-synergy—it's about incidental synergy rather than forced synergy. Your [[Sylvan Library]] works with your [[Sensei's Divining Top]] naturally; you didn't build around that interaction.

## Common Commanders

- [[Kenrith, the Returned King]] — Five-color access to all staples
- [[Thrasios, Triton Hero]] + [[Tymna the Weaver]] — Card advantage in four colors
- [[Chulane, Teller of Tales]] — Value on creature ETBs
- [[Korvold, Fae-Cursed King]] — Draws cards from incidental sacrifices
