# Group Hug

## Overview

Group Hug decks provide symmetrical benefits to all players — extra draws, ramp, and resources — while secretly positioning to win through accumulated hidden advantages, alternate win conditions, or sudden combo finishes. The archetype weaponizes generosity.

## Core Strategy

Group Hug operates on a deceptive premise:
1. **Appear harmless** — Give everyone presents
2. **Accelerate the game** — Speed up everyone's game plan
3. **Hidden advantage** — Your deck is built for the chaos
4. **Sudden win** — Capitalize before opponents realize

### The Political Shield

Group Hug survives through political goodwill:
- Players don't want to kill the player giving them stuff
- Attacking you seems "mean" when you're helping
- You're rarely the biggest visible threat

## Building Tips

1. **Have a real win condition** — Group hug without a finish is kingmaking
2. **Asymmetrical advantage** — Build for the resources you're giving
3. **Don't be too helpful** — Enabling combo decks is dangerous
4. **Political play** — Make deals, build alliances
5. **Sudden shifts** — Ability to pivot from helpful to lethal

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Kingmaking** — Helping someone else win without winning yourself
2. **No win condition** — Just helping everyone do their thing
3. **Enabling combo** — Drawing cards for combo players kills you
4. **Being too passive** — Need to actually play to win
5. **Forgetting to pivot** — Staying in hug mode when you should close

## Synergy Partners

Group Hug pairs well with:
- **Pillowfort** — Protection while hugging
- **Combo finishers** — Sudden win from hugging position
- **Wheels** — Everyone draws, you benefit most
- **Politics** — Natural alliance building
- **Control elements** — Answer threats to your win

## Meta Considerations

- **Combo-heavy metas** — Feeding draw to combo is suicide
- **Aggro metas** — Hug provides protection buffer
- **Removal-light metas** — Your pieces stay in play
- **Political pods** — Group hug thrives
- **Competitive pods** — Group hug is usually too slow

## Political Guidelines

Group Hug is the most political archetype:

**Do:**
- Make deals and honor them
- Be transparent about your gifts
- Help the underdog (within reason)
- Build genuine goodwill
- Have a clear win plan

**Don't:**
- King-make someone else
- Feed the combo player
- Be passive all game
- Forget you're trying to win
- Help one player dominate
