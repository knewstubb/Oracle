# Group Slug

## Overview

Group slug decks punish everyone at the table, including yourself. Unlike group hug (which benefits everyone), group slug damages everyone's life, resources, and board state. The goal is to break parity by being better positioned to survive the pain.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Hurt everyone equally, but position yourself to benefit from the chaos. Use damage doublers, punisher effects, and symmetrical damage to drain the table while you survive or profit.

**Key Elements:**
- Symmetrical damage effects
- Punisher enchantments
- Damage doublers
- Lifegain or damage prevention for yourself
- Fast clocks to end games before you die

## Essential Cards

### Punisher Enchantments
- [[Sulfuric Vortex]] — No lifegain, 2 damage each upkeep
- [[Manabarbs]] — Lands deal damage
- [[Painful Quandary]] — Pay life or discard on spells
- [[Spellshock]] — 2 damage per spell cast
- [[Polluted Bonds]] — Opponents lose life on land drops
- [[Zo-Zu the Punisher]] — Damage on land ETB

### Damage Multipliers
- [[Furnace of Rath]] — Double all damage
- [[Fiery Emancipation]] — Triple your damage
- [[Dictate of the Twin Gods]] — Flash damage doubler
- [[Wound Reflection]] — Opponents lose life lost again
- [[Archfiend of Despair]] — Same as Wound Reflection

### Draw Punishers
- [[Underworld Dreams]] — Damage on opponent draw
- [[Fate Unraveler]] — Same effect on creature
- [[Sheoldred, the Apocalypse]] — You gain, they lose
- [[Ob Nixilis, the Hate-Twisted]] — Draw = damage

### Resource Punishers
- [[Ankh of Mishra]] — Land drops hurt
- [[Citadel of Pain]] — Untapped lands hurt
- [[Mogis, God of Slaughter]] — Sacrifice or take 2
- [[Havoc Festival]] — Half life each upkeep
- [[Descent into Avernus]] — Treasures but damage

### Breaking Parity
- [[Personal Sanctuary]] — No damage on your turn
- [[Mark of Asylum]] — Prevent damage to your creatures
- [[Exquisite Blood]] — Convert their loss to your gain
- [[Vilis, Broker of Blood]] — Draw from your life loss

## Building Tips

1. **Include ways to break parity** — You need to survive too
2. **Run fast clocks** — Games must end, not stall
3. **Don't rely on one enchantment** — Removal exists
4. **Include spot removal** — Still need interaction
5. **Communicate with table** — Politics can help survive

## Common Pitfalls

- **Hurting yourself as much as opponents** — You're outnumbered 3-to-1
- **No way to close games** — Damage needs a finisher
- **Drawing too much hate** — Politics matter
- **Symmetrical effects benefiting opponents more** — Damage doublers help their creatures too

## Synergy Partners

Group slug pairs well with:
- **Lifegain** — Offset your own damage
- **Wheels** — [[Nekusar]] combines naturally
- **Aristocrats** — Death triggers from global damage

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Lifegain decks (can't keep up)
- Slow decks (die to accumulated damage)

**Weak against:**
- Fast combo (ignores life total)
- Aggro (can race you)
- Enchantment removal (shuts you down)

**Bracket 1-2:** Powerful, accelerates games
**Bracket 3-4:** Viable, but draw hate
**cEDH:** Not competitive
