# Lands Matter

## Overview

Lands matter strategies leverage lands as a primary resource beyond just mana production. Through landfall triggers, land recursion, and land-based win conditions, these decks turn the most reliable card type in Magic into a powerful engine.

## Core Strategy

Lands matter decks exploit several unique properties:
- Lands are harder to interact with than other permanents
- Land drops happen every turn naturally
- Extra land drops multiply triggers exponentially
- Graveyard lands can be replayed repeatedly

### The Landfall Engine

1. **Extra land drops** — Play multiple lands per turn
2. **Landfall payoffs** — Effects that trigger on land entering
3. **Land recursion** — Replay lands from graveyard
4. **Sacrifice synergies** — Fetch lands, cycling lands, sac outlets

## Building Tips

1. **Higher land count** — Run 38-42 lands; they're your engine
2. **Fetchland priority** — Even budget fetches trigger twice
3. **Balance payoffs and enablers** — Need both to function
4. **Include land tutors** — [[Expedition Map]], [[Crop Rotation]] find key lands
5. **Protect Crucible effects** — They're removal magnets

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too few lands** — Cutting lands in a lands-matter deck defeats the purpose
2. **No recursion** — Without Crucible effects, fetch lands are one-shots
3. **All payoffs, no setup** — Landfall does nothing without extra land drops
4. **Vulnerable to land destruction** — [[Blood Moon]], [[Armageddon]] devastate
5. **Slow starts** — Need early ramp to get the engine running

## Synergy Partners

Lands matter pairs well with:
- **Tokens** — [[Scute Swarm]], [[Avenger of Zendikar]]
- **Elementals** — Omnath variants, [[Risen Reef]]
- **Graveyard strategies** — Land dredge synergies
- **Aristocrats** — [[Tireless Tracker]] clues, [[Zuran Orb]] sac synergies
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Avenger of Zendikar]] plant tokens

## Meta Considerations

- **Land destruction** — [[Blood Moon]], [[Back to Basics]], [[Ruination]] are devastating
- **Graveyard hate** — Stops land recursion plans
- **Fast combo** — Landfall decks need time to build up
- **Stax** — [[Root Maze]], [[Thalia, Heretic Cathar]] slow lands entering
