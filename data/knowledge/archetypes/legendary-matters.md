# Legendary Matters

## Overview

Legendary-matters decks leverage the legendary supertype for synergies and payoffs. With the explosion of legendary creatures and "historic" support, this archetype has become increasingly viable.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Every card in your deck is already legendary (your commander), so extend that theme with legendary payoffs and a high density of legendary permanents.

**Key Elements:**
- High legendary creature density
- Historic synergies (legendary, artifact, saga)
- Legends-matter payoffs
- Protection for key legendary permanents
- Often overlaps with tribal or voltron themes

## Building Tips

1. **Balance legendary density** — Not every card needs to be legendary
2. **Include legendary lands** — Count as legendary but don't clog creature slots
3. **Run mass recursion** — [[Primevals' Glorious Rebirth]] is a blowout
4. **Consider historic overlap** — Artifacts and sagas also trigger historic
5. **Protect key legends** — They're often removal magnets

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too many legends, not enough support** — Need payoffs not just legendary cards
- **Legendary land count too high** — Can't have multiples
- **Ignoring removal** — Still need interaction
- **Overcounting commander** — Your commander is always legendary already

## Synergy Partners

Legendary-matters pairs well with:
- **Equipment** — Many great legendary equipment
- **Blink** — Re-trigger ETB legends
- **Reanimator** — Legends in graveyard fuel recursion

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Fair midrange (higher card quality)
- Creature-focused decks

**Weak against:**
- Combo (doesn't matter what's legendary)
- Heavy removal decks

**Bracket 1-2:** Strong, legendary cards are often powerful
**Bracket 3-4:** Solid, Jodah and Sisay are popular
**cEDH:** Sisay combo exists
