# Lifegain

## Overview

Lifegain decks use life total increases as a resource and win condition rather than just survival. By converting lifegain triggers into card advantage, creature power, or direct damage, these decks turn a defensive mechanic into an aggressive strategy.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Gain life incidentally through efficient creatures and effects, then convert those triggers into tangible advantages—cards, tokens, power, or direct damage. The goal isn't to gain 1000 life; it's to profit from every point gained.

**Key Elements:**
- Incidental lifegain (lifelink creatures, triggers)
- Lifegain payoffs (draw, tokens, damage)
- Life-as-resource effects (pay life for cards/mana)
- One-shot win conditions ([[Aetherflux Reservoir]], [[Felidar Sovereign]])
- Protection for key payoffs

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize triggers over big gains** — 10 triggers of 1 life beats one 10-life gain
2. **Include payoffs before enablers** — Lifegain without payoffs is just stalling
3. **Run backup win conditions** — [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] gets removed
4. **Balance colors carefully** — White has enablers, black has payoffs
5. **Protect key permanents** — [[Sanguine Bond]] is a removal magnet
6. **Don't overcommit to the theme** — Still need removal and interaction

## Common Pitfalls

- **Gaining life without payoffs** — 200 life means nothing if you can't win
- **Too many slow enablers** — 1 life per turn doesn't cut it
- **Ignoring the board** — Lifegain doesn't stop combo players
- **No protection for key pieces** — [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] will be targeted
- **Over-relying on upkeep triggers** — [[Felidar Sovereign]] rarely survives

## Synergy Partners

Lifegain pairs well with:
- **Aristocrats** — [[Blood Artist]] effects trigger constantly
- **Tokens** — [[Soul Warden]] + tokens = massive triggers
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Archangel of Thune]] synergy
- **Voltron** — [[Licia, Sanguine Tribune]] gets huge

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Aggro decks (out-race them)
- Burn strategies
- Combat-focused decks

**Weak against:**
- Combo (life total irrelevant)
- Infect (bypasses life)
- Commander damage (21 is 21)
- [[Tainted Remedy]] effects

**Bracket 1-2:** Very strong, life matters more
**Bracket 3-4:** Solid, but combo is faster
**cEDH:** Not viable as primary strategy
