# Mill

## Overview

Mill strategies aim to win by depleting opponents' libraries rather than reducing life totals. In Commander, this means milling 300+ cards across three opponents — a daunting task that requires dedicated enablers or alternative win conditions that leverage an empty library.

## Core Strategy

Mill in Commander typically takes one of three approaches:

1. **Infinite mill combos** — Loop effects to deck all opponents at once
2. **Mass mill effects** — High-volume mill that scales with Commander
3. **Self-mill with payoffs** — Use your own graveyard, mill opponents incidentally

### The 300-Card Problem

Milling 100+ cards per opponent is inefficient without:
- Effects that scale (mill equal to cards in graveyard)
- Infinite combos
- Alt-win conditions ([[Thassa's Oracle]], [[Laboratory Maniac]])

## Building Tips

1. **Include win conditions beyond mill** — Pure mill is slow; have combo finish or [[Thassa's Oracle]]
2. **Consider opponent graveyards** — Mill feeds reanimator decks; include graveyard hate
3. **Self-mill backup** — If you can't mill opponents, mill yourself to win
4. **Scaling effects** — Cards that get better as graveyards fill
5. **Interaction** — Need counterspells to protect combos

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Feeding opponent strategies** — Milling a reanimator deck is helping them
2. **No win condition** — Milling without a closer drags games
3. **Single target focus** — Milling one player makes you their enemy
4. **Shuffle effects** — [[Eldrazi titans]], [[Nexus of Fate]] reset libraries
5. **Slow clock** — Without combo, mill is often too slow

## Synergy Partners

Mill pairs well with:
- **Zombie tribal** — Many zombies care about graveyards
- **Reanimator** — Use opponents' milled creatures
- **Control shell** — Protect mill engine
- **Self-mill combo** — [[Thassa's Oracle]] wins
- **Wheels** — Force draws to trigger mill

## Meta Considerations

- **Graveyard decks** — Milling opponents who want cards in graveyard is counterproductive
- **Shuffle effects** — Eldrazi titans and similar reset progress
- **Lifegain** — Doesn't affect mill plan
- **Fast combo** — Mill is slow; need interaction
- **Exile removal** — Handle Eldrazi titans before milling them

## Special Considerations

### Eldrazi Problem
Cards like [[Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre]] shuffle graveyard into library when milled. Solutions:
- [[Leyline of the Void]] — Exile instead of graveyard
- [[Rest in Peace]] — Same effect
- [[Surgical Extraction]] — Remove in response to trigger
- [[Bojuka Bog]] — Respond by exiling graveyard

### Milling as Incidental
Sometimes mill is incidental to another strategy:
- [[Altar of Dementia]] in aristocrats
- Self-mill in reanimator
- [[Syr Konrad]] in aristocrats
