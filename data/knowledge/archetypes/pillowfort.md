# Pillowfort

## Overview

Pillowfort strategies create defensive barriers that discourage or prevent attacks, allowing the pilot to build advantages unmolested. Rather than killing threats, pillowfort makes attacking unprofitable, redirecting aggression elsewhere until the deck can win through combo, attrition, or slow value.

## Core Strategy

Pillowfort operates on deterrence:
1. **Attack taxes** — Make attacking you expensive
2. **Attack prevention** — Stop attacks entirely
3. **Combat punishment** — Negative consequences for attacking
4. **Political positioning** — Look like a bad target

### Defensive Layers

Multiple overlapping defenses create redundancy:
- Tax effects slow aggression
- Fog effects buy time
- Board wipes reset aggressive boards
- Win conditions that don't require attacking

## Building Tips

1. **Layer defenses** — Multiple taxes compound
2. **Include win conditions** — Pillow without winning = kingmaker
3. **Board wipes** — Reset when defenses fail
4. **Card advantage** — Enchantress package keeps hand full
5. **Political play** — Let others fight while you build

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No win condition** — Stalling without closing is just wasting time
2. **Passive play** — Pure pillow makes you a non-factor
3. **Enchantment removal** — [[Bane of Progress]] destroys everything
4. **Non-combat damage** — Burn/combo ignores pillowfort
5. **Taking too long** — Annoying to play against if games drag

## Synergy Partners

Pillowfort pairs well with:
- **Enchantress** — Draw cards from pillow pieces
- **Lifegain** — Gain life faster than damage
- **Control** — Answer what gets through
- **Combo** — Stall until combo assembled
- **Superfriends** — Protect planeswalkers

## Meta Considerations

- **Combo decks** — Don't care about combat; go right over pillow
- **Burn** — Direct damage ignores attack taxes
- **Enchantment removal** — Heavy removal metas punish pillow
- **Aggressive metas** — Pillow excels against creature combat
- **Politics** — Works best when there are better targets

## Political Notes

Pillowfort is inherently political:
- You're incentivizing attacks elsewhere
- You're often not the biggest threat
- You risk being seen as a "doing nothing" player
- Clear win conditions help maintain engagement

Be active politically: suggest attacks, offer information, make deals. Passive pillow is boring; active pillow is strategic.
