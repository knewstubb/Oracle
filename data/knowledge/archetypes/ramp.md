# Ramp

## Overview

Ramp decks accelerate mana production in early turns to deploy expensive threats ahead of schedule. The strategy capitalizes on Commander's longer games by trading early-game presence for overwhelming late-game power.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Sacrifice early board presence for mana acceleration, then deploy haymakers that dominate the late game. The goal is to hit 7-10+ mana while opponents are still at 4-5.

**Key Elements:**
- Dense early ramp package (10-15 pieces)
- High-impact expensive threats
- Minimal mid-game cards (skip the 4-5 drops)
- Ways to refuel after deploying threats
- Protection for key permanents

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize land ramp over artifacts** — More resilient to removal
2. **Aim for turn 3-4 ramp** — Critical acceleration window
3. **Include card draw tied to lands** — [[Tireless Provisioner]], [[Lotus Cobra]]
4. **Run few 4-5 mana spells** — Skip the mid-game
5. **Protect your investments** — [[Heroic Intervention]], [[Tyvar's Stand]]
6. **Consider land untappers** — [[Seedborn Muse]], [[Wilderness Reclamation]]

## Common Pitfalls

- **All ramp, no payoffs** — Balance acceleration with threats
- **Fragile mana rocks only** — One [[Vandalblast]] shouldn't set you back 5 turns
- **No card advantage** — Running out of gas with 15 mana is embarrassing
- **Too many expensive spells** — Can't cast anything if you draw wrong half
- **Ignoring early threats** — Ramping into death isn't winning

## Synergy Partners

Ramp pairs well with:
- **Landfall** — Extra triggers from acceleration
- **+1/+1 Counters** — Big mana into [[Hydroid Krasis]] style threats
- **Big mana X-spells** — [[Torment of Hailfire]], [[Finale of Devastation]]
- **Lands-matter** — Natural synergy

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Fair midrange decks
- Creature-focused strategies
- Decks without early pressure

**Weak against:**
- Fast aggro decks
- Mass land destruction
- Early counterspell decks
- Stax effects like [[Winter Orb]]

**Bracket 1-2:** Very strong, time to deploy threats
**Bracket 3-4:** Solid, but watch for fast combo
**cEDH:** Green-based fast mana enables many strategies
