# Reanimator

## Overview

Reanimator decks cheat expensive creatures into play by putting them in the graveyard first, then using reanimation spells to bring them to the battlefield at a fraction of their mana cost. Why pay 9 mana for a creature when you can pay 1-4 mana to reanimate it?

## Core Strategy

The Reanimator game plan has three phases:
1. **Fill the graveyard** with high-value creatures (enablers)
2. **Reanimate** those creatures onto the battlefield (reanimation spells)
3. **Win** with overwhelming threats or triggered abilities (payoffs)

Balancing these three components is critical — drawing all reanimation spells with an empty graveyard, or a full graveyard with no way to bring things back, leaves you stuck.

## Reanimator vs Graveyard Decks

Every Reanimator deck is a graveyard deck, but not every graveyard deck is Reanimator:
- **Reanimator**: Specifically focused on returning creatures to the battlefield
- **Graveyard**: Broader strategies including flashback, dredge, Muldrotha-style recursion

## Enablers (Graveyard Fillers)

### Discard/Loot Effects
Put cards from hand to graveyard while drawing fresh cards.

- Faithless Looting — 1 mana, loot 2, flashback
- Frantic Search — Free with untap, loot 2
- Entomb — 1 mana, tutor to graveyard
- Buried Alive — 3 mana, tutor 3 creatures to graveyard
- Unmarked Grave — 2 mana, tutor non-legendary
- Vile Entomber — Creature with Entomb ETB

### Self-Mill
Fill graveyard with library cards.

- Stitcher's Supplier — Mill 3 on ETB and death
- Satyr Wayfinder — Mill 4, land to hand
- Grisly Salvage — Mill 5, creature or land to hand
- Mesmeric Orb — Mill on untap (everyone)
- Altar of Dementia — Sacrifice outlet that mills

### Discard Outlets
Permanents that let you discard repeatedly.

- Putrid Imp — Discard for flying (free)
- Wild Mongrel — Discard for +1/+1 (free)
- Fauna Shaman — Discard creature, tutor creature
- Survival of the Fittest — Same but enchantment

## Reanimation Spells

### Tier 1: Cheap and Efficient
- **Reanimate** (B) — 1 mana, any graveyard, pay life
- **Animate Dead** (1B) — 2 mana, enchantment reanimation
- **Dance of the Dead** (1B) — 2 mana, doesn't untap (quirky)
- **Necromancy** (2B) — 3 mana, instant-speed option
- **Life // Death** (1B) — 2 mana, only your graveyard

### Tier 2: Mid-Cost Value
- **Persist** (2B) — 3 mana, creature gets -1/-1
- **Unburial Rites** (4B) — 5 mana, but flashback 3W
- **Victimize** (2B) — Sac a creature, return 2
- **Dread Return** (2BB) — 4 mana, flashback by saccing 3

### Tier 3: Repeatable/Mass
- **Karmic Guide** (3WW) — ETB reanimate, echo
- **Reveillark** (4W) — Death returns two small creatures
- **Living Death** (3BB) — Mass swap graveyard/battlefield
- **Patriarch's Bidding** — Tribal mass reanimate
- **Rise of the Dark Realms** (7BB) — All creatures all graveyards

### Creature-Based Reanimation
- Apprentice Necromancer — Sac to reanimate, creature has haste
- Doomed Necromancer — Same pattern
- Chainer, Nightmare Adept — Discard outlet + reanimation
- Sheoldred, the Apocalypse — Upkeep reanimate

## Key Synergies

### Self-Recurring Creatures
Some creatures reanimate themselves:
- Bloodghast — Returns on landfall
- Nether Traitor — Returns when creature dies
- Gravecrawler — Returns for B (needs Zombie)
- Reassembling Skeleton — Returns for 1B

### Reanimator + Aristocrats
Sacrifice reanimated creatures, bring them back, repeat:
- Victimize saccing a previously reanimated creature
- Dread Return flashback
- Kokusho, the Evening Star loops

### Reanimator + Blink
Blink reanimation auras to reanimate again:
- Animate Dead + Felidar Guardian
- Necromancy at instant speed

## Building Tips

1. **Balance the triangle** — Enablers, reanimation, targets need equal attention
2. **Include looting** — Discard effects let you dump targets while drawing reanimation
3. **Run self-mill** — More graveyard options = more flexibility
4. **Protect the graveyard** — Run answers to graveyard hate
5. **Goldfish extensively** — Practice the sequencing, it's complex

## Common Pitfalls

- **Wrong side of the deck** — All enablers, no reanimation (or vice versa)
- **No early plays** — Waiting for big reanimation turns with empty board
- **Overcommitting** — Dumping everything, getting yard exiled
- **Ignoring opponent graveyards** — Reanimate targets their bombs too

## Graveyard Hate and Answers

### Hate to Watch For
- Bojuka Bog — Free exile
- Rest in Peace / Leyline of the Void — Continuous exile
- Tormod's Crypt — Instant-speed exile
- Dauthi Voidwalker — Steals your cards

### Protecting Your Yard
- Perpetual Timepiece — Shuffle yard into library
- Witchbane Orb — Hexproof prevents targeted exile
- Orbs of Warding — Same protection
- Return to Nature — Remove the hate piece

## Meta Considerations

- **Power Level**: Highly scalable; Turn 1 Entomb → Turn 2 Jin-Gitaxias is cEDH
- **Speed**: Can be very fast (turn 2-3 bombs) or grindy
- **Weakness**: Graveyard hate, exile effects
- **Strength**: Cheats mana costs, resilient if yard hate removed
