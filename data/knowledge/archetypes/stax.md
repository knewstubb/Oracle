# Stax

## Overview

Stax (derived from [[Smokestack]]) is a control-adjacent strategy that aims to restrict opponents' ability to play the game through taxing effects, resource denial, and symmetry-breaking. Rather than answering threats reactively, stax prevents them from happening.

## Core Strategy

Stax operates by:
1. **Breaking symmetry** — Your stax hurts opponents more than you
2. **Resource denial** — Attacking mana, cards, or board states
3. **Inevitability** — Slowly grinding out advantages under restrictions

### Stax Categories

- **Tax effects** — Make actions cost more ([[Thalia]])
- **Denial effects** — Prevent actions entirely ([[Rule of Law]])
- **Sacrifice effects** — Force resource loss ([[Smokestack]])
- **Static hate** — Continuous punishment ([[Blood Moon]])

## Building Tips

1. **Break symmetry first** — Include ways to play under your own stax
2. **Layered effects** — Multiple small taxes are often better than one big one
3. **Protect key pieces** — Stax creatures are removal targets
4. **Include win conditions** — Don't just stall; have a way to close
5. **Mana efficiency** — Running under mana denial requires low curves

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No clock** — Games that drag on indefinitely with no winner
2. **Self-inflicted locks** — Getting stuck under your own stax
3. **Removal magnets** — Overcommitting stax pieces to removal-heavy board
4. **Social consequences** — Stax is the least-liked archetype; manage politics
5. **Unfun games** — Even winning with stax can feel hollow to some players

## Synergy Partners

Stax pairs well with:
- **Combo finishers** — Win once lock is established
- **Creature decks** — Your creatures work under artifact/spell stax
- **Hatebears** — Creatures that tax while attacking
- **Control shell** — Counter key threats while stax does work

## Meta Considerations

- **Creature-heavy metas** — Stax is good against creature decks
- **Artifact/enchantment removal** — High removal = stax is harder to maintain
- **Fast combo** — Need fast stax or combo can race
- **Social contract** — Many playgroups dislike stax; discuss first

## Rule 0 Discussion

Stax is the most polarizing archetype. Before playing:
- Ask if stax is okay at your table
- Discuss specific cards that might be too oppressive
- Have a backup deck if the table prefers not to face stax
- Be prepared for political targeting; stax draws hate
