# Superfriends

## Overview

Superfriends decks leverage planeswalkers as their primary value engines, building around loyalty abilities, ultimate payoffs, and planeswalker-specific synergies. The archetype aims to stick multiple walkers and protect them until their cumulative value buries opponents.

## Core Strategy

Superfriends operates on a unique axis:
1. **Deployment** — Land planeswalkers consistently
2. **Protection** — Keep walkers alive through blockers, pillowfort, and proliferate
3. **Accumulation** — Multiple walkers generate exponential value
4. **Ultimates** — Build to game-ending ultimate abilities

### Planeswalker Fundamentals

- Planeswalkers are permanents that require protection
- They divert attacks from your life total
- Each plus ability is building toward an ultimate
- Multiple walkers share damage — opponents can't focus one

## Building Tips

1. **Lower walker curve** — 3-4 mana walkers are more consistent than 5-6
2. **Protect the first walker** — The first walker draws all attacks
3. **Include board wipes** — Clear creatures threatening your walkers
4. **Creature blockers** — Token makers and rattlesnakes protect walkers
5. **Don't overload on walkers** — 15-20 is usually the right number

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too many high-cost walkers** — Can't deploy fast enough
2. **No protection** — Walkers die to attacks before ultimating
3. **Ignoring board state** — Focusing walkers while losing to creatures
4. **Not enough win conditions** — Value generation without closing
5. **Telegraphing** — Doubling Season warns opponents

## Synergy Partners

Superfriends pairs well with:
- **Proliferate** — Atraxa builds
- **Pillowfort** — Protect your walkers
- **Control shell** — Answer threats to walkers
- **Tokens** — Walkers make blockers

## Meta Considerations

- **Aggressive metas** — Walkers die to creature swarms
- **Haste** — Can't protect against instant attacks
- **Hexproof commanders** — Voltron ignores your blockers
- **Counterspells** — Countering walkers is devastating
- **Creature removal** — Your blockers get removed, walkers die
