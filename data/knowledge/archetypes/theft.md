# Theft / Clone

## Overview

Theft decks turn opponents' resources against them. Rather than building your own threats, you steal, copy, or borrow the best cards at the table. This creates adaptive, meta-dependent gameplay where your deck's power scales with your opponents'.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Why build your own threats when you can use theirs? Theft decks adapt to any table by leveraging opponents' best cards. You're always playing at the table's power level.

**Key Elements:**
- Permanent theft (creatures, artifacts)
- Temporary theft (Threaten effects)
- Clone effects (copying creatures/spells)
- Cards cast from opponents' decks
- Sacrifice outlets (to keep temporarily stolen permanents)

## Building Tips

1. **Include sacrifice outlets** — Keep [[Threaten]] targets permanently
2. **Run blink effects** — Retrigger ETB theft like [[Gonti]]
3. **Plan for different power levels** — Your deck scales naturally
4. **Include some of your own threats** — Can't steal from an empty board
5. **Pack instant-speed removal** — Still need interaction
6. **Consider graveyard hate** — Opponents want their stuff back

## Common Pitfalls

- **Relying entirely on opponents** — Empty boards mean dead draws
- **No sacrifice outlets** — Threaten effects return creatures
- **Ignoring your own card advantage** — Still need to draw cards
- **Too much temporary theft** — Permanent acquisition is stronger
- **Forgetting tracking** — Keep clear which cards are yours

## Synergy Partners

Theft pairs well with:
- **Blink** — Retrigger ETB theft effects
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice stolen creatures for value
- **Control** — Steal the threats you don't counter
- **Chaos** — [[Confusion in the Ranks]] synergy

## Meta Considerations

**Strong against:**
- Creature-heavy decks (more targets)
- Big mana decks (steal their payoffs)
- Any deck with powerful permanents

**Weak against:**
- Combo (few permanents to steal)
- Token decks (stealing 1/1s doesn't matter)
- Sacrifice decks (they remove their own threats)

**Bracket 1-2:** Scales well, always table-appropriate
**Bracket 3-4:** Strong, steals best threats
**cEDH:** Niche but playable (Xanathar stax)

**Politics:** Theft creates enemies. Be diplomatic about whose stuff you take.
