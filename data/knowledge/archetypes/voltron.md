# Voltron

## Overview

Voltron strategies focus on building a single threat — typically the commander — into an overwhelming force through equipment, auras, and power-boosting effects. The goal is commander damage kills (21 damage from a single commander eliminates a player regardless of life total).

## Core Strategy

Voltron operates on a simple equation: commander + evasion + damage boosting = dead opponents.

### The Three Pillars

1. **Protection** — Keep your commander alive through shroud, hexproof, indestructible, or regeneration
2. **Evasion** — Ensure your commander connects through flying, trample, unblockable, or shadow
3. **Power Boost** — Increase damage through equipment, auras, double strike, and extra combats

### Equipment vs Auras

| Aspect | Equipment | Auras |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Commander death | Stays on board | Goes to graveyard |
| Reusability | Can equip other creatures | Lost if creature dies |
| Initial cost | Equip costs extra mana | Single cast cost |
| Protection | Generally less vulnerable | 2-for-1 risk |

Most voltron decks lean equipment-heavy for resilience, with select auras for efficiency.

## Building Tips

1. **Protection is priority one** — A voltron deck with a dead commander does nothing
2. **Ramp aggressively** — You need mana for commander + equipment + equip costs
3. **Include tutors** — Finding the right equipment at the right time wins games
4. **Secondary threats** — [[Kemba, Kha Regent]], [[Balan, Wandering Knight]] if commander is locked out
5. **Low equipment curve** — High equip costs slow you down; prioritize cheap equips

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All-in on commander** — Getting Darksteel Mutationed or stolen with no backup plan
2. **Too expensive to equip** — Loading up on equipment you can't afford to use
3. **No card advantage** — Voltron is mana-hungry; running out of gas is common
4. **Ignoring interaction** — Need some removal; can't just race every deck
5. **Wrong protection** — Shroud prevents you from equipping; hexproof is usually better

## Synergy Partners

Voltron pairs well with:
- **Equipment tribal** — [[Nahiri, the Lithomancer]], [[Kemba]]
- **Extra combats** — More attacks = faster kills
- **Infect** — Only need 10 damage with Grafted Exoskeleton
- **Control shell** — Protect your investment with counterspells

## Meta Considerations

- **Removal density** — In removal-heavy metas, voltron struggles
- **Speed** — Voltron needs to kill before combo decks go off
- **Sacrifice effects** — [[Grave Pact]], [[Dictate of Erebos]] bypass hexproof
- **Board wipes** — Less devastating than against tokens, but still tempo loss
- **Stax effects** — Equipment often avoids creature-based stax
