# Wheels

## Overview

Wheel strategies center on mass hand cycling effects — cards that force all players to discard and draw new hands. By building around these effects with damage triggers, graveyard synergies, and discard punishment, wheel decks convert symmetrical effects into asymmetrical advantages.

## Core Strategy

Wheels are inherently symmetrical (everyone draws), so the strategy requires:
1. **Breaking symmetry** — Benefit more from drawing/discarding than opponents
2. **Damage payoffs** — Punish opponents for drawing
3. **Graveyard leverage** — Use discarded cards
4. **Resource denial** — Opponents discard key pieces

### Wheel Types

- **Full wheels** — Everyone discards hand, draws 7 ([[Wheel of Fortune]])
- **Partial wheels** — Draw and discard specific numbers ([[Windfall]])
- **Punisher wheels** — Wheels that also damage ([[Wheel of Misfortune]])

## Building Tips

1. **Stack damage triggers** — Multiple Nekusar effects = lethal wheels
2. **Protect yourself** — Wheels give opponents cards; be ready
3. **Graveyard leverage** — Your discards should help you
4. **Lock opponents** — Narset/Notion Thief + wheel is devastating
5. **Include removal** — Opponents will target your pieces

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Helping opponents** — Wheels give everyone fresh hands; be prepared
2. **No damage payoffs** — Just wheeling doesn't win; need the punisher effects
3. **Overextending** — Wheeling away your answers
4. **Missing lock pieces** — Narset + wheel is brutal; missing it is bad
5. **No follow-up** — One wheel isn't enough; chain them

## Synergy Partners

Wheels pair well with:
- **Reanimator** — Wheel into graveyard, reanimate threats
- **Mill** — Many wheels fill graveyards
- **Madness** — Cast discards for madness cost
- **Spellslinger** — Wheels are instants/sorceries
- **Zombies** — Often have graveyard synergies

## Meta Considerations

- **Lifegain** — Counters damage from Nekusar effects
- **Graveyard decks** — Wheels help reanimator opponents
- **Combo decks** — Fresh hands might find combo pieces
- **Fast metas** — Wheels give opponents more gas
- **High removal** — Damage enchantments get removed

## The Notion Thief Play

The single most devastating play in wheels:
1. Cast [[Notion Thief]] or have [[Narset, Parter of Veils]]
2. Cast any wheel effect
3. Result: You draw 28 cards, opponents draw 0-1

This is often game-ending and makes both pieces high-priority targets.
