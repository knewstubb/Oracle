# Abzan (White/Black/Green)

## Overview

Abzan combines efficient removal with recursion and ramp. The wedge excels at +1/+1 counters, aristocrats, and graveyard-based value.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** White (W) + Black (B) + Green (G)
**Clan:** Abzan Houses (Tarkir)
**Philosophy:** Endurance and community, outlast enemies

## Strengths

- **Removal:** Complete removal coverage
- **Recursion:** Best creature recursion
- **Ramp:** Green acceleration
- **+1/+1 Counters:** Strong counter synergies
- **Aristocrats:** Death trigger support

## Weaknesses

- **Card Draw:** Creature-dependent draw
- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Direct Damage:** No burn
- **Spell-Based Strategies:** Creature-focused
