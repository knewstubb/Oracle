# Azorius (White/Blue)

## Overview

Azorius combines white's structure and protection with blue's card advantage and counterspells. The pair excels at control, blink strategies, and flying-based tempo.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** White (W) + Blue (U)
**Guild:** Azorius Senate (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Order through law, control through knowledge

## Strengths

- **Counterspells + Removal:** Best reactive combination
- **Card Draw:** Blue's engines with white's efficient creatures
- **Board Wipes:** Access to both white wraths and blue bounces
- **Protection:** Hexproof, indestructible, counterspells
- **Flying:** Strong aerial presence

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Very limited mana acceleration
- **Graveyard Recursion:** Weak at getting cards back
- **Direct Damage:** No burn effects
- **Artifact/Enchantment Removal:** White carries this alone
