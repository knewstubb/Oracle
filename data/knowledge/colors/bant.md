# Bant (Green/White/Blue)

## Overview

Bant combines ramp and card draw with protection and control. The shard excels at blink, enchantress, and creature-based value strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Green (G) + White (W) + Blue (U)
**Shard:** Bant (Alara)
**Philosophy:** Honor and growth, civilized nature

## Strengths

- **Ramp + Draw:** Green ramp with blue draw
- **Protection:** Counterspells, hexproof, indestructible
- **Blink:** Best ETB abuse colors
- **Enchantress:** Strong enchantment support
- **Removal:** Exile, bounce, naturalize

## Weaknesses

- **Creature Removal:** Relies on exile/bounce
- **Graveyard Recursion:** Minimal options
- **Direct Damage:** No burn
- **Sacrifice Synergies:** No aristocrats
