# Black (Mono-Black)

## Color Identity

Black represents ambition, power, and willingness to pay any price for victory. In Commander, black offers the best tutors, powerful removal, card advantage through life payment, and recursion — at a cost.

## Strengths

### Tutors
Black has the most efficient tutors in the format:
- [[Demonic Tutor]] — 2 mana, any card to hand
- [[Vampiric Tutor]] — 1 mana instant to top
- [[Imperial Seal]] — 1 mana sorcery to top
- [[Diabolic Intent]] — Sacrifice tutor
- [[Grim Tutor]] — 3 mana + life

### Removal
Efficient creature removal:
- [[Fatal Push]] — Conditional but cheap
- [[Go for the Throat]] — 2 mana instant
- [[Infernal Grasp]] — 2 mana, pay 2 life
- [[Deadly Rollick]] — Free with commander
- [[Damnation]] — 4 mana wrath

### Card Advantage
Black draws cards at a cost:
- [[Necropotence]] — Pay life for cards
- [[Phyrexian Arena]] — 1 card, 1 life per turn
- [[Sign in Blood]] — 2 cards, 2 life
- [[Night's Whisper]] — Same effect
- [[Read the Bones]] — Scry + draw

### Recursion
Strong creature recursion:
- [[Reanimate]] — 1 mana, any creature
- [[Animate Dead]] — 2 mana enchantment
- [[Necromancy]] — Flash reanimation
- [[Living Death]] — Mass swap
- [[Yawgmoth's Will]] — One turn graveyard replay

## Weaknesses

### Artifact/Enchantment Removal
Black cannot destroy artifacts or enchantments:
- Must rely on colorless options
- [[Feed the Swarm]] — Enchantment removal with life loss
- [[Pharika's Libation]] — Sacrifice-based
- Limited and inefficient options

### Ramp
Limited acceleration:
- [[Dark Ritual]] — Temporary burst
- [[Cabal Ritual]] — Threshold burst
- [[Cabal Coffers]] — Big mana with swamps
- [[Crypt Ghast]] — Mana doubling
- Relies on artifacts

### Flying/Evasion
Many black creatures are ground-bound:
- Demons provide flying
- Limited evasive options otherwise

## Mono-Black Strategies

### Best Fit
- Aristocrats/Sacrifice
- Reanimator
- Big mana
- Zombie tribal
- Demon tribal

### Good Fit
- Control (with limitations)
- Voltron
- Discard/hand attack
- Life manipulation

### Challenging
- Spellslinger (fewer good options)
- Artifact/enchantment heavy strategies
- Go-wide tokens (limited anthems)

## Mana Base Tips

### Utility Lands
- [[Cabal Coffers]] — Big mana essential
- [[Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth]] — Make everything swamps
- [[Phyrexian Tower]] — Sacrifice for mana
- [[Volrath's Stronghold]] — Creature recursion
- [[Castle Locthwain]] — Card draw

### Swamp Count
With Coffers/Urborg engine, run 28-32 swamps (including fetchable).

### Count
Run 35-37 lands; black has decent mana acceleration.

## Building Tips

1. **Include tutors** — Black's strength; use it
2. **Life is a resource** — Don't be afraid to pay life for advantage
3. **Feed the Swarm** — Your only enchantment answer; include it
4. **Big mana package** — Coffers/Urborg is powerful
5. **Recursion** — Black excels; leverage graveyard

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Ignoring enchantment removal** — [[Feed the Swarm]] is essential
2. **Too greedy with life** — Track life payments carefully
3. **No win conditions** — Need finishers like Gary or X-spells
4. **Skipping recursion** — Black's graveyard tools are excellent
5. **Overextending** — Your wraths hurt you too

## Gary (Gray Merchant)

[[Gray Merchant of Asphodel]] is black's signature finisher:
- ETB drains based on black devotion
- Easily deals 10-20 damage in dedicated decks
- Flicker/reanimate for multiple triggers
- Include in any mono-black deck with permanents
