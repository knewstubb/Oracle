# Boros (Red/White)

## Overview

Boros combines red's aggression with white's protection and anthems. The pair excels at combat-focused strategies, equipment, and aggressive go-wide tactics.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Red (R) + White (W)
**Guild:** Boros Legion (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Justice through action, righteous warfare

## Strengths

- **Combat:** Best combat tricks and extra combats
- **Equipment:** Strong synergies in both colors
- **Removal:** Exile + burn covers most threats
- **Aggression:** Efficient creatures with haste
- **Board Wipes:** Access to both damage and exile wipes

## Weaknesses

- **Card Draw:** Historically weakest pair for draw
- **Ramp:** Very limited acceleration
- **Counterspells:** None
- **Graveyard Interaction:** Minimal recursion
