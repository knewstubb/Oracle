# Dimir (Blue/Black)

## Overview

Dimir combines blue's card manipulation with black's tutors and removal. The pair excels at mill, reanimator, and control strategies with excellent card selection.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Blue (U) + Black (B)
**Guild:** House Dimir (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Knowledge is power, secrets are weapons

## Strengths

- **Card Advantage:** Draw, tutors, and graveyard as resource
- **Removal:** Creature removal + counterspells
- **Mill:** Primary mill color pair
- **Graveyard Synergies:** Self-mill and reanimation
- **Evasion:** Unblockable, flying, shadow

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Very limited acceleration
- **Artifact/Enchantment Removal:** Virtually none
- **Board Wipes:** Limited to creature removal
- **Lifegain:** Minimal sustain options

## Staples

### Card Advantage
- [[Rhystic Study]] — Tax-based draw
- [[Necropotence]] — Life for cards
- [[Mystic Remora]] — Early game draw

### Tutors
- [[Demonic Tutor]] — Best tutor

### Removal
- [[Toxic Deluge]] — Scalable wipe
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Asymmetric bounce
- [[Deadly Rollick]] — Free removal
