# Esper (White/Blue/Black)

## Overview

Esper combines control elements with artifact synergies and flexible removal. The shard excels at control, artifacts, and value-based strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** White (W) + Blue (U) + Black (B)
**Shard:** Esper (Alara)
**Philosophy:** Perfection through artifice, control through knowledge

## Strengths

- **Control:** Counterspells, wraths, removal
- **Card Advantage:** Draw, tutors, recursion
- **Artifact Synergy:** Strong artifact support
- **Removal:** Complete removal coverage
- **Protection:** Hexproof, indestructible, exile

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Very limited acceleration
- **Creature Quality:** Smaller bodies
- **Direct Damage:** No burn effects
- **Land-Based Strategies:** Weak landfall support
