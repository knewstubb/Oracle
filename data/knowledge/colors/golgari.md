# Golgari (Black-Green)

## Color Identity

Golgari represents the cycle of life and death — growth through decay. This color combination excels at graveyard strategies, creature recursion, sacrifice synergies, and attrition-based gameplay. It's the premier color pair for aristocrats and graveyard-focused decks.

## Strengths

### Recursion
Golgari has unmatched creature recursion:
- [[Reanimate]] — 1 mana, any creature
- [[Animate Dead]] — 2 mana enchantment reanimation
- [[Living Death]] — Mass swap
- [[Victimize]] — 2-for-1 reanimation
- [[Eternal Witness]] — Creature-based Regrowth

### Removal
Black adds premium removal to green:
- [[Assassin's Trophy]] — Universal instant removal
- [[Abrupt Decay]] — Uncounterable cheap removal
- [[Toxic Deluge]] — Scalable board wipe
- [[Beast Within]] — Green universal
- [[Maelstrom Pulse]] — Sorcery removal

### Sacrifice Synergies
- [[Blood Artist]] — Drain on death
- [[Zulaport Cutthroat]] — Similar effect
- [[Grave Pact]] — Forces sacrifices
- [[Dictate of Erebos]] — Flash Grave Pact
- [[Viscera Seer]] — Free sac outlet

### Ramp
Green provides excellent ramp:
- Standard land ramp package
- [[Golgari Signet]] — 2-mana rock
- [[Deathrite Shaman]] — Graveyard + mana

## Weaknesses

### Counterspells
No blue means no counterspells:
- Must rely on proactive answers
- [[Imp's Mischief]] — Black redirect, not counter
- [[Veil of Summer]] — Protection, not counter

### Card Draw
Less efficient than blue:
- [[Phyrexian Arena]] — Slow but steady
- [[Necropotence]] — Premium but restrictive
- [[Sign in Blood]] — Efficient but small
- Must rely on creature-death triggers

### Artifact/Enchantment Interaction
Limited options:
- [[Beast Within]] — Green removal
- [[Assassin's Trophy]] — 2-mana universal
- [[Krosan Grip]] — Split second
- [[Nature's Claim]] — 1-mana

## Golgari Strategies

### Excellent Fit
- Aristocrats/Sacrifice
- Reanimator
- Graveyard value
- Attrition/Stax
- Elf tribal

### Good Fit
- Lands-matter (Gitrog)
- +1/+1 counters
- Golgari dredge
- Tokens (sacrifice fodder)

### Challenging
- Spellslinger (creature-focused)
- Control (no counterspells)
- Combo (fewer options)

## Key Synergies

### Aristocrats Engine
- Sacrifice outlet (Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Altar)
- Death trigger (Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat)
- Recursion (Meren, Eternal Witness)
- Fodder (tokens, recursive creatures)

### Reanimator
- Self-mill (Grisly Salvage, Stitcher's Supplier)
- Reanimation (Reanimate, Animate Dead, Living Death)
- Big targets (Craterhoof, Razaketh, Vilis)

### Meren Value
- [[Meren of Clan Nel Toth]] gains experience
- Small creatures generate experience
- Bring back increasing sizes
- Eventually reanimate bombs for free

## Building Tips

1. **Include sacrifice outlets** — Free sac is essential
2. **Death triggers** — Convert deaths to value
3. **Graveyard filling** — Self-mill helps reanimator
4. **Don't forget removal** — Assassin's Trophy, Beast Within
5. **Board wipes** — Toxic Deluge is essential

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No sacrifice outlets** — Need free ways to sac
2. **Ignoring graveyard hate** — Include protection
3. **Too much self-mill** — Need things to do with graveyard
4. **Skipping removal** — Still need to answer threats
5. **No win conditions** — Value doesn't win alone

## Meren Experience

[[Meren of Clan Nel Toth]] is the quintessential Golgari commander:

### Building Experience
- Sacrifice cheap creatures early
- Each creature death = 1 experience
- More experience = bigger recursion

### Key Targets
- Low CMC: [[Sakura-Tribe Elder]], [[Caustic Caterpillar]]
- Mid CMC: [[Ravenous Chupacabra]], [[Fleshbag Marauder]]
- High CMC: [[Protean Hulk]], [[Razaketh]]

### Play Pattern
1. Deploy small creatures, sacrifice for value
2. Gain experience counters
3. Bring back increasingly large creatures
4. Eventually free Craterhoof/Razaketh recursion
