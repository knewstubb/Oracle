# Green (Mono-Green)

## Color Identity

Green represents nature, growth, and the natural order. In Commander, green provides the best ramp, creature-based strategies, and access to the largest bodies — excelling at building mana advantage and deploying threats.

## Strengths

### Ramp
Green has the best mana acceleration in the format:
- [[Cultivate]] / [[Kodama's Reach]] — Two-land ramp
- [[Three Visits]] / [[Nature's Lore]] — Efficient fetch
- [[Rampant Growth]] — Basic ramp
- [[Llanowar Elves]] family — Mana dorks
- [[Birds of Paradise]] — Flying fixing

### Creature Quality
Green has the best creature stats:
- Large bodies for mana cost
- Trample is common
- ETB effects on creatures
- Creature-based card advantage

### Artifact/Enchantment Removal
Green destroys noncreature permanents efficiently:
- [[Nature's Claim]] — 1 mana instant
- [[Krosan Grip]] — Split second
- [[Beast Within]] — Universal removal
- [[Force of Vigor]] — Free removal
- [[Bane of Progress]] — Mass removal

### Card Advantage
Green draws through creatures:
- [[Beast Whisperer]] — Cast trigger
- [[Guardian Project]] — ETB trigger
- [[The Great Henge]] — Draw + counters
- [[Sylvan Library]] — Selection
- [[Harmonize]] — Pure draw

## Weaknesses

### Removal
Limited creature removal:
- [[Beast Within]] — Gives 3/3 beast
- [[Kenrith's Transformation]] — Pacifism effect
- Fight cards require your creatures
- No exile-based removal

### Counterspells
No stack interaction:
- [[Veil of Summer]] — Protection
- [[Heroic Intervention]] — Protection
- Cannot counter spells

### Flying
Most green creatures are groundbound:
- [[Nylea's Forerunner]] — Trample helps
- [[Rhonas's Monument]] — Trample
- Reach creatures block flyers

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize ramp** — Green's core strength
2. **Include creature-based draw** — Beast Whisperer, Guardian Project
3. **Beast Within is essential** — Your main removal
4. **Finishers** — Craterhoof or similar to close games
5. **Protection** — Heroic Intervention saves your board

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All ramp, no payoffs** — Need things to do with mana
2. **No removal** — Include Beast Within and Nature's Claim
3. **Missing finishers** — Big creatures need trample or overrun
4. **Overextending** — Board wipes devastate; hold back threats
5. **Ignoring flyers** — Include reach or trample through

## Craterhoof Math

[[Craterhoof Behemoth]] is the classic green finisher:
- Gives all creatures +X/+X and trample
- X = number of creatures you control
- 5 creatures = +5/+5 to all = 25+ damage
- 10 creatures = 100+ damage easily
- Include haste enablers for surprise kills
