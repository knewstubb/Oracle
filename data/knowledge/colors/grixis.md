# Grixis (Blue/Black/Red)

## Overview

Grixis combines spellslinger strategies with sacrifice synergies and control. The shard excels at storm, reanimator, and spell-based value.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Blue (U) + Black (B) + Red (R)
**Shard:** Grixis (Alara)
**Philosophy:** Power through sacrifice, undeath and destruction

## Strengths

- **Spells:** Strong instant/sorcery support
- **Card Advantage:** Draw, tutors, impulse draw
- **Counterspells:** Full counter suite
- **Removal:** Creature removal, exile, damage
- **Graveyard:** Reanimation and recursion

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Relies on artifacts and rituals
- **Enchantment Removal:** Critical weakness
- **Artifact Removal:** Blue only ([[Cyclonic Rift]])
- **Protection:** Limited defensive options

## Staples

- [[Grixis Charm]] — Modal utility
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Asymmetric bounce
- [[Demonic Tutor]] — Best tutor
- [[Jeska's Will]] — Ritual + impulse draw
