# Gruul (Red/Green)

## Overview

Gruul combines green's ramp and creatures with red's aggression and haste. The pair excels at big creature strategies, land destruction, and overwhelming force.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Red (R) + Green (G)
**Guild:** Gruul Clans (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Nature's fury, primal strength

## Strengths

- **Ramp:** Green's acceleration + red's rituals
- **Creature Quality:** Biggest, most efficient bodies
- **Haste:** Immediate threat deployment
- **Trample:** Damage gets through
- **Land/Artifact Destruction:** Strong hate pieces

## Weaknesses

- **Card Draw:** Limited to creature-based draw
- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Creature Removal:** Relies on fight and burn
- **Enchantment Interaction:** Green only

## Staples

### Ramp
- [[Cultivate]] / [[Kodama's Reach]] — Two lands
- [[Three Visits]] / [[Nature's Lore]] — Untapped land
- [[Sol Ring]] — Format staple
- [[Selvala, Heart of the Wilds]] — Creature-based massive ramp

### Card Advantage
- [[Greater Good]] — Sacrifice draw
- [[Beast Whisperer]] — Creature draw
- [[Garruk's Uprising]] — Trample + draw
- [[Return of the Wildspeaker]] — Burst draw

### Removal
- [[Beast Within]] — Any permanent
- [[Chaos Warp]] — Flexible removal
- [[Blasphemous Act]] — Cheap wrath
- [[Decimate]] — Four-for-one
