# Jeskai (Blue/Red/White)

## Overview

Jeskai combines spellslinger strategies with control elements. The wedge excels at instant/sorcery synergies, prowess, and spell copying.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Blue (U) + Red (R) + White (W)
**Clan:** Jeskai Way (Tarkir)
**Philosophy:** Cunning and discipline, enlightened warfare

## Strengths

- **Spells:** Best instant/sorcery support
- **Counterspells:** Full counter suite
- **Removal:** Exile, burn, and bounce
- **Card Draw:** Blue and red impulse draw
- **Protection:** White's defensive options

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Very limited acceleration
- **Graveyard Recursion:** Spell recursion only
- **Creature Quality:** Smaller bodies
- **Tutors:** Limited tutor access

- [[Jeskai Charm]] — Modal utility
- [[Narset's Reversal]] — Copy and bounce
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Asymmetric bounce
