# Jund (Black/Red/Green)

## Overview

Jund combines sacrifice synergies with ramp and aggressive creatures. The shard excels at aristocrats, -1/-1 counters, and value-focused midrange.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Black (B) + Red (R) + Green (G)
**Shard:** Jund (Alara)
**Philosophy:** Survival of the fittest, predatory nature

## Strengths

- **Aristocrats:** Best sacrifice payoffs
- **Ramp:** Green acceleration
- **Removal:** Creature and artifact coverage
- **Card Advantage:** Various draw engines
- **Graveyard:** Recursion and reanimation

## Weaknesses

- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Enchantment Removal:** Green only
- **Protection:** Limited defensive options
- **Flyers:** Fewer evasive threats
