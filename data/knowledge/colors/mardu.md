# Mardu (Red/White/Black)

## Overview

Mardu combines aggressive combat with sacrifice synergies and flexible removal. The wedge excels at aristocrats, aggressive strategies, and equipment-based builds.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Red (R) + White (W) + Black (B)
**Clan:** Mardu Horde (Tarkir)
**Philosophy:** Speed and ruthlessness, war and plunder

## Strengths

- **Removal:** Best removal suite (exile, destroy, damage)
- **Aristocrats:** Sacrifice payoffs in all three colors
- **Combat:** Extra combats, haste, first strike
- **Tutors:** Black's tutoring power
- **Recursion:** White and black graveyard access

## Weaknesses

- **Ramp:** Very limited, relies on treasures/rituals
- **Card Draw:** Improved but still behind blue
- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Enchantment Synergy:** Weak enchantment support

## Staples

- [[Smothering Tithe]] — Treasure generation
- [[Swords to Plowshares]] — Efficient removal
- [[Anguished Unmaking]] — Flexible exile
- [[Toxic Deluge]] — Scalable wrath
