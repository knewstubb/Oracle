# Naya (Red/Green/White)

## Overview

Naya combines big creatures with combat support and ramp. The shard excels at dinosaurs, go-wide tokens, and creature-based strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Red (R) + Green (G) + White (W)
**Shard:** Naya (Alara)
**Philosophy:** Nature's might, primal celebration

## Strengths

- **Creatures:** Biggest, most efficient bodies
- **Ramp:** Green acceleration + cost reduction
- **Combat:** Best combat tricks and extra combats
- **Tokens:** Strong token generation
- **Removal:** Exile + naturalize + damage

## Weaknesses

- **Card Draw:** Creature-dependent
- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Graveyard Recursion:** Limited options
- **Tutors:** Creature tutors only
