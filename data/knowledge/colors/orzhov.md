# Orzhov (White-Black)

## Color Identity

Orzhov represents organized religion, taxation, and debt — life and death manipulation through institutional power. In Commander, Orzhov excels at aristocrats, lifegain/drain, and attrition strategies that grind opponents down while maintaining your resources.

## Strengths

### Removal
The best removal combination in Magic:
- [[Swords to Plowshares]] / [[Path to Exile]] — White exile
- [[Go for the Throat]] / [[Infernal Grasp]] — Black destruction
- [[Anguished Unmaking]] — Universal exile
- [[Vindicate]] — Any permanent
- [[Vanishing Verse]] — Mono-colored exile

### Lifegain/Drain
Orzhov owns this space:
- [[Blood Artist]] / [[Zulaport Cutthroat]] — Death drain
- [[Exquisite Blood]] + [[Sanguine Bond]] — Combo
- [[Soul Warden]] effects — ETB lifegain
- [[Gray Merchant of Asphodel]] — Devotion drain
- [[Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] — Damage on lifegain

### Recursion
Strong creature recursion:
- [[Karmic Guide]] — White reanimation
- [[Sun Titan]] — Small permanent recursion
- [[Reanimate]] / [[Animate Dead]] — Black reanimation
- [[Living Death]] — Mass swap
- [[Reveillark]] — Leave-battlefield recursion

### Sacrifice Synergies
Aristocrats is Orzhov's signature:
- [[Viscera Seer]] — Free sacrifice + scry
- [[Grave Pact]] / [[Dictate of Erebos]] — Force sacrifices
- [[Pitiless Plunderer]] — Treasure on death
- [[Teysa Karlov]] — Double death triggers

## Weaknesses

### Ramp
Limited mana acceleration:
- No green land ramp
- Relies on artifacts
- [[Smothering Tithe]] is the exception
- [[Pitiless Plunderer]] for treasure

### Card Draw
Less efficient than blue:
- [[Phyrexian Arena]] — Slow but steady
- [[Sign in Blood]] variants
- [[Esper Sentinel]] — Tax draw
- [[Welcoming Vampire]] — Creature draw

### Counterspells
No stack interaction:
- Must rely on proactive answers
- [[Grand Abolisher]] prevents interaction on your turn
- [[Silence]] effects temporarily

## Key Synergies

### Aristocrats Engine
- Sacrifice outlet (Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Altar)
- Death trigger (Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat)
- Grave Pact effects (force opponent sacrifices)
- Recursion (Karmic Guide, Sun Titan)
- Teysa Karlov doubles all death triggers

### Lifegain Loop
- [[Soul Warden]] effects gain life on ETB
- [[Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] — Life gain = damage
- [[Exquisite Blood]] + [[Sanguine Bond]] — Infinite combo
- [[Aetherflux Reservoir]] — Life to damage

### Teysa Double Triggers
- [[Teysa Karlov]] doubles creature death triggers
- [[Blood Artist]] deals 2 instead of 1
- [[Grave Pact]] triggers twice
- [[Pitiless Plunderer]] makes 2 treasures
- Extremely powerful aristocrats engine

## Building Tips

1. **Include sacrifice outlets** — Free sac is essential
2. **Death triggers** — Blood Artist effects are core
3. **Recursion package** — Bring back key creatures
4. **Smothering Tithe** — Best white ramp; don't skip
5. **Balance removal** — Orzhov has the best; use it

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No sacrifice outlets** — Need free ways to sac
2. **Ignoring card advantage** — Include draw engines
3. **Too much removal** — Need win conditions too
4. **Missing Smothering Tithe** — Premium ramp for white
5. **No recursion** — Orzhov excels here; leverage it

## Exquisite Blood Combo

[[Exquisite Blood]] + [[Sanguine Bond]] is Orzhov's signature combo:
- Any life loss or gain triggers infinite loop
- Opponent loses 1 life → you gain 1 life
- Sanguine Bond deals 1 damage → Exquisite Blood gains 1 life
- Repeat until opponents dead

### Setup
- Either piece can be tutored
- [[Enlightened Tutor]], [[Vampiric Tutor]]
- [[Idyllic Tutor]] for enchantments
- Any incidental life gain/loss starts the loop
