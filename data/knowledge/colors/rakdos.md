# Rakdos (Black/Red)

## Overview

Rakdos combines black's sacrifice and tutors with red's aggression and impulse draw. The pair excels at aristocrats, aggro, and chaos strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Black (B) + Red (R)
**Guild:** Cult of Rakdos (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Hedonism and destruction, chaos and pain

## Strengths

- **Creature Removal:** Best creature kill combination
- **Sacrifice Synergies:** Aristocrats and death triggers
- **Aggression:** Fast damage and haste
- **Impulse Draw:** Red's exile-draw + black's tutors
- **Discard:** Hand attack and madness synergies

## Weaknesses

- **Enchantment Removal:** Cannot remove enchantments (critical weakness)
- **Counterspells:** No stack interaction
- **Ramp:** Limited to treasures and rituals
- **Card Draw:** Relies on life payment or impulse
