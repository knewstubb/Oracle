# Red (Mono-Red)

## Color Identity

Red represents passion, chaos, freedom, and immediate action. In Commander, red provides explosive damage, artifact synergies, treasure generation, and impulsive card advantage — trading long-term resources for immediate impact.

## Strengths

### Direct Damage
Red deals damage efficiently:
- [[Lightning Bolt]] — 3 damage for 1 mana
- [[Chaos Warp]] — Universal removal
- [[Blasphemous Act]] — 13 damage wrath
- [[Comet Storm]] — X-spell multi-target
- [[Crackle with Power]] — Massive finisher

### Artifact Synergies
Red excels with artifacts:
- [[Dockside Extortionist]] — Treasure generation
- [[Goblin Welder]] — Artifact recursion
- [[Daretti, Scrap Savant]] — Artifact manipulation
- [[Trash for Treasure]] — Reanimation
- [[Faithless Looting]] — Artifact setup

### Treasure Generation
Red creates abundant treasures:
- [[Dockside Extortionist]] — Scales with opponents
- [[Professional Face-Breaker]] — Combat treasure
- [[Goldspan Dragon]] — Treasure on target
- [[Storm-Kiln Artist]] — Spell trigger treasure
- [[Xorn]] — Extra treasure

### Impulsive Draw
Red's card advantage is temporary:
- [[Faithless Looting]] — Looting
- [[Light Up the Stage]] — Spectacle draw
- [[Jeska's Will]] — Mana + impulse
- [[Wheel of Fortune]] — Classic wheel
- [[Reforge the Soul]] — Miracle wheel

## Weaknesses

### Enchantment Removal
Red cannot destroy enchantments directly:
- [[Chaos Warp]] — Random but universal
- [[Wild Magic Surge]] — Same effect
- Limited options otherwise

### Sustained Card Advantage
Impulsive draw has timing restrictions:
- Must cast same turn (usually)
- Loses cards if unable to cast
- Wheels give opponents cards too

### Lifegain
Red has almost no lifegain:
- Lifelink is rare
- Cannot recover life totals easily

## Mono-Red Strategies

### Best Fit
- Goblin tribal
- Artifact-based strategies
- Dragon tribal
- Burn/direct damage
- Treasure themes

### Good Fit
- Spellslinger (more limited than Izzet)
- Chaos/random effects

### Challenging
- Control (few answers)
- Lifegain (wrong color)
- Graveyard (limited recursion)

## Building Tips

1. **Leverage treasure** — Red's best ramp mechanic
2. **Include Chaos Warp** — Your main universal answer
3. **Artifact synergies** — Red's hidden strength
4. **Impulsive draw** — Plan to cast the cards you exile
5. **Haste enablers** — Red creatures want to attack immediately

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No enchantment answers** — [[Chaos Warp]] is essential
2. **Wasting impulsive draw** — Don't exile what you can't cast
3. **Overcommitting** — Board wipes hurt you too
4. **Ignoring artifacts** — Red has great artifact synergies
5. **No sustained value** — Need repeatable draw sources

## Wheels

Red's wheels refill hands but are symmetric:
- [[Wheel of Fortune]] — Classic
- [[Wheel of Misfortune]] — With damage
- [[Reforge the Soul]] — Miracle cost
- [[Magus of the Wheel]] — Creature version

### Wheel Strategy
- Empty hand first, then wheel
- Combine with [[Notion Thief]] effects (in other colors)
- [[Narset, Parter of Veils]] locks opponents out
- [[Smothering Tithe]] generates treasure from wheels
