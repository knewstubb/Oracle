# Selesnya (Green/White)

## Overview

Selesnya combines green's ramp and creatures with white's protection and anthems. The pair excels at tokens, go-wide strategies, and creature-based value.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Green (G) + White (W)
**Guild:** Selesnya Conclave (Ravnica)
**Philosophy:** Community and growth, harmony with nature

## Strengths

- **Tokens:** Best token generation and support
- **Anthems:** Pump effects for wide boards
- **Ramp:** Green's acceleration + white's cost reduction
- **Removal:** Flexible exile and naturalize effects
- **Protection:** Hexproof, indestructible, regenerate

## Weaknesses

- **Card Draw:** Limited, creature-dependent
- **Counterspells:** None
- **Graveyard Recursion:** White has some, green minimal
- **Direct Damage:** No burn effects

## Staples

### Ramp
- [[Cultivate]] / [[Kodama's Reach]] — Two lands
- [[Sol Ring]] — Format staple
- [[Three Visits]] / [[Nature's Lore]] — Untapped land
- [[Mirari's Wake]] — Mana doubler + anthem

### Token Support
- [[Doubling Season]] — Double tokens and counters
- [[Anointed Procession]] — Double tokens
- [[Parallel Lives]] — Double tokens
- [[Cathars' Crusade]] — +1/+1 counters on creature ETB

### Removal
- [[Swords to Plowshares]] — Efficient exile
- [[Beast Within]] — Any permanent
- [[Generous Gift]] — Any permanent exile
- [[Aura Shards]] — ETB artifact/enchantment removal
