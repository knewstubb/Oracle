# Simic (Green-Blue)

## Color Identity

Simic combines green's ramp and creature focus with blue's card draw and manipulation. This creates one of Commander's most powerful color combinations — natural card advantage and mana acceleration with access to counterspells and combo protection.

## Strengths

### Ramp
Green provides the best ramp in the format:
- [[Cultivate]] / [[Kodama's Reach]] — Classic land ramp
- [[Three Visits]] / [[Nature's Lore]] — Efficient 2-mana ramp
- [[Rampant Growth]] — Baseline
- [[Sol Ring]] — Colorless staple
- [[Arcane Signet]] — 2-color fixing

### Card Draw
Blue adds exceptional draw:
- [[Rhystic Study]] — Taxation draw
- [[Mystic Remora]] — Early game draw
- [[Consecrated Sphinx]] — Explosive draw
- [[Brainstorm]] / [[Ponder]] — Cantrips
- [[Blue Sun's Zenith]] — X-spell draw

### Counterspells
Blue's signature interaction:
- [[Counterspell]] — Classic
- [[Swan Song]] — Efficient
- [[Force of Will]] / [[Fierce Guardianship]] — Free counters
- [[Mana Drain]] — Premium
- [[Arcane Denial]] — Multipurpose

## Weaknesses

### Removal
Limited creature/permanent removal:
- [[Beast Within]] — Green universal
- [[Rapid Hybridization]] / [[Pongify]] — Creature removal with downside
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Bounce, not removal
- No exile-based creature removal
- No artifact/enchantment destruction in blue

### Board Wipes
Limited options:
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Bounce, not destruction
- [[Curse of the Swine]] — Mass creature exile
- [[Devastation Tide]] — Bounce all
- No traditional wraths

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize ramp** — Green's strength; use it aggressively
2. **Include counterspells** — Blue's interaction is valuable
3. **Beast Within is essential** — Your main removal
4. **Cyclonic Rift** — The best card in the format
5. **Balance threats and interaction** — Don't be all draw/ramp

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All ramp, no threats** — Need things to do with mana
2. **Skipping interaction** — Counterspells are blue's gift
3. **No removal** — [[Beast Within]] is your friend
4. **Overcommitting** — Still vulnerable to board wipes
5. **No win condition** — Value doesn't win without closers

## Simic Power Level

Simic is widely considered one of the strongest color pairs due to:
- Natural card advantage through draw + ramp
- Access to counterspells for protection
- Excellent commanders at all power levels
- Strong combo potential

Many competitive EDH decks include Simic in their color identity.
