# Sultai (Black/Green/Blue)

## Overview

Sultai combines graveyard synergies with card advantage and ramp. The wedge excels at reanimator, self-mill, and value-based strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Black (B) + Green (G) + Blue (U)
**Clan:** Sultai Brood (Tarkir)
**Philosophy:** Ruthless ambition, death as resource

## Strengths

- **Graveyard:** Best graveyard interaction suite
- **Card Advantage:** Draw, tutors, and recursion
- **Ramp:** Green acceleration + mana doublers
- **Removal:** Creature, artifact, enchantment coverage
- **Counterspells:** Stack interaction available

## Weaknesses

- **Combat:** Fewer combat tricks
- **Direct Damage:** No burn effects
- **Artifact Synergy:** Limited artifact support
- **Aggro:** Tends toward slower strategies
