# Temur (Green/Blue/Red)

## Overview

Temur combines ramp, card draw, and aggression. The wedge excels at big creatures, landfall, and spell-based value strategies.

## Color Identity

**Colors:** Green (G) + Blue (U) + Red (R)
**Clan:** Temur Frontier (Tarkir)
**Philosophy:** Savage wisdom, primal power

## Strengths

- **Ramp + Draw:** Best mana + cards combination
- **Big Creatures:** Largest bodies with haste
- **Landfall:** Strong land-based synergies
- **Cascade:** Many cascade commanders
- **Elemental Synergy:** Strong tribal support

## Weaknesses

- **Creature Removal:** Relies on fight/damage
- **Exile Effects:** No exile-based removal
- **Tutors:** Creature tutors only
- **Aristocrats:** No sacrifice payoffs

## Staples

- [[Temur Sabertooth]] — Bounce for value
- [[Rhystic Study]] — Tax draw
- [[Beast Within]] — Any permanent removal
- [[Cyclonic Rift]] — Asymmetric bounce
