# White (Mono-White)

## Color Identity

White represents order, law, community, and protection. In Commander, white offers the best removal, strong creature protection, and powerful group effects, but historically struggles with card advantage and ramp.

## Strengths

### Removal
White has the most efficient and versatile removal:
- [[Swords to Plowshares]] — 1 mana exile creature
- [[Path to Exile]] — 1 mana exile with land ramp
- [[Generous Gift]] / [[Beast Within]] — Universal removal
- [[Wrath of God]] / [[Day of Judgment]] — Classic board wipes
- [[Farewell]] — Modal mass exile

### Protection
- [[Teferi's Protection]] — Ultimate protection
- [[Boros Charm]] — Indestructible mode
- [[Flawless Maneuver]] — Free indestructible
- [[Heroic Intervention]] — Hexproof + indestructible (green splash)
- [[Mother of Runes]] — Repeatable protection

### Recursion
White excels at creature recursion:
- [[Karmic Guide]] — ETB reanimation
- [[Sun Titan]] — Recurring small permanents
- [[Reveillark]] — Leave-battlefield reanimation
- [[Sevinne's Reclamation]] — Permanent recursion

## Weaknesses

### Card Advantage
White traditionally lacks efficient draw:
- Few unconditional draw effects
- Relies on conditional triggers
- Must work harder for card parity

### Ramp
Limited mana acceleration:
- No green-style land ramp
- Relies on artifacts and catch-up mechanics
- [[Land Tax]] and [[Smothering Tithe]] are exceptions

## Recent Improvements

WotC has addressed white's weaknesses:

### New Draw Options
- [[Esper Sentinel]] — Tax-based draw
- [[Welcoming Vampire]] — Creature draw
- [[Trouble in Pairs]] — Opponent-triggered draw
- [[Smuggler's Share]] — Draw and ramp
- [[Archivist of Oghma]] — Search-triggered draw

### New Ramp
- [[Smothering Tithe]] — Premier white ramp
- [[Archaeomancer's Map]] — Land catching
- [[Keeper of the Accord]] — Land + tokens
- [[Monologue Tax]] — Treasure generation
- [[Deep Gnome Terramancer]] — Flash land grab

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize new draw** — Include Esper Sentinel, Welcoming Vampire types
2. **Smothering Tithe is essential** — Don't skip it
3. **Include recursion** — [[Sun Titan]], [[Karmic Guide]]
4. **Board wipes** — You have the best ones; use them
5. **Protection pieces** — [[Teferi's Protection]] and friends

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Skipping new draw options** — White has more draw now; use it
2. **Not enough removal** — White's strength; don't skimp
3. **Ignoring ramp** — Still need it; include [[Smothering Tithe]]
4. **Overextending** — You have wraths; opponents do too
5. **No game-ending threats** — Include finishers
