# Commander Deck Anatomy

## Overview

Every Commander deck needs a balanced foundation of "vegetables" — the essential categories that keep the deck functional regardless of strategy. Without these fundamentals, even the best synergy-focused deck will stumble.

## Standard Deck Composition

A typical 100-card Commander deck breaks down roughly as:

| Category | Count | Purpose |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Lands | 36-38 | Mana production |
| Ramp | 10-12 | Mana acceleration |
| Card Draw | 8-10 | Maintain hand size |
| Removal | 8-10 | Answer threats |
| Board Wipes | 3-4 | Reset problematic boards |
| Win Conditions | 7-10 | Close out games |
| Synergy Pieces | 25-35 | Theme-specific cards |

## Lands

### Count Guidelines
- **36 lands**: Low-curve decks (avg CMC < 2.5) with heavy ramp
- **37 lands**: Average decks (avg CMC 2.5-3.5)
- **38+ lands**: High-curve decks or land-focused strategies

### Land Quality Tiers
1. **Fetch lands** — Fix colors, thin deck, trigger landfall twice
2. **Shock lands** — Fetchable dual lands
3. **Bond lands / Battlebond lands** — Untapped in multiplayer
4. **Check lands** — Usually untapped by turn 2-3
5. **Pathway lands** — Modal, always untapped
6. **Basics** — Required for some ramp, immune to nonbasic hate

### Utility Lands to Consider
- **Command Tower** — Auto-include in multicolor decks
- **Reliquary Tower** — If deck draws many cards
- **Bojuka Bog** — Free graveyard hate
- **Castle cycle** — Cheap utility if running enough basics
- **Creature lands** — Evasive threats that dodge sorcery-speed removal

## Ramp

### Count by Commander Cost
| Commander CMC | Ramp Spells |
|---------------|-------------|
| 2-3 | 8 |
| 4-5 | 9 |
| 6+ | 10+ |

### Ramp Types

**Mana Dorks** (creatures that tap for mana)
- Vulnerable to removal and board wipes
- Best in green; provide early acceleration
- Examples: Llanowar Elves, Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender

**Mana Rocks** (artifacts that produce mana)
- More resilient than dorks (dodges creature removal)
- Vulnerable to artifact hate
- Examples: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Signets, Talismans

**Land Ramp** (putting extra lands into play)
- Most resilient — land removal is rare
- Green specialty
- Examples: Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Nature's Lore, Three Visits

**Cost Reducers**
- Make specific spell types cheaper
- Cumulative with other reducers
- Examples: Urza's Incubator (creatures), Goblin Electromancer (instants/sorceries)

### 2-Mana Ramp Priority
Prioritize 2-CMC ramp to cast your commander a turn early. The jump from T2 ramp to T3 commander is critical for tempo.

Top 2-CMC ramp:
- Sol Ring (1 CMC, but special)
- Signets (Arcane Signet, guild signets)
- Talismans
- Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Farseek
- Fellwar Stone, Mind Stone

## Card Draw

### Target: 8-10 sources minimum

Without consistent draw, you'll run out of gas. Commander games are long — you need to see more cards than your one draw per turn.

### Draw Engine Types

**Repeatable Draw**
- Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora (tax-based)
- Phyrexian Arena, Sylvan Library (incremental)
- Guardian Project, Beast Whisperer (creature-based)
- Skullclamp (sacrifice-based)

**Burst Draw**
- Wheel effects: Wheel of Fortune, Windfall
- Big draw spells: Pull from Tomorrow, Blue Sun's Zenith
- Refill spells: Shamanic Revelation, Return of the Wildspeaker

**Cantrips** (draw that replaces itself)
- Ponder, Preordain, Brainstorm (blue)
- Sign in Blood, Night's Whisper (black)

### Color-Specific Draw
- **White**: Mentor of the Meek (small creatures), Welcoming Vampire
- **Blue**: Card draw central — everything works
- **Black**: Pay life for cards — Necropotence, Ad Nauseam
- **Red**: Impulse draw (exile, cast this turn) — Jeska's Will, Light Up the Stage
- **Green**: Creature-based — Garruk's Packleader, Greater Good

## Removal

### Target: 8-10 pieces

You need answers. Every deck runs threats; you need ways to handle them.

### Removal Categories

**Targeted Removal**
- Creatures: Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Go for the Throat
- Artifacts/Enchantments: Nature's Claim, Krosan Grip, Generous Gift
- Planeswalkers: Direct damage, Vampire Hexmage, The Immortal Sun
- Any permanent: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Vindicate

**Board Wipes**
- Creature wipes: Wrath of God, Damnation, Toxic Deluge
- Artifact/Enchantment wipes: Vandalblast, Austere Command
- Selective wipes: Cyclonic Rift, Blasphemous Act

**Counterspells** (blue)
- Hard counters: Counterspell, Mana Drain
- Cheap counters: Swan Song, An Offer You Can't Refuse
- Free counters: Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship

### Efficiency Matters
- 1-2 mana removal is premium
- Instant-speed preferred over sorcery
- Modal removal (Abrade, Izzet Charm) provides flexibility

## Win Conditions

### Every deck needs a plan to end the game

Common win condition types:
- **Combat damage** — Big creatures, commander damage, go-wide tokens
- **Combo** — Two or more cards that win immediately
- **Drain effects** — Blood Artist loops, Exsanguinate
- **Alternative wins** — Thassa's Oracle, Lab Maniac, Felidar Sovereign

### Include redundancy
Don't rely on a single win condition. If it gets exiled, you need a backup plan.

## Interaction Timing

### When to hold vs. when to use removal

- **Threats to you specifically**: Remove immediately
- **Threats to the table**: Let others spend resources first
- **Combo pieces**: Remove on sight
- **Value engines**: Remove before they generate too much advantage

## Building Tips

1. **Start with the core** — Lands, ramp, draw, removal first
2. **Then add synergy** — Theme-specific cards
3. **Test and adjust** — Track what you never cast, what you always want
4. **Goldfish** — Practice opening hands to check consistency
5. **Curve check** — Ensure you have plays at every mana level

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too many high-cost cards** — Can't cast anything early
- **Not enough lands** — Consistent mana screw
- **No interaction** — Can't stop opponents
- **All synergy, no fundamentals** — Deck folds to disruption
- **Too many "win more" cards** — Only good when already winning
