# Cascade / Discover

## Overview

Cascade exiles cards from your library until you hit a spell with lower mana value, then casts it free. Discover is the newer, more controlled version. Both generate massive card advantage and enable powerful synergies.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Stack your deck with high-value cascade targets, manipulate the cascade hits, or build around repeated cascade triggers for overwhelming advantage.

**Key Elements:**
- Cascade spells with high mana values
- Low-cost high-impact targets
- Ways to stack the top of library
- Cascade doublers and repeaters
- Protection for cascade chains

## Building Tips

1. **Consider cascade targets** — Every spell under your cascade costs matters
2. **Avoid low-impact hits** — No 1-mana duds in a 7+ cascade deck
3. **Include library manipulation** — Stack what you cascade into
4. **Watch for 0-cost spells** — Suspend cards are powerful cascade hits
