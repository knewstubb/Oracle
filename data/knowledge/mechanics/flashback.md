# Flashback / Graveyard Casting

## Overview

Flashback lets you cast instants and sorceries from your graveyard for an alternate cost. Related mechanics (jump-start, retrace, disturb) provide similar graveyard value. These mechanics effectively double your spell count.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Your graveyard is a second hand. Mill yourself aggressively, then recast your best spells for additional value. Combine with spell payoffs for multiplied triggers.

**Key Elements:**
- Self-mill to fill graveyard
- Flashback/jump-start spells
- Spell payoffs (magecraft, etc.)
- Graveyard protection
- Cost reduction for expensive flashback costs

- **[[Sevinne, the Chronoclasm]]** — Copy first flashback spell
- **[[Kess, Dissident Mage]]** — Cast one instant/sorcery per turn from graveyard
- **[[Mizzix of the Izmagnus]]** — Cost reduction for spells
- **[[Lier, Disciple of the Drowned]]** — All instants/sorceries have flashback

## Building Tips

1. **Mill yourself aggressively** — Fill graveyard with targets
2. **Include graveyard protection** — [[Perpetual Timepiece]]
3. **Run spell payoffs** — Double up triggers
4. **Watch flashback costs** — Often more expensive
