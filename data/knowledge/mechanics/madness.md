# Madness / Discard Synergies

## Overview

Madness lets you cast cards for an alternate cost when you discard them. Combined with discard outlets, madness turns a "downside" into card advantage. Related synergies reward discarding for value.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Turn discard costs into benefits. Discard outlets become draw engines when combined with madness spells and discard payoffs.

**Key Elements:**
- Madness spells (cast when discarded)
- Free or beneficial discard outlets
- Discard payoffs (draw, tokens, damage)
- Graveyard recursion backup
- Wheel effects for mass discard

## Common Commanders

- **[[Anje Falkenrath]]** — Madness + draw engine
- **[[Chainer, Nightmare Adept]]** — Discard for haste + recursion
- **[[Olivia, Mobilized for War]]** — Discard for counters + haste
- **[[The Raven Man]]** — Tokens on discard

## Building Tips

1. **Run lots of discard outlets** — Need consistent activation
2. **Include non-madness payoffs** — Backup value
3. **Consider [[Anje]]** — Lets you cycle through deck
4. **Don't overload on madness** — Still need normal cards
