# Commander Format Rules

## Overview

Commander (also known as EDH - Elder Dragon Highlander) is a multiplayer format where each player builds a 100-card singleton deck led by a legendary creature.

## Deck Construction Rules

### Commander Requirements
- Your commander must be a legendary creature (or a card that explicitly states it can be your commander)
- Partner commanders: You may have two commanders if both have Partner or "Partner with [specific card]"
- Background: A legendary creature with "Choose a Background" can pair with a Background enchantment

### Deck Size & Singleton
- Exactly 100 cards including your commander(s)
- Singleton format: Only one copy of each card (except basic lands and cards that explicitly override this rule like [[Relentless Rats]])

### Color Identity
- Your deck can only contain cards whose color identity is a subset of your commander's color identity
- Color identity includes: mana cost colors, color indicators, mana symbols in rules text
- Color identity does NOT include: reminder text, color words in names/text, symbols in other zones
- Hybrid mana symbols count as BOTH colors for color identity
- Phyrexian mana symbols count as their colored component
- Basic land types have intrinsic mana abilities that don't affect color identity

### Banned Cards
- The format has a banned list maintained by the Commander Rules Committee
- Cards banned as commander cannot be in the command zone but may be in the 99 (if not also banned outright)

## Gameplay Rules

### The Command Zone
- Your commander begins the game in the command zone (not in your deck)
- You may cast your commander from the command zone
- If your commander would go to your hand, library, graveyard, or exile, you may instead put it into the command zone (state-based action replacement)
- This is a replacement effect you choose each time

### Commander Tax
- Each time you cast your commander from the command zone, it costs an additional {2} for each previous time you've cast it from the command zone this game
- This applies to all commanders (including partners — each tracked separately)
- Alternative costs (like [[Derevi, Empyrial Tactician]]'s activated ability) bypass commander tax but still count as casting for tax purposes

### Commander Damage
- Damage dealt by a commander is tracked separately
- If a player is dealt 21 or more combat damage by a single commander over the course of the game, that player loses
- Only combat damage counts (not damage from abilities)
- Each commander's damage is tracked separately
- Commander damage persists even if the commander changes zones or control

### Starting Life Total
- Each player starts at 40 life (not 20)

### Multiplayer Rules
- Standard multiplayer rules apply (attack left, defend yourself, etc.)
- Free-for-all is the default format
- When a player leaves the game, all cards they own leave with them, all spells/abilities they control cease to exist, and all effects giving them control of permanents end

## Common Rules Interactions

### Command Zone Mechanics
- Eminence abilities work from the command zone
- Companion can be used with a commander (companion goes to command zone after being put into hand)
- "Dies" triggers still work if you choose to send commander to command zone (it changes zones through the graveyard)

### Color Identity Edge Cases
- Extort reminder text doesn't add black/white to color identity
- Devoid cards have color identity based on their mana cost, not their color (colorless)
- DFCs use the color identity of both faces combined
- Adventure spells count the Adventure's mana cost too
