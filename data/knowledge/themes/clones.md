# Clones / Copy Effects

## Overview

Clone effects copy creatures (yours or opponents'), providing flexibility and value. Copy the best threat on the board, double up on your own ETB creatures, or create multiple copies of legendary creatures with the right support.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Why build your own threats when you can copy the best on the board? Clones scale with table power level and provide incredible flexibility.

**Key Elements:**
- Clone creatures for flexibility
- ETB value creatures to copy
- Ways to copy your own commander
- Spell copying for instants/sorceries
- Token copying for exponential value

## Essential Cards

### Creature Clones
- [[Clone]] — Original, copies any creature
- [[Phyrexian Metamorph]] — Creature or artifact, 2 life option
- [[Clever Impersonator]] — Any nonland permanent
- [[Sakashima of a Thousand Faces]] — Ignores legend rule
- [[Spark Double]] — +1/+1 or loyalty counter

### Spell Copying
- [[Reverberate]] — Copy instant or sorcery
- [[Twincast]] — Blue version
- [[Fork]] — Red original
- [[Narset's Reversal]] — Copy and bounce
- [[Bonus Round]] — All spells copied this turn

### Token Copying
- [[Esika's Chariot]] — Copy tokens on attack
- [[Progenitor Mimic]] — Copy each upkeep
- [[Rite of Replication]] — 5 copies kicked
- [[Cackling Counterpart]] — Instant speed token
- [[Second Harvest]] — Double all tokens

### Support
- [[Sakashima's Student]] — Ninjutsu clone
- [[Mirror Gallery]] — Ignore legend rule
- [[Mirrorpool]] — Land that copies
- [[Double Major]] — Copy creature spell

## Common Commanders

- **[[Sakashima of a Thousand Faces]] + [[Partner]]** — Copy your commander
- **[[Riku of Two Reflections]]** — Copy creatures and spells
- **[[Volo, Guide to Monsters]]** — Automatic creature copies
- **[[Orvar, the All-Form]]** — Copy on targeting

## Building Tips

1. **Include ETB value creatures** — Best clone targets
2. **Consider your commander** — Can you copy it legally?
3. **Run [[Sakashima]]** — Enables commander copies
4. **Mix in spell copying** — Instants/sorceries too
