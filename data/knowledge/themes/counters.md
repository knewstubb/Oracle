# +1/+1 Counters

## Overview

Counters decks treat +1/+1 counters as a resource to accumulate, multiply, and convert into wins. The theme spans all colors but centers in green and white, with strong support in blue (proliferate) and black (undying/persist).

## Identity

When counters is your **theme**, the deck is *about* growing creatures through counters. When counters is a **mechanic** in another deck (like Edgar Markov vampires), it's a supporting tool, not the identity.

## Core Mechanics

- **Proliferate** — Add counters to any number of permanents
- **Counter Doublers** — Doubling Season, Hardened Scales, Branching Evolution
- **Undying/Persist** — Creatures return with counters, reset via proliferate
- **Counter Movement** — Redistribute counters (Forgotten Ancient, The Ozolith)

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Accumulator + Doubler
Cards that passively gain counters + effects that double them = exponential growth.
- Forgotten Ancient + Doubling Season
- Managorger Hydra + Hardened Scales

### Undying + Counter Removal
Undying creatures return with +1/+1 counters. Remove the counter, sacrifice again, repeat.
- Undying creature + Ozolith + sac outlet = infinite with payoff
- Yawgmoth, Thran Physician removes counters while drawing cards

### Counters → Damage/Evasion
Convert counter size into damage or make creatures unblockable.
- Champion of Lambholt — counters = evasion for team
- Triskelion/Walking Ballista — counters = direct damage

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too many doublers, not enough creatures** — Doublers multiply what you have; with nothing to double, they're dead cards
2. **Ignoring protection** — A board wipe resets your progress. Run Inspiring Call, Tyvar's Stand
3. **No card draw** — Counters decks dump hands fast. Include draw engines
4. **Vanilla beaters** — A 20/20 with no abilities loses to a 1/1 chump. Prioritize counters + keywords

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Midrange** — Grind value, win through accumulated advantage
- **Aggro** — Fast counters, explosive damage (Animar, Hamza)
- **Combo** — Walking Ballista/Heliod, undying loops
