# Energy

## Overview

Energy decks use energy counters as a personal resource that doesn't deplete naturally. The theme involves generating energy and spending it on powerful activated abilities that scale with accumulated reserves.

## Identity

When energy is your **theme**, you're building around energy generation and payoffs. Energy is narrow — it only appeared in Kaladesh block and Modern Horizons 3.

## Core Mechanics

- **Energy Generation** — Cards that give you energy counters on ETB or triggers
- **Energy Spending** — Activated abilities that cost energy
- **Proliferate** — Add energy counters since they're on a player
- **Self-Contained** — Energy doesn't interact with other mechanics naturally

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Generate + Spend Loop
Continuously generate and spend energy for value.
- Aetherworks Marvel — spin into free spells
- Architect of the Untamed — make 6/6 beasts

### Proliferate Acceleration
Proliferate adds energy counters to you.
- Atraxa + energy generators = massive energy reserves
- Evolution Sage + fetch lands = repeated proliferate

### Energy Burst
Accumulate energy, spend all at once for explosive turn.
- Harnessed Lightning — scalable removal
- Die Young — scalable -X/-X

## Role Categories

| Role | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Generation | Aether Hub, Attune with Aether, Glimmer of Genius |
| Payoffs | Aetherworks Marvel, Architect of the Untamed |
| Repeatable | Bristling Hydra, Longtusk Cub |
| Proliferate | Atraxa, Flux Channeler |

## Budget Considerations

Energy cards are generally cheap since the mechanic is narrow and wasn't heavily played. The expensive cards are those that saw Standard/Modern play (Aetherworks Marvel).

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too few energy producers** — Energy payoffs need fuel. Run more generators than spenders
2. **No proliferate** — Proliferate multiplies your energy. Auto-include in energy decks
3. **Wrong commander** — Few commanders actually support energy. Choose carefully

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Midrange** — Accumulate energy, convert to value
- **Combo** — Aetherworks Marvel into big spells
- **Counters** — Proliferate synergy with +1/+1 counters
