# Equipment

## Overview

Equipment decks treat equipment cards as the primary strategy, not just support. The theme involves assembling powerful equipment, reducing equip costs, and turning even small creatures into massive threats.

## Identity

When equipment is your **theme**, you're running 15+ equipment and building around equipment synergies. When you run a Sword of X and Y for value, equipment is incidental.

## Core Mechanics

- **Equip** — Attach equipment to creatures (costs mana by default)
- **Equip Cost Reduction** — Make equipping free or cheap
- **Auto-Equip** — Equipment that attaches automatically on ETB
- **Equipment Tutors** — Find specific equipment pieces

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Free Equip + Multiple Equipment
Stack multiple equipment on one creature for free.
- Puresteel Paladin (metalcraft) — equip for 0
- Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist — attach all equipment at combat

### Equipment + Double Strike
Double strike doubles equipment damage bonuses.
- Sword of Fire and Ice + double strike = 4 damage, 2 cards, 2 damage ping

### Voltron Assembly
Build one creature into a lethal threat.
- Colossus Hammer + free equip = +10/+10 for minimal cost
- Commander + Swiftfoot Boots + damage equipment = commander damage kills

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not enough creatures** — Equipment does nothing without bodies. Run creatures
2. **Equip costs ignored** — Without free equip enablers, equipment is too slow
3. **No protection** — Losing equipped creature loses all equipment value. Run hexproof/indestructible
4. **Wrong equipment** — Run equipment that ends games, not marginal buffs

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Voltron** — Classic equipment strategy
- **Aggro** — Fast, efficient equipment creatures
- **Midrange** — Value equipment with card draw
