# Exile (Cast from Exile)

## Overview

Exile-matters decks treat exile as a resource zone, not a graveyard replacement. The theme involves exiling cards from the top of your library (impulse draw) or from opponents, then casting them for value.

## Identity

When exile is your **theme**, you're building around effects that let you cast exiled cards. This is distinct from using exile as removal — you want to *play* from exile.

## Core Mechanics

- **Impulse Draw** — Exile top card(s), cast until end of turn
- **Opponent Exile** — Exile from opponent's library, cast their cards
- **Cascade/Discover** — Exile cards until you find a castable one
- **Foretell/Suspend** — Exile cards to cast later at reduced cost

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Prosper Engine
Impulse draw triggers Prosper, making treasures.
- Each exiled card you cast = treasure token
- Treasures help cast more expensive exiled cards

### Cascade Chains
Cascade into cascade into cascade.
- Maelstrom Wanderer cascades twice
- Imoti, Celebrant of Bounty gives cascade to 6+ MV spells

### Opponent's Deck Theft
Exile and cast opponent's cards.
- Gonti, Lord of Luxury — exile from opponent's library
- Etali, Primal Storm — exile from all libraries on attack

## Common Mistakes

1. **Mana curve too high** — You can't cast exiled cards you can't afford. Run lower curve
2. **No extra mana** — Impulse draw needs mana to cast. Run treasures, rituals
3. **Wrong timing** — "Until end of turn" means you lose uncast cards. Plan your turns
4. **Whiffing on cascade** — Cascade deck needs targets at each MV range

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Aggro-Value** — Prosper style, generate value while pressuring
- **Combo** — Cascade chains into specific targets
- **Theft** — Cast opponents' cards against them
