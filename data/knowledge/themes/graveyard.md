# Graveyard

## Overview

Graveyard-focused decks treat the graveyard as a second hand or library. The theme involves filling the graveyard deliberately and extracting value through recursion, reanimation, or graveyard-casting effects.

## Identity

When graveyard is your **theme**, you're actively milling yourself, discarding, and building around graveyard synergies. When you just run Eternal Witness for value, graveyard is incidental.

## Core Mechanics

- **Self-Mill** — Intentionally put cards from library into graveyard
- **Reanimation** — Return creatures from graveyard to battlefield
- **Recursion** — Return cards to hand or cast from graveyard
- **Delve/Escape/Flashback** — Use graveyard as resource

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Mill + Reanimation
Fill graveyard with fatties, cheat them into play.
- Entomb/Buried Alive + Reanimate/Animate Dead
- Mesmeric Orb + Syr Konrad = mill and damage

### Creature Loops
Repeatedly return creatures for value.
- Muldrotha + Lion's Eye Diamond = infinite mana/casts
- Meren + Spore Frog = repeatable fog

### Threshold/Delirium
Cards that improve with graveyard count.
- Threshold (7+ cards): Wonder, Genesis
- Delirium (4+ types): Deathreap Ritual, Ishkanah

## Role Categories

| Role | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Self-Mill | Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Altar of Dementia |
| Reanimation | Animate Dead, Reanimate, Living Death |
| Recursion | Eternal Witness, Regrowth, Muldrotha |
| Payoffs | Wonder (flying), Filth (swampwalk), Anger (haste) |
| Protection | Witchbane Orb, Ground Seal (meta-dependent) |

## Budget Alternatives

| Premium | Budget | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Entomb ($25) | Buried Alive ($2) | Gets 3 creatures instead of 1 |
| Reanimate ($15) | Animate Dead ($5) | Enchantment removal hits it |
| Life from the Loam ($15) | Crucible of Worlds ($8) | Different angle, similar effect |

## Common Mistakes

1. **No graveyard hate protection** — One Bojuka Bog ruins your day. Run hexproof or response
2. **All enablers, no payoffs** — Milling yourself does nothing without reanimation targets
3. **Too slow** — Graveyard decks can be glacial. Include burst turns (Living Death)
4. **Wrong targets** — Reanimate a value creature, not a mana dork

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Reanimator** — Cheat big creatures into play early
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice loops with recursion
- **Control** — Grind value through repeated recursion
- **Combo** — Graveyard-based combo lines
