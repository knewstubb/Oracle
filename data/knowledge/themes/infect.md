# Infect

## Overview

Infect decks use poison counters as an alternate win condition. Since players lose at 10 poison (not 40 life), infect fundamentally changes the math. The theme is aggressive, often voltron-adjacent, aiming to deal 10 poison as fast as possible.

## Identity

When infect is your **theme**, you're all-in on poison as your win condition. When you run one infect creature for incidental value, infect is a mechanic.

## Core Mechanics

- **Infect** — Damage to players is poison counters, damage to creatures is -1/-1 counters
- **Toxic** — Deals poison on combat damage (fixed amount, not damage-based)
- **Proliferate** — Add poison counters to already-poisoned players
- **Evasion** — Unblockable/flying to guarantee damage connects

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Evasion + Pump
Get through blockers, pump for lethal poison.
- Blighted Agent (unblockable) + pump spells = one-shot potential
- Distortion Strike + Giant Growth = 6 poison, repeatable

### Proliferate Acceleration
Don't need 10 damage — get some poison, then proliferate.
- Deal 5 poison, then proliferate 5 times
- Atraxa proliferates every end step

### -1/-1 Counter Control
Infect creatures' damage to creatures removes blockers.
- Plague Stinger kills small creatures while poisoning players
- Combine with wither effects for board control

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not enough infect creatures** — If your one infect creature dies, you have no win condition
2. **Splitting damage types** — Don't run infect and regular damage. Pick a lane
3. **No protection** — Infect creatures are kill-on-sight. Run hexproof, regenerate, indestructible
4. **Forgetting proliferate** — You don't need 10 infect damage, just 10 poison total

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Voltron** — Pump one creature for lethal poison
- **Aggro** — Fast, evasive infect creatures
- **Combo** — Proliferate-based poison acceleration

## Social Contract Note

Infect is controversial in casual pods. The 10 poison threshold feels "unfair" to some players. Discuss with your group before building.
