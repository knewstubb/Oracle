# Landfall

## Overview

Landfall decks trigger abilities whenever lands enter the battlefield. The theme involves maximizing land drops (extra lands, fetch lands, land recursion) to generate repeated value from landfall triggers.

## Identity

When landfall is your **theme**, you're building around landfall triggers and land drop acceleration. When you run one landfall card for value, landfall is a mechanic.

## Core Mechanics

- **Landfall Triggers** — Abilities that trigger on land ETB
- **Extra Land Drops** — Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Azusa
- **Fetch Lands** — Sacrifice for land = 2 landfall triggers
- **Land Recursion** — Replay lands from graveyard (Crucible, Ramunap)

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Fetch + Landfall
Fetch lands sacrifice then find a land — two triggers.
- Evolving Wilds + Omnath, Locus of Creation = 4 mana, 4 life
- Fetch + Lotus Cobra = 2 mana net positive

### Extra Drops + Payoffs
More land drops = more triggers per turn.
- Azusa + Avenger of Zendikar = 3 landfall triggers for tokens
- Exploration + Tatyova = draw multiple cards per turn

### Land Recursion Loop
Replay lands from graveyard for repeated triggers.
- Crucible of Worlds + fetch land = 1 landfall every turn forever
- Splendid Reclamation = massive burst turn

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not enough lands** — Landfall needs lands to play. Run 38-40 lands
2. **No extra land drops** — One landfall per turn is too slow
3. **Wrong payoffs** — Not all landfall cards are equal. Prioritize impact
4. **Ignoring the graveyard** — Recursion dramatically extends gas

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Ramp** — Natural fit, lands = mana and triggers
- **Tokens** — Avenger of Zendikar builds
- **Combo** — Infinite landfall with bounce lands
