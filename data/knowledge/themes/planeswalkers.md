# Planeswalkers (Superfriends)

## Overview

Planeswalker-focused decks (superfriends) treat planeswalkers as the primary value engines. The theme involves protecting walkers, proliferating loyalty, and ultimating for game-ending effects.

## Identity

When planeswalkers is your **theme**, you're running 15-25+ planeswalkers and building around their synergies. When it's incidental (1-3 walkers for value), it's not the theme.

## Core Mechanics

- **Proliferate** — Add loyalty counters to all your walkers at once
- **Loyalty Manipulation** — Doubling Season lets walkers ultimate immediately
- **Protection** — Prevent damage, fog effects, pillowfort pieces
- **Static Abilities** — War of the Spark uncommon walkers provide passive value

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Doubling Season + Walker
Walkers enter with double loyalty. Many can ultimate immediately.
- Doubling Season + Tamiyo, Field Researcher = instant emblem
- Doubling Season + Ugin, the Spirit Dragon = enters at 14

### Proliferate Engine
Proliferate all walkers simultaneously.
- Atraxa, Praetors' Voice — proliferate every end step
- Evolution Sage — landfall proliferate
- Flux Channeler — noncreature spell proliferate

### Walker Protection Loop
Protect walkers while they accumulate value.
- Fog effects buy time (Constant Mists, Spike Weaver)
- Pillowfort pieces deter attacks (Propaganda, Sphere of Safety)
- Token generation blocks (Elspeth, Sun's Champion)

## Common Mistakes

1. **No protection plan** — Walkers die to combat. Run fogs, tokens, or pillowfort
2. **All ultimates, no value** — If you can't protect them, run walkers with good +1 abilities
3. **Ignoring the board** — Walkers can't block. You need creatures or token generation
4. **Too greedy on colors** — 4-5 color superfriends needs excellent mana

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Control** — Counter threats, protect walkers, ultimate for win
- **Midrange** — Value walkers that generate incremental advantage
- **Pillowfort** — Deter attacks, slowly accumulate loyalty
