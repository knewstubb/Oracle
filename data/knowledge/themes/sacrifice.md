# Sacrifice

## Overview

Sacrifice decks treat creatures (and other permanents) as fuel. The theme involves generating disposable permanents, sacrificing them for value, and triggering death effects. Often called "aristocrats" when focused on drain effects.

## Identity

When sacrifice is your **theme**, you're building around death triggers and sacrifice outlets. When you just sacrifice occasionally (Korvold eating a treasure), sacrifice is a mechanic, not the theme.

## Core Mechanics

- **Sacrifice Outlets** — Ways to sacrifice at will (free preferred)
- **Death Triggers** — Effects when creatures die
- **Token Generation** — Create fodder to sacrifice
- **Recursion** — Bring back sacrificed creatures to repeat

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Aristocrat Core
Sacrifice outlet + death trigger payoff + fodder = consistent drain.
- Viscera Seer + Blood Artist + Reassembling Skeleton
- Free sac outlet + Grave Pact = one-sided board control

### Treasure/Token Conversion
Convert tokens into cards, mana, or damage.
- Korvold + treasures = draw + counters
- Pitiless Plunderer + sac outlet = treasure per death

### Persist/Undying Loops
Creatures that return when they die.
- Persist creature + counter remover + sac outlet = infinite
- Mikaeus + Triskelion = infinite damage

| Role | Examples |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not enough fodder** — You need things to sacrifice. Run token producers
2. **Too few sac outlets** — Drawing payoffs without outlets feels bad. Run 8-10 outlets
3. **No recursion** — Running out of creatures to sacrifice. Include ways to rebuy
4. **Ignoring life totals** — Aristocrats wins through small increments. Track opponent life

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Aristocrats** — Classic drain-based sacrifice
- **Combo** — Infinite loops with persist/undying
- **Control** — Grave Pact effects control the board
- **Reanimator** — Sacrifice then reanimate bigger
