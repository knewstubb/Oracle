# Spellslinger

## Overview

Spellslinger decks treat instants and sorceries as the primary game plan. The theme involves casting many spells, copying them, and winning through spell-based damage or combo finishes rather than creature combat.

## Identity

When spellslinger is your **theme**, the deck runs few creatures and many instants/sorceries. When you just run good instants in a creature deck, it's incidental.

## Core Mechanics

- **Spell Copy** — Fork, Twincast, copying your own spells
- **Magecraft/Prowess** — Triggers when casting or copying spells
- **Storm** — Spells with storm copy themselves
- **Spell Cost Reduction** — Make spells cheaper to chain

## Key Synergy Patterns

### Magecraft Engine
Each spell cast (and copy) triggers magecraft.
- Archmage Emeritus — draw per spell
- Storm-Kiln Artist — treasure per spell

### Copy Chains
Copy your own copy spells for exponential effect.
- Fork + Fork + big spell = 4 copies
- Thousand-Year Storm — each spell copies based on prior spells

### Ritual → Storm
Generate mana, cast cheap spells, storm off.
- Mana Geyser + Bonus Round + Grapeshot
- Underworld Breach + Brain Freeze

## Role Categories

| Role | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Cost Reduction | Goblin Electromancer, Baral, Primal Amulet |
| Copy Effects | Fork, Twincast, Fury Storm |
| Payoffs | Guttersnipe, Archmage Emeritus, Storm-Kiln Artist |
| Finishers | Grapeshot, Aetherflux Reservoir, Crackle with Power |
| Protection | Counterspells, Red Elemental Blast |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not enough draw** — Spellslinger dumps hands fast. Run cantrips
2. **No protection** — Single counterspell ruins your storm turn
3. **Wrong finishers** — Brain Freeze needs critical mass; Aetherflux needs life
4. **Creatures dying** — Guttersnipe is kill-on-sight. Run multiple payoffs

## Compatible Archetypes

- **Storm/Combo** — Chain spells to lethal storm count
- **Control** — Counter-heavy with spell-based wins
- **Burn** — Spell damage to face
