# Vehicles

## Overview

Vehicles are artifact creatures that require crewing—tapping creatures with total power equal to or greater than the crew cost. They dodge sorcery-speed removal while not crewed and often have powerful stats relative to cost.

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Vehicles provide efficient stats and dodge board wipes when not crewed. Combine with tokens, cheap creatures, and pilot synergies for consistent crewing.

**Key Elements:**
- Efficient vehicles with low crew costs
- Token generators for crew fodder
- Pilot creature synergies
- Ways to animate vehicles without crewing
- Artifact synergies

## Building Tips

1. **Run token generators** — Need creatures to crew
2. **Include artifact synergies** — Vehicles are artifacts
3. **Consider animation effects** — Skip crewing entirely
4. **Watch crew costs** — Lower is more reliable
