# Angels

## Overview

Angels are iconic white creatures representing divine power through flying, lifelink, and protective abilities. Angel tribal in Commander combines defensive resilience with powerful individual threats, typically leveraging lifegain synergies and board control.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Flying evasion on virtually every creature
- Lifelink is extremely common
- Strong individual bodies (4/4 minimum)
- Excellent defensive and protective abilities

**Weaknesses:**
- Very high mana costs (average CMC 5-6)
- Slow to develop without ramp
- Few low-curve options
- Limited to primarily white

**Typical Colors:** White (primary), some in Red, Black, or multicolor

## Unique Mechanics

### Angel-Specific Themes
- **Lifelink** — Built into many angels
- **Protection effects** — Angels often grant indestructible or protection
- **Flying tribal** — All angels fly by default
- **Lifegain payoffs** — Many trigger on life gain

### Play Patterns

Angel tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Ramp aggressively
2. **Mid game** — Deploy first angels, stabilize
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming air force with lifelink
4. **Win** — Life total advantage + flying damage

## Building Tips

1. **Ramp is critical** — Angels are expensive; prioritize mana acceleration
2. **Cost reduction** — Herald of War, Urza's Incubator, Starnheim Aspirant
3. **Don't overload high CMC** — Include some 4-5 CMC angels
4. **Lifegain matters** — Build around the lifegain synergies
5. **Protection** — Avacyn, Shalai, or board protection spells

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All expensive angels** — Can't cast anything until turn 5-6
2. **No ramp** — Relying on natural mana development is too slow
3. **Missing low-curve** — Need early plays while ramping
4. **Ignoring lifegain synergy** — Angels have great lifegain payoffs
5. **No interaction** — Need some removal, not just creatures

## Synergy Partners

Angels pair well with:
- **Lifegain** — Natural synergy with lifelink
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Archangel of Thune]] distribution
- **Flicker/Blink** — ETB angels have strong effects
- **Reanimator** — Expensive creatures from graveyard

## Meta Considerations

- **Creature removal** — High-value angels draw removal
- **Board wipes** — Less devastating than tokens but still hurts
- **Flying hate** — [[Gravity Well]], [[Bower Passage]] ground angels
- **Fast aggro** — Angels are slow; need early interaction
- **Lifegain hate** — [[Erebos]], [[Sulfuric Vortex]] shut down lifegain

## Giada Builds

[[Giada, Font of Hope]] is the premier angel commander:

### Mana Acceleration
Giada provides angel-specific ramp:
- Tap for mana specifically for angel spells
- Combines with cost reducers for explosive turns
- Can cast bigger angels earlier than expected

### +1/+1 Counter Synergy
Angels ETB with counters equal to angels controlled:
- First angel gets 1 counter (Giada counts)
- Scales quickly with more angels
- Pairs with counter doublers for explosive growth

### Key Synergies
- [[The Ozolith]] — Save counters when angels die
- [[Archangel of Thune]] — Distribute more counters
- [[Inspiring Call]] — Draw and protect based on counters
