# Bears

## Overview

Bears are green's iconic 2/2 for 2 creatures, but they've grown into a legitimate tribal strategy with Ayula. Bear tribal focuses on fight mechanics and efficient green creatures.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: None
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-green

## Key Mechanics

- **Fight**: Bears frequently fight other creatures
- **Efficient Stats**: 2/2 baseline is solid
- **Counter Growth**: Several bears grow with counters
- **Simple Aggro**: Straightforward beatdown

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Ayula, Queen Among Bears — Fight or counters on bear ETB
- Ayula's Influence — Discard land to make bears
- Words of Wilding — Replace draws with bears

### Notable Bears
- Runeclaw Bear — The original 2/2 for 2
- Ashcoat Bear — Flash bear
- Wilson, Refined Grizzly — Backgrounds commander
- Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma — Cost reduction

### Support
- Bearscape — Exile from graveyard for bears
- Bear Umbra — Untap lands on attack

## Common Commanders

- **Ayula, Queen Among Bears** — The definitive bear tribal commander
- **Wilson, Refined Grizzly** — Choose a Background option
- **Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma** — Big creature support

## Synergy Partners

Bears pair well with:
- **Fight** — Core mechanic via Ayula
- **Counters** — Growth option
- **Stompy** — Efficient creatures
- **Budget** — Bears are cheap

## Budget Notes

Bear tribal is one of the most budget-friendly strategies in Commander. Most bears cost pennies.
