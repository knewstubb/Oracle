# Beasts

## Overview

Beasts are green's primal creatures — big, aggressive, and numerous. They range from efficient midrange threats to massive finishers. Beast tribal leverages green's creature-based ramp and card advantage to overwhelm opponents with raw power.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Red
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-green, Gruul (RG), Naya (WRG)

## Key Mechanics

- **High Power/Toughness**: Efficient stats for cost
- **Trample**: Common keyword
- **Mana Dorks**: Some beasts produce mana
- **Provoke/Fight**: Creature-based removal

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Ravenous Baloth — Sacrifice for life
- Contested Cliffs — Repeatable fight effect
- Wirewood Savage — Draw on beast ETB
- Krosan Warchief — Cost reduction and regeneration

### Efficient Beasts
- Thragtusk — ETB life, leaves behind token
- Craterhoof Behemoth — Game-ending finisher
- Elder Gargaroth — Modal attack/block triggers

### Support
- Garruk's Packleader — Draw on 3+ power creatures
- Where Ancients Tread — 5 damage on big creature ETB

## Common Commanders

- **Marath, Will of the Wild** — Naya counters/tokens
- **Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma** — Cost reduction and trample
- **Surrak Dragonclaw** — Temur creatures can't be countered

## Synergy Partners

Beasts pair well with:
- **Ramp** — Play big creatures fast
- **Counters** — Many beasts have counter synergies
- **Stompy** — Big creature aggro
- **Landfall** — Green's land synergies

## Notes

Beast is a broad category — many green creatures are beasts without explicit tribal support. The tribe benefits more from general "big creature" synergies than narrow tribal effects.
