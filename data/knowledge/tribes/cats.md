# Cats

## Overview

Cats are a Selesnya-focused tribe combining aggressive combat with lifegain synergies. They feature solid lords, token generation, and equipment synergies.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** White, Green (Selesnya)
**Secondary:** Naya with [[Rin and Seri, Inseparable]]

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Build a board of efficient cats, pump them with lords, and generate value through lifegain and equipment synergies.

## Essential Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- [[King of the Pride]] — +2/+1 anthem
- [[Feline Sovereign]] — Lord + artifact/enchantment hate
- [[Regal Caracal]] — Lord + lifelink tokens
- [[Kaheera, the Orphanguard]] — Vigilance lord

### Token Generators
- [[Pride Sovereign]] — Exert for cat tokens
- [[Brimaz, King of Oreskos]] — Tokens on attack/block
- [[Kemba, Kha Regent]] — Tokens based on equipment
- [[Adorned Pouncer]] — Eternalize for 4/4 double strike

### Support
- [[Arahbo, Roar of the World]] — Eminence pump
- [[Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist]] — Combat control
- [[Qasali Slingers]] — Removal on cat ETB
- [[White Sun's Zenith]] — Instant speed cat army

## Common Commanders

- **[[Arahbo, Roar of the World]]** — Eminence pump from command zone
- **[[Rin and Seri, Inseparable]]** — Cat/dog hybrid
- **[[Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist]]** — Combat-focused
- **[[Jetmir, Nexus of Revels]]** — Go-wide payoff (Naya)

## Building Tips

1. **Lean into equipment** — Many cats synergize
2. **Include lifegain payoffs** — Natural lifelink tribe
3. **Run token doublers** — Cats go wide effectively
4. **Consider [[Arahbo]]** — Eminence is always active
