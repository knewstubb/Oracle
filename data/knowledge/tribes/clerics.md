# Clerics

## Overview

Clerics are a white-black creature type focused on lifegain, sacrifice, and religious themes. They bridge the gap between aggressive white weenie strategies and black's drain effects, making them natural fits for Orzhov aristocrats builds.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: White, Black
- **Secondary**: None
- **Common Pairs**: Orzhov (WB)

## Key Mechanics

- **Lifegain**: Many clerics gain life or have lifelink
- **Life Payment**: Black clerics pay life for effects
- **Sacrifice**: Cleric-specific sacrifice effects
- **Party**: Counts as a party member

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Rotlung Reanimator — Zombie on cleric death
- Edgewalker — Cleric cost reduction
- Battletide Alchemist — Prevent damage based on cleric count
- Orah, Skyclave Hierophant — Reanimates on cleric death

### Key Clerics
- Shadowborn Apostle — 6 can tutor any demon
- Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim — Sacrifice outlet, exile
- Starlit Sanctum — Land that sacrifices clerics
- Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose — Lifegain → damage

### Lifegain Support
- Soul Warden / Soul's Attendant — Trigger on creatures
- Suture Priest — Damages opponents too
- Righteous Valkyrie — Anthem at 27+ life

## Common Commanders

- **Orah, Skyclave Hierophant** — Cleric death triggers chain reanimation
- **Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim** — Sacrifice and lifegain
- **Athreos, God of Passage** — Death tax on clerics

## Synergy Partners

Clerics pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Death triggers and sacrifice
- **Lifegain** — Core mechanic
- **Party** — Cleric is a party type
- **Shadowborn Apostle** — Unique cleric deck

## Unique Build: Shadowborn Apostles

Running 30+ Shadowborn Apostles creates a unique deck that tutors demons repeatedly. Combine with Thrumming Stone for explosive turns.
