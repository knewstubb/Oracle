# Constructs

## Overview

Constructs are colorless artifact creatures representing magical automatons and machines. They synergize with artifact-matters strategies and often have modular or counter-based mechanics.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Colorless
- **Secondary**: Blue, White
- **Common Pairs**: Colorless, Azorius (WU), Esper (WUB)

## Key Mechanics

- **Artifact Synergy**: Benefit from artifact lords
- **Modular**: +1/+1 counters move on death
- **Colorless Costs**: Often have generic mana costs
- **Token Generation**: Many effects create construct tokens

## Signature Cards

### Modular Constructs
- Arcbound Ravager — Sacrifice artifacts, move counters
- Arcbound Overseer — Distributes counters
- Zabaz, the Glimmerwasp — Modular lord

### Notable Constructs
- Metalwork Colossus — Cost reduction from artifacts
- Phyrexian Triniform — Creates tokens on death
- Triplicate Titan — ETB and death triggers

### Support
- Urza's Saga — Tutors and makes constructs
- The Antiquities War — Animate artifacts

## Common Commanders

- **Karn, Silver Golem** — Animates artifacts
- **Zabaz, the Glimmerwasp** — Modular tribal
- **Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter** — Flash for artifacts

## Synergy Partners

Constructs pair well with:
- **Artifacts-matter** — Core synergy
- **Counters** — Modular mechanics
- **Colorless** — Eldrazi crossover
- **Affinity** — Cost reduction
