# Demons

## Overview

Demons are black's iconic flying creatures — powerful but with a cost. They typically have high stats, evasion, and devastating abilities balanced by life payment, sacrifice requirements, or other drawbacks. Demon tribal embraces these Faustian bargains.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Black
- **Secondary**: Red (Rakdos demons)
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-black, Rakdos (BR)

## Key Mechanics

- **Life Payment**: Power at the cost of life
- **Sacrifice Requirements**: Feed creatures to demons
- **Flying**: Nearly universal evasion
- **Deals/Pacts**: Powerful effects with downsides

## Signature Cards

### Iconic Demons
- Griselbrand — Draw 7 for 7 life (banned in Commander)
- Razaketh, the Foulblooded — Tutor on sacrifice
- Vilis, Broker of Blood — Draw cards when losing life
- Archfiend of Despair — Opponents can't gain life, double damage

### Lords and Support
- Liliana's Contract — Win condition if you control 4+ demons
- Ob Nixilis, Unshackled — Punishes searching
- Belzenlok — Draw demons, lose life

### Reanimation Targets
- Rune-Scarred Demon — Tutor on ETB
- Sire of Insanity — Hellbent enabler
- Demon of Dark Schemes — Energy and reanimation

## Common Commanders

- **Rakdos, Lord of Riots** — Cost reduction based on life lost
- **Kaalia of the Vast** — Cheats demons (and angels/dragons) into play
- **Be'lakor, the Dark Master** — Demon tribal payoffs

## Synergy Partners

Demons pair well with:
- **Reanimator** — Cheat expensive demons into play
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice synergies
- **Lifegain** — Offset life payment costs
- **Big Mana** — High casting costs

## Budget Considerations

Demon tribal can be expensive — many iconic demons are $10+ due to casual demand. Budget builds focus on reanimation rather than hardcasting.
