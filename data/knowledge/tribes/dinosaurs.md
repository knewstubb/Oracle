# Dinosaurs

## Overview

Dinosaurs are massive, aggressive creatures introduced in Ixalan. The tribe features high-power creatures with the unique Enrage mechanic—abilities that trigger when the dinosaur takes damage.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Red, Green, White (Naya)
**Secondary:** Some mono-green and Gruul options exist

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Ramp into massive dinosaurs early, trigger Enrage for additional value, and overwhelm opponents with enormous creatures that demand answers.

**Key Elements:**
- Heavy ramp package for expensive creatures
- Enrage enablers (controlled damage)
- Cost reduction effects
- Haste enablers for immediate impact
- Creature tutors to find key dinosaurs

## Building Tips

1. **Run 10+ ramp sources** — Dinosaurs are expensive
2. **Include Enrage enablers** — Core synergy
3. **Add haste** — Immediate impact from expensive threats
4. **Consider [[Marauding Raptor]]** — Auto-enrage on ETB
