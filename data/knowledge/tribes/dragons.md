# Dragons

## Overview

Dragons are Magic's most iconic creature type — massive flying threats that dominate combat and often carry devastating abilities. Dragon tribal in Commander leverages cost reduction, reanimation, and ETB effects to deploy these expensive threats efficiently.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Flying evasion on virtually every member
- Powerful individual bodies (4/4 minimum, often 6/6+)
- Many have ETB or attack triggers
- Strong thematic support and dedicated lords

**Weaknesses:**
- High mana costs (average CMC 5-7)
- Vulnerable to flyer hate
- Difficult to rebuild after board wipes
- Slow without cost reduction

**Typical Colors:** Red (primary), Green (ramp), White (tokens), Black (reanimation), Blue (control dragons)

## Unique Mechanics

### Dragon-Specific Support
- **Treasure synergy** — Many dragons create treasure ([[Goldspan Dragon]], [[Ancient Copper Dragon]])
- **Cost reduction** — Dragon-specific reducers ([[Dragonlord's Servant]], [[Dragonspeaker Shaman]])
- **Dragon tokens** — Generate more dragons ([[Lathliss, Dragon Queen]], [[Dragon Broodmother]])
- **Dragon counting** — Effects that scale with dragons ([[Dragon Tempest]], [[Kolaghan, the Storm's Fury]])

### Play Patterns

Dragon tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Ramp and cost reduction
2. **Mid game** — Deploy first dragon threats
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming flying force with triggers

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize cost reduction** — Dragons are expensive; reduce costs or cheat into play
2. **Include reanimation** — [[Bladewing]], [[Patriarch's Bidding]] recover from wraths
3. **Haste enablers** — Dragons need to attack; [[Dragon Tempest]], [[Fires of Yavimaya]]
4. **Ramp aggressively** — 38-40 lands plus significant ramp package
5. **Don't overload 6+ drops** — Keep curve reasonable; include some 4-5 CMC dragons

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All expensive dragons** — Can't cast anything before turn 5-6
2. **No acceleration** — Relying on natural curve is too slow
3. **Overextending into wraths** — Keep threats in hand; don't dump everything
4. **Ignoring early game** — Need blockers/interaction before dragons come down
5. **Cutting non-dragons** — Support cards are critical; don't go 40+ dragons

## Synergy Partners

Dragons pair well with:
- **Treasure** — Many dragons make treasure; synergizes with payoffs
- **Reanimator** — Cheat expensive dragons from graveyard
- **ETB themes** — Blink or copy dragons for triggers
- **Power-matters** — Dragons have high power naturally

## Meta Considerations

- **Flying hate** — [[Gravity Well]], [[Dense Canopy]] ground dragons
- **Creature removal** — High-value targets draw removal
- **Board wipes** — Devastating; include recursion
- **Fast combo** — Dragons are slow; need interaction
- **Stax** — Tax effects hurt expensive dragons
