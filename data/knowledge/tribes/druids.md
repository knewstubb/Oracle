# Druids

## Overview

Druids are green's spellcasting creature type focused on mana production, lands, and nature magic. Many of green's best mana dorks are druids, making the tribe central to ramp strategies.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: White, Black
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-green, Selesnya (GW), Abzan (WBG)

## Key Mechanics

- **Mana Production**: Many druids tap for mana
- **Land Synergy**: Landfall and land tutoring
- **Creature Ramp**: Enable bigger plays
- **Untap Abilities**: Self-untap for extra mana

## Signature Cards

### Mana Druids
- Llanowar Elves — Classic 1-drop
- Elvish Archdruid — Counts elves (druid subtype)
- Priest of Titania — Taps for elf count
- Circle of Dreams Druid — Taps for creature count

### Lords and Payoffs
- Gilt-Leaf Archdruid — Draw on druid cast, steal lands
- Seton, Krosan Protector — All druids tap for G

### Notable Druids
- Oracle of Mul Daya — Extra land drops
- Seedborn Muse — Untap all on opponents' turns
- Reclamation Sage — Naturalize on a body

## Common Commanders

- **Seton, Krosan Protector** — Druid mana tribal
- **Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury** — Elf/druid tokens
- **Yedora, Grave Gardener** — Lands-matter druids

## Synergy Partners

Druids pair well with:
- **Elves** — Massive type overlap
- **Ramp** — Core identity
- **Lands Matter** — Natural synergy
- **Untap** — Seedborn/Seton combos
