# Eldrazi

## Overview

Eldrazi are cosmic horrors from the Blind Eternities—colorless creatures of immense power. The tribe features the devastating Annihilator mechanic and abilities that exile and process cards.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Colorless
**Secondary:** Devoid Eldrazi span all colors but are colorless

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Ramp into massive colorless threats that warp the game. Eldrazi demand answers and punish opponents who can't deal with them immediately.

**Key Elements:**
- Massive mana acceleration (colorless sources)
- Annihilator for resource destruction
- Cast triggers (many Eldrazi trigger on cast, not ETB)
- Processor/Ingest synergies
- Protection for investments

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize colorless mana sources** — Wastes, colorless lands
2. **Include ramp artifacts** — Sol Ring, Thran Dynamo
3. **Protect your investments** — Counterspells, protection
4. **Cast triggers matter** — Many Eldrazi trigger on cast, not ETB
