# Elementals

## Overview

Elementals are a diverse tribe spanning all five colors, often tied to landfall and natural forces. The tribe has excellent commander options and strong synergies with land-based strategies.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Green, Red, Blue
**Popular Combinations:** Temur (GUR), 4-color Omnath, 5-color Horde

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Generate value through landfall triggers and elemental synergies. Many elementals provide ramp or card advantage, creating engines that snowball.

**Key Elements:**
- Landfall synergies (many elementals trigger on land ETB)
- Elemental lords and payoffs
- Ramp package (natural fit)
- Graveyard recursion ([[Horde of Notions]])
- Evoke creatures for value

## Essential Cards

### Tribal Payoffs
- [[Risen Reef]] — Draw/ramp on elemental ETB
- [[Voice of Many]] — Draw based on creatures
- [[Omnath, Locus of the Roil]] — Elemental ETB damage/counters

### Landfall Elementals
- [[Omnath, Locus of Rage]] — 5/5s on landfall
- [[Omnath, Locus of Creation]] — Multi-landfall triggers
- [[Avenger of Zendikar]] — Plant army + landfall pump
- [[Roil Elemental]] — Steal creatures on landfall
- [[Scute Swarm]] — Exponential tokens

### Evoke Value
- [[Mulldrifter]] — Draw 2, cheap with evoke
- [[Shriekmaw]] — Removal on legs
- [[Solitude]] — Free exile removal
- [[Fury]] — Free damage-based removal
- [[Foundation Breaker]] — Naturalize on legs

## Common Commanders

- **[[Horde of Notions]]** — 5-color, graveyard recursion
- **[[Omnath, Locus of Rage]]** — Gruul landfall tokens
- **[[Omnath, Locus of Creation]]** — 4-color landfall value
- **[[Maelstrom Wanderer]]** — Temur cascade
- **[[Animar, Soul of Elements]]** — Cost reduction

## Building Tips

1. **Lean into landfall** — Natural synergy
2. **Include [[Risen Reef]]** — Core engine card
3. **Run fetch lands** — Extra landfall triggers
4. **Consider evoke creatures** — Value even without tribal focus
