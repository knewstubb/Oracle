# Elves

## Overview

Elves are Magic's premier mana-generating tribe, capable of explosive ramp that funds massive plays or overwhelming board states. Elf tribal in Commander excels at converting a few early elves into an army through mana multiplication and elf-count payoffs.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional mana generation
- Fast board development
- Strong card draw in green
- Excellent synergy density
- Budget-friendly staples

**Weaknesses:**
- Fragile creatures (most are 1/1 or 2/2)
- Vulnerable to board wipes
- Relies on critical mass
- Limited flying/evasion

**Typical Colors:** Green (primary), Black (elfball + removal), White (go-wide support)

## Unique Mechanics

### Mana Dorks
Elves have more mana dorks than any other tribe:
- [[Llanowar Elves]], [[Elvish Mystic]], [[Fyndhorn Elves]] — The 1-drop trio
- [[Priest of Titania]] — Tap for mana equal to all elves
- [[Elvish Archdruid]] — Same plus lord effect
- [[Circle of Dreams Druid]] — Creature-based Gaea's Cradle

### Elf-Count Matters
Many payoffs scale with elf count:
- [[Ezuri, Renegade Leader]] — Overrun for X
- [[Elvish Champion]] — Forestwalk for all elves
- [[Lathril, Blade of the Elves]] — Tap elves to drain
- [[Timberwatch Elf]] — Power boost equal to elves

### Play Patterns

Elf tribal is consistently explosive:
1. **Turn 1-2** — Deploy mana dorks
2. **Turn 3-4** — Mana multiplication (Priest of Titania)
3. **Turn 4-5** — Explosive turn with card draw into more elves
4. **Win** — Overrun effects or combo finish

## Building Tips

1. **Mana dork density** — Run 10-15 mana-producing elves
2. **Card draw is essential** — Elves empty hands fast; refill constantly
3. **Protection** — [[Heroic Intervention]], [[Ezuri's regeneration]] for wrath recovery
4. **Untap effects** — Maximize value from Priest of Titania
5. **Low curve** — Most of your elves should be 1-3 CMC

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No card draw** — Dumping hand with no refill
2. **All mana, no payoffs** — Need to convert mana into wins
3. **Overextending** — Board wipes devastate; hold some back
4. **Cutting mana dorks** — They're the engine; don't skimp
5. **Ignoring interaction** — Need some removal/protection

## Synergy Partners

Elves pair well with:
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Ezuri, Claw of Progress]], [[Rishkar]]
- **Tokens** — Many elves make tokens
- **Landfall** — [[Ashaya]] makes creatures into lands
- **Combo** — Natural infinite mana potential

## Meta Considerations

- **Board wipes** — Elves fold to wraths; need protection/recursion
- **Flying** — Few elves fly; can be flown over
- **Artifact hate** — Doesn't affect elves much
- **Fast combo** — Elves can race most combo decks
- **Stax** — Tax effects slow down cheap creatures less

## Infinite Mana Combos

Elves have multiple paths to infinite mana:
- **Priest of Titania + Umbral Mantle** — With 4+ elves
- **Marwyn + Umbral Mantle** — With 4+ power on Marwyn
- **Devoted Druid + Vizier of Remedies** — Classic combo
- **Ashaya + Quirion Ranger** — With mana dork

Convert infinite mana through [[Ezuri]], [[Staff of Domination]], [[Walking Ballista]], or similar.
