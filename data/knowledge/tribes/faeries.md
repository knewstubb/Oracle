# Faeries

## Overview

Faeries are a blue-black tribe focused on evasion, flash, and disruption. They play a tempo-control game, deploying threats at instant speed while countering and bouncing opponent's plays. Faeries reward patient, reactive gameplay.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Blue, Black
- **Secondary**: None (strictly Dimir)
- **Common Pairs**: Dimir (UB)

## Key Mechanics

- **Flash**: Most faeries can be cast at instant speed
- **Flying**: Universal evasion
- **Disruption**: Counters, discard, bounce
- **Tribal Payoffs**: Effects that count faeries

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Scion of Oona — Flash, shroud for faeries, +1/+1
- Sprite Noble — Flying anthem
- Glen Elendra Liege — Anthems for blue and black

### Tempo Pieces
- Spellstutter Sprite — Counter spell with MV ≤ faerie count
- Mistbind Clique — Champion a faerie, tap all lands
- Vendilion Clique — Hand disruption

### Token Generation
- Bitterblossom — Iconic 1/1 flyer each upkeep
- Faerie Formation — Draw and make tokens
- Oona, Queen of the Fae — Mill and make tokens

## Common Commanders

- **Alela, Artful Provocateur** — Artifacts/enchantments make faeries
- **Oona, Queen of the Fae** — Mill-based token generation
- **Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor** — Faerie death triggers

## Synergy Partners

Faeries pair well with:
- **Control** — Tempo and disruption
- **Artifacts/Enchantments** — Alela synergy
- **Rogues** — Tribal overlap
- **Mill** — Oona strategy

## Budget Considerations

Bitterblossom is expensive but central to the strategy. Budget builds can work without it but lose consistency.
