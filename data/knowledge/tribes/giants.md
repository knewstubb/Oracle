# Giants

## Overview

Giants are a red-white tribe of massive humanoid creatures. Kaldheim expanded giant tribal significantly, giving them a dedicated commander and cohesive strategy around casting giants and copying spells.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Red
- **Secondary**: White
- **Common Pairs**: Boros (WR), Mono-red

## Key Mechanics

- **High Power**: 4+ power typically
- **Cost Reduction**: Tribal discounts
- **Combat Focus**: Attack triggers
- **Spell Copying**: Kaldheim giants copy spells

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Kalemne, Disciple of Iroas — Experience counters
- Sunrise Sovereign — Trample and +2/+2
- Stinkdrinker Daredevil — Cost reduction

### Kaldheim Giants
- Aegar, the Freezing Flame — Draw on excess damage
- Calamity Bearer — Double damage from giants
- Crystalline Giant — Collects keywords

### Notable Giants
- Hamletback Goliath — Grows massively
- Brion Stoutarm — Fling creatures
- Borderland Behemoth — Power from giant count

## Common Commanders

- **Aegar, the Freezing Flame** — Izzet damage tribal
- **Kalemne, Disciple of Iroas** — Boros giants
- **Ruhan of the Fomori** — Chaos giant

## Synergy Partners

Giants pair well with:
- **Big Creatures** — Natural affinity
- **Fling Effects** — High power
- **Damage Doubling** — Calamity Bearer
- **Boros Aggro** — Combat focus
