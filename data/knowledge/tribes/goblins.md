# Goblins

## Overview

Goblins are Magic's premier aggressive swarming tribe, flooding the board with cheap creatures and leveraging quantity over quality. Goblin tribal in Commander excels at explosive token generation, sacrifice combos, and overwhelming damage through sheer numbers.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Extremely low mana curve
- Explosive token generation ([[Krenko]])
- Strong sacrifice synergies
- Fast clock against slower decks
- Budget-friendly core

**Weaknesses:**
- Fragile bodies (mostly 1/1s)
- Vulnerable to board wipes
- Limited card advantage
- Struggles against pillow fort

**Typical Colors:** Red (primary), Black (sacrifice payoffs)

## Unique Mechanics

### Goblin-Specific Themes
- **Exponential token generation** — [[Krenko, Mob Boss]] doubles goblins
- **Sacrifice for damage** — [[Siege-Gang Commander]], [[Skirk Prospector]]
- **Goblin count matters** — Effects that scale with number of goblins
- **Haste** — Many goblins have or grant haste

### Play Patterns

Goblin tribal typically follows:
1. **Turn 1-2** — Deploy cheap goblins
2. **Turn 3-4** — Krenko or other multiplier hits the board
3. **Turn 4-5** — Explosive turns with exponential goblin growth
4. **Win** — Attack or sacrifice for lethal damage

## Building Tips

1. **Protect Krenko** — [[Lightning Greaves]], [[Swiftfoot Boots]] are essential
2. **Untap effects** — [[Thornbite Staff]], [[Umbral Mantle]] enable infinite goblins
3. **Haste enablers** — Goblins need to attack or tap immediately
4. **Don't overload on lords** — Need goblins to boost
5. **Include finishers** — Purphoros/Impact Tremors close games fast

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No protection for Krenko** — He's a massive target
2. **Missing haste** — Krenko needs to tap immediately
3. **Overextending into wraths** — Hold some back
4. **No sacrifice outlets** — Convert goblins to damage
5. **Ignoring card advantage** — Goblins empty hands fast

## Synergy Partners

Goblins pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Natural sacrifice synergy
- **Tokens** — Goblin generation is token-based
- **Spell copying** — [[Zada]], [[Wort]] builds
- **Artifact synergy** — [[Daretti]] goblin builds

## Meta Considerations

- **Board wipes** — Devastating to goblin armies
- **Pillow fort** — Tax effects slow down swarm attacks
- **Flying** — Goblins are almost all groundbound
- **Lifegain** — Counteracts damage strategies
- **Fast combo** — Goblins can race many combo decks

## Infinite Combos

Goblins have several infinite combo lines:

### Krenko + Thornbite Staff
With a sacrifice outlet that causes a creature to die:
1. Tap Krenko to make goblins
2. Sacrifice a goblin (triggers Thornbite Staff to untap Krenko)
3. Repeat for infinite goblins

### Krenko + Umbral Mantle
With 4+ goblins initially:
1. Tap Krenko to make goblins
2. Pay 3 and sacrifice to untap with Umbral Mantle
3. Repeat (net positive goblins each time)

### Skirk Prospector Loops
- [[Skirk Prospector]] + [[Phyrexian Altar]] + sacrifice payoff
- Endless mana and death triggers
