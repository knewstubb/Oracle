# Gods

## Overview

Gods are indestructible legendary creatures with devotion requirements or other activation conditions. They represent the pinnacle of their colors' philosophies and provide powerful, resilient threats that are difficult to remove permanently.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: All colors equally
- **Common Pairs**: Any — gods exist in all color combinations

## God Types

### Theros Gods (Devotion)
- Require devotion to become creatures
- Always indestructible enchantments
- Powerful static abilities

### Amonkhet Gods
- Always creatures, indestructible
- Return from graveyard under conditions
- Attack/block restrictions

### Kaldheim Gods
- Two-sided MDFC cards
- Front: God, Back: Related permanent
- No indestructibility by default

## Signature Cards

### Mono-Color Standouts
- Heliod, Sun-Crowned — Lifelink and counter distribution
- Thassa, Deep-Dwelling — Blink at end step
- Erebos, God of the Dead — No lifegain, card draw
- Purphoros, God of the Forge — 2 damage per creature ETB
- Nylea, God of the Hunt — Trample and pump

### Multi-Color
- Xenagos, God of Revels — Double power, haste
- Athreos, God of Passage — Death tax
- Karametra, God of Harvests — Ramp on creature cast

## Common Commanders

- **Purphoros, God of the Forge** — Burn via tokens
- **The Locust God** — Draw = flying tokens
- **Heliod, Sun-Crowned** — Combo with Walking Ballista

## Synergy Partners

Gods pair well with:
- **Devotion** — Enchantments that add pips
- **Superfriends** — Theros gods are enchantments
- **Indestructible** — Wrath-proof threats
- **Specific Archetypes** — Each god supports its theme

## Power Level Notes

Several gods are cEDH viable — Heliod combos, Purphoros burn, Thassa blink. Indestructibility makes them very resilient commanders.
