# Golems

## Overview

Golems are artifact creatures representing magical constructs made from various materials. They overlap with splicers from New Phyrexia, which create golem tokens with keyword abilities.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Colorless
- **Secondary**: White (splicers)
- **Common Pairs**: Colorless, Azorius (WU)

## Key Mechanics

- **Artifact Creature**: Benefit from artifact synergies
- **Splicer Synergy**: Splicers create and buff golems
- **Token Generation**: Many effects make golem tokens
- **High Toughness**: Durable blockers

## Signature Cards

### Splicers
- Blade Splicer — 3/3 first strike golem
- Sensor Splicer — Vigilance golems
- Master Splicer — +1/+1 to golems
- Wing Splicer — Flying golems

### Golem Support
- Precursor Golem — Copies spells to all golems
- Titan Forge — Makes 9/9 golems
- Golem Foundry — Counters become golems

### Notable Golems
- Blightsteel Colossus — Infect finisher
- Darksteel Colossus — Indestructible
- Phyrexian Colossus — Untap with life

## Common Commanders

- **Ich-Tekik, Salvage Splicer** — Golem tribal payoffs
- **Alibou, Ancient Witness** — Artifact creature attacks
- **Rebbec, Architect of Ascension** — Protection for artifacts

## Synergy Partners

Golems pair well with:
- **Blink** — Splicer ETB triggers
- **Artifacts-matter** — Core identity
- **Tokens** — Go-wide with golems
- **Partner** — Ich-Tekik combinations
