# Humans

## Overview

Humans are Magic's most prevalent creature type, spanning all colors with diverse abilities. Human tribal in Commander leverages this diversity for toolbox strategies, hatebear tactics, or go-wide aggression, often with excellent support cards that care about "human" as a type.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Available in all five colors
- Extremely diverse ability pool
- Strong hatebear options
- Excellent lord/support density
- Many pushed competitive cards are humans

**Weaknesses:**
- No inherent synergy (just a type)
- Less thematic cohesion than other tribes
- Requires building around "human matters" cards
- Can feel like "goodstuff" with human subtheme

**Typical Colors:** White (primary), Green (go-wide), Red (aggressive), all five colors viable

## Unique Mechanics

### Human-Specific Support
Unlike most tribes, humans don't have inherent mechanical identity. Instead, they benefit from:
- Generic tribal support that happens to be human
- "Human matters" cards like [[Champion of the Parish]]
- Hatebear bodies that are coincidentally human
- [[Winota]] and similar human-counting effects

### Play Patterns

Human tribal varies by build:
- **Hatebears** — Disrupt opponents with tax effects
- **Go-wide aggro** — Flood with tokens and lords
- **Toolbox** — Diverse answers for any situation
- **Reanimator** — Many key reanimator targets are human

## Building Tips

1. **Choose a direction** — Hatebears, aggro, or toolbox; don't mix too much
2. **Low curve for go-wide** — Champions and Lieutenants want cheap humans
3. **Hatebears need synergy** — Random hate pieces need to work together
4. **Include finishers** — Go-wide needs [[Craterhoof]] or similar effects
5. **Protection for key pieces** — Hatebears draw removal

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No focus** — "Goodstuff humans" without synergy
2. **Too many lords, not enough bodies** — Need humans to boost
3. **Missing removal** — Rely on hatebears too much
4. **Overextending** — Board wipes hurt go-wide
5. **Ignoring card advantage** — Humans empty hands fast

## Synergy Partners

Humans pair well with:
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Thalia's Lieutenant]], [[Champion of the Parish]]
- **Tokens** — Many human token generators
- **Hatebears/Stax** — Many hatebears are human
- **Equipment** — Humans can carry equipment well

## Meta Considerations

- **Board wipes** — Go-wide humans are vulnerable
- **Creature removal** — Key hatebears get targeted
- **Flying** — Most humans are groundbound
- **Fast combo** — Hatebears slow combo; aggro can race
- **Control** — Hatebears disrupt; aggro pressures

## Winota Builds

[[Winota, Joiner of Forces]] creates a unique human build:

### Non-Human Attackers
Need non-humans to trigger Winota:
- [[Raise the Alarm]] — Instant speed soldiers
- [[Blade Splicer]] — Golem + first strike human
- Token makers like [[Goblin Rabblemaster]]

### Human Payoffs
Cheated humans should have impact:
- [[Angrath's Marauders]] — Double damage
- [[Combat Celebrant]] — Extra combat
- [[Blade Historian]] — Double strike for team
- [[Thalia's Lieutenant]] — Grows as more humans enter

### Play Pattern
1. Deploy non-humans
2. Cast Winota
3. Attack, trigger Winota for each non-human
4. Chain into massive board of humans
