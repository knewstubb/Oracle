# Hydras

## Overview

Hydras are green's iconic X-cost creatures — they grow based on mana invested. Hydra tribal is a big mana strategy that aims to cast increasingly massive creatures, often with +1/+1 counter synergies.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Red, Black (Jund)
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-green, Gruul (RG), Simic (UG)

## Key Mechanics

- **X Costs**: Scale with available mana
- **+1/+1 Counters**: Enter with counters, often grow more
- **Trample**: Common keyword
- **Heads**: Flavor mechanic, counters represent heads

## Signature Cards

### Efficient Hydras
- Primordial Hydra — Doubles counters each upkeep
- Kalonian Hydra — Doubles counters on attack
- Managorger Hydra — Grows on any spell cast
- Hungering Hydra — Grows from damage dealt to it

### X-Cost Payoffs
- Unbound Flourishing — Double X costs
- Nyxbloom Ancient — Triple mana
- Rosheen Meanderer — Adds 4 for X costs

### Support
- Hardened Scales — Extra counter on each
- Doubling Season — Double counters
- Gyre Sage — Evolve mana dork

## Common Commanders

- **Gargos, Vicious Watcher** — Cost reduction, fight on target
- **Zaxara, the Exemplary** — X spells make hydra tokens
- **Gyrus, Waker of Corpses** — Jund hydra reanimation

## Synergy Partners

Hydras pair well with:
- **Counters** — Doubling Season, Hardened Scales
- **Ramp** — Big mana for big X costs
- **Stompy** — Overwhelming creature size
- **Simic** — Draw and counters

## Budget Considerations

Doubling Season and similar effects are expensive but dramatically improve the deck. Budget builds can focus on efficient hydras and counter doublers like Branching Evolution.
