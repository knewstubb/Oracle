# Knights

## Overview

Knights are an aggressive Mardu-aligned tribe with strong equipment synergies. Throne of Eldraine significantly boosted the tribe with powerful lords and payoffs.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** White, Black (Orzhov)
**Secondary:** Mardu (WBR), with red adding aggression

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Deploy efficient knights with first strike and other combat keywords, pump them with lords, and leverage equipment synergies.

## Essential Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- [[The Circle of Loyalty]] — Anthem + knight tokens
- [[Inspiring Veteran]] — +1/+1 anthem
- [[Worthy Knight]] — Tokens on knight cast
- [[Knight Exemplar]] — Indestructible knights

### Efficient Knights
- [[Murderous Rider]] — Removal + body
- [[Cavalier of Dawn]] — ETB vindicate
- [[Skyhunter Strike Force]] — Melee keyword
- [[Puresteel Paladin]] — Equipment draw engine

### Equipment Synergy
- [[Syr Gwyn, Hero of Ashvale]] — Free equip, draws cards
- [[Fervent Champion]] — Free equip on attack
- [[Danitha Capashen, Paragon]] — Cost reduction

## Common Commanders

- **[[Syr Gwyn, Hero of Ashvale]]** — Knights + equipment
- **[[Aryel, Knight of Windgrace]]** — Knight tokens + removal
- **[[Sidar Jabari of Zhalfir]]** — Eminence recursion

## Building Tips

1. **Lean into equipment** — Natural synergy
2. **Include first strike lords** — Makes combat favorable
3. **Run efficient knights** — Tribe rewards aggressive curves
4. **Consider going Mardu** — Access to best options
