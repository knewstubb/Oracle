# Krakens

## Overview

Krakens (along with Leviathans, Octopuses, and Serpents) are blue's massive sea monsters. They require heavy mana investment but provide devastating threats. Often grouped together as "sea monsters" tribal.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Blue
- **Secondary**: Green (Simic)
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-blue, Simic (UG)

## Key Mechanics

- **High Mana Cost**: 6+ mana typically
- **Islandwalk**: Unblockable vs islands
- **Cost Reduction**: Often have alternative costs
- **Mass Size**: 6/6 or larger bodies

## Signature Cards

### Iconic Krakens
- Kraken of the Straits — Blocks based on islands
- Tromokratis — Must be blocked by all
- Nadir Kraken — Grows with card draw
- Charix, the Raging Isle — Huge with islands

### Sea Monster Support
- Quest for Ula's Temple — Cheat monsters into play
- Whelming Wave — Board wipe that keeps monsters
- Serpent of Yawning Depths — Unblockable for monsters
- Slinn Voda, the Rising Deep — Bounces non-monsters

### Ramp
- Thassa's Oracle — Draw-based win (also a merfolk)
- Spawning Kraken — Makes kraken tokens
- Stormtide Leviathan — Turns lands to islands

## Common Commanders

- **Arixmethes, Slumbering Isle** — Simic sea monster ramp
- **Charix, the Raging Isle** — Mono-blue defender
- **Runo Stromkirk** — Sea monster reanimation

## Synergy Partners

Krakens pair well with:
- **Big Mana** — Ramp into expensive creatures
- **Devotion** — Blue pips
- **Sea Monster** tribal — Broader theme
- **Islandwalk** — Evasion strategy
