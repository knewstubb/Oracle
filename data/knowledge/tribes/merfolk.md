# Merfolk

## Overview

Merfolk are Magic's classic blue tribe, combining lords, evasion through islandwalk, and card manipulation. Merfolk tribal in Commander excels at consistent tempo through counter distribution, unblockable damage, and steady card advantage.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Excellent lord density
- Islandwalk provides evasion
- Strong card advantage/selection
- Counter-based growth synergies
- Consistent, resilient strategies

**Weaknesses:**
- Lower individual power than other tribes
- Islandwalk is meta-dependent
- Limited removal options in blue
- Requires critical mass to function

**Typical Colors:** Blue (primary), Green (counters), White (tokens)

## Unique Mechanics

### Merfolk-Specific Themes
- **Islandwalk** — Unblockable against island controllers
- **Lord density** — Many cheap lords available
- **+1/+1 counter distribution** — Especially in Simic
- **Card selection** — Draw and manipulation effects

### Play Patterns

Merfolk tribal typically follows:
1. **Turn 1-2** — Deploy cheap merfolk, establish presence
2. **Turn 3-4** — Lords and counter distribution
3. **Turn 5+** — Overwhelming with unblockable or large creatures
4. **Win** — Islandwalk through or counter-based overrun

## Building Tips

1. **Multiple lords** — Merfolk scale with lord count; run 6-8
2. **Islandwalk enablers** — Don't rely on opponents playing islands
3. **Card advantage** — Merfolk have great draw; use it
4. **Low curve** — Most merfolk are 2-3 CMC; leverage this
5. **Counter synergy** — In Simic, prioritize +1/+1 counter effects

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No islandwalk enablers** — Opponents without islands stop your plan
2. **Too few lords** — Individual merfolk are weak without anthems
3. **Ignoring card advantage** — Merfolk have great draw options
4. **Overcommitting** — Board wipes hurt; don't dump everything
5. **No interaction** — Blue has counterspells; use them

## Synergy Partners

Merfolk pair well with:
- **+1/+1 Counters** — Simic merfolk builds
- **Draw matters** — Many merfolk trigger on draw
- **Tempo** — Counterspells + cheap creatures
- **Landwalk** — Theme extension beyond islandwalk

## Meta Considerations

- **Island players** — Islandwalk is free evasion against blue decks
- **Non-blue metas** — Need [[Spreading Seas]] effects
- **Board wipes** — Devastating; run counterspells
- **Flying** — Merfolk are mostly groundbound (but have islandwalk)
- **Fast combo** — Merfolk can apply pressure + disrupt

## Kumena Tap Synergies

[[Kumena, Tyrant of Orazca]] unlocks tap-based merfolk strategies:

### Tap Abilities
- Tap 1 merfolk: Kumena can't be blocked
- Tap 3 merfolk: Draw a card
- Tap 5 merfolk: +1/+1 counter on each merfolk

### Enablers
- [[Merrow Reejerey]] — Untap merfolk on cast
- [[Murkfiend Liege]] — Untap all creatures each upkeep
- [[Seedborn Muse]] — Untap on each player's turn
- [[Quest for Renewal]] — Untap on each player's turn

### Synergy Payoffs
- Generate card advantage repeatedly
- Grow entire board with counters
- [[Intruder Alarm]] — Untap on any creature entering
