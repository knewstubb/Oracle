# Myr

## Overview

Myr are small artifact creatures from Mirrodin, known for mana production and tribal synergies. They enable artifact-based ramp and combo strategies through their interconnected abilities.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Colorless
- **Secondary**: All colors (via mana myr)
- **Common Pairs**: Colorless, any color

## Key Mechanics

- **Mana Production**: Mana myr tap for colored mana
- **Artifact Synergy**: Core artifact creatures
- **Token Generation**: Myr Battlesphere and others
- **Combo Potential**: Infinite mana combos

## Signature Cards

### Mana Myr
- Gold Myr, Silver Myr, etc. — One for each color
- Palladium Myr — Taps for 2 colorless
- Alloy Myr — Any color

### Payoffs
- Myr Battlesphere — Creates 4 myr tokens
- Myr Galvanizer — Untaps myr (combo piece)
- Myr Turbine — Tutors or makes tokens
- Brudiclad — Turns all tokens into copies

### Support
- Myr Superion — 5/6 for 2 (creature mana only)
- Myr Retriever — Return artifact from grave
- Myr Matrix — Makes myr indestructible

## Common Commanders

- **Urtet, Remnant of Memnarch** — Dedicated myr commander
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — Token transformation
- **Ich-Tekik, Salvage Splicer** — Artifact creature synergy

## Synergy Partners

Myr pair well with:
- **Artifacts-matter** — Core identity
- **Combo** — Myr Galvanizer infinite mana
- **Colorless** — Easy to splash
- **Tokens** — Battlesphere strategy

## Combo: Infinite Mana

2x Myr Galvanizer + 3 mana myr = infinite mana (untap all myr, tap for mana, repeat).
