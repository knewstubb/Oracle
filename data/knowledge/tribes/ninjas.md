# Ninjas

## Overview

Ninjas are a Dimir tribe built around the Ninjutsu mechanic—swapping unblocked attackers for ninjas from your hand. They reward evasive setups and combat damage triggers.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Blue, Black (Dimir)
**Secondary:** Mono-blue exists but loses key cards

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Deploy cheap evasive creatures, then swap them for ninjas using Ninjutsu. Repeat saboteur triggers by bouncing ninjas and re-deploying.

## Essential Cards

### Key Ninjas
- [[Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow]] — Commander Ninjutsu, damage + draw
- [[Ingenious Infiltrator]] — Draw on ninja damage
- [[Fallen Shinobi]] — Exile and cast opponent's cards
- [[Silent-Blade Oni]] — Steal from opponent's hand
- [[Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni]] — Reanimate from opponent's graveyard

### Enablers (Evasive 1-drops)
- [[Changeling Outcast]] — Unblockable changeling
- [[Ornithopter]] — Free flyer
- [[Slither Blade]] — Unblockable
- [[Triton Shorestalker]] — Unblockable
- [[Faerie Seer]] — Scry + flying

### Support
- [[Sakashima's Student]] — Clone as ninja
- [[Moon-Circuit Hacker]] — Draw on first damage
- [[Prosperous Thief]] — Treasure on ninja damage
- [[Cover of Darkness]] — Fear for creature type

## Common Commanders

- **[[Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow]]** — Bypasses commander tax
- **[[Satoru Umezawa]]** — Give ninjutsu to all creatures
- **[[Higure, the Still Wind]]** — Tutor ninjas on damage

## Building Tips

1. **Run 12+ cheap evasive creatures** — Need enablers
2. **Include ways to bounce ninjas** — Re-use ninjutsu
3. **Stack top of library** — Yuriko triggers want high CMC
4. **Consider changeling support** — Count as ninjas
