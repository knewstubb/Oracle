# Pirates

## Overview

Pirates are a Grixis tribe focused on treasure generation, theft effects, and aggressive combat. Ixalan blocks provided the tribal foundation.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Blue, Black, Red (Grixis)
**Secondary:** Dimir or Rakdos variants

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Generate treasure through combat damage triggers, steal opponents' resources, and leverage artifact synergies with treasure tokens.

## Essential Cards

### Treasure Generators
- [[Captain Lannery Storm]] — Treasure on attack
- [[Dockside Extortionist]] — Mass treasure (honorary pirate)
- [[Professional Face-Breaker]] — Treasure + impulse draw
- [[Pitiless Plunderer]] — Treasure on creature death
- [[Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator]] — Treasure on pirate damage

### Tribal Payoffs
- [[Admiral Beckett Brass]] — Steal permanents with 3 pirates
- [[Breeches, Brazen Plunderer]] — Exile and cast on damage
- [[Coastline Marauders]] — Encore, scales with artifacts
- [[Dire Fleet Ravager]] — Mass life loss

### Support
- [[Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer]] — Treasure + theft
- [[Port Razer]] — Extra combats
- [[Merchant Raiders]] — Flash, creates treasure
- [[Storm Fleet Sprinter]] — Haste, can't be blocked

## Common Commanders

- **[[Admiral Beckett Brass]]** — Steal with 3 pirates
- **[[Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator]] + [[Breeches, Brazen Plunderer]]** — Partner treasure/theft
- **[[Marchesa, the Black Rose]]** — Recursive pirates

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize evasion** — Pirates need to connect
2. **Build treasure synergies** — Natural artifact subtheme
3. **Include theft effects** — On-theme and powerful
4. **Run haste enablers** — Aggressive tribe
