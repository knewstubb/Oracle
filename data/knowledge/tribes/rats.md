# Rats

## Overview

Rats are an underrated mono-black tribe with a unique mechanic: two rats ([[Rat Colony]] and [[Relentless Rats]]) allow any number of copies, breaking the singleton rule.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Black (mono-black)
**Secondary:** Occasionally Dimir with [[Karumonix, the Rat King]]

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Swarm the board with rats that grow stronger together. The "any number" rats create consistent, redundant threats that scale with quantity.

**Key Elements:**
- [[Rat Colony]] / [[Relentless Rats]] density
- Mass token generation
- Pump effects and anthems
- Sacrifice synergies
- Card draw to maintain swarm

## Essential Cards

### Any-Number Rats
- [[Rat Colony]] — +1/+0 per rat, any number
- [[Relentless Rats]] — +1/+1 per rat, any number

### Rat Lords/Payoffs
- [[Marrow-Gnawer]] — Sac rat, make rats
- [[Ogre Slumlord]] — Tokens on creature death
- [[Piper of the Swarm]] — Tapped rats make more rats
- [[Karumonix, the Rat King]] — Toxic, impulse draw

### Support
- [[Thrumming Stone]] — Ripple 4 for rat chains
- [[Secret Salvage]] — Tutor all copies of a card
- [[Pack Rat]] — Discard to make rats
- [[Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni]] — Ninjutsu, reanimation

## Common Commanders

- **[[Marrow-Gnawer]]** — Classic rat commander
- **[[Karumonix, the Rat King]]** — Toxic rats
- **[[Ashcoat of the Shadow Swarm]]** — Mill/recursion

## Building Tips

1. **Run 25-35 Rat Colony** — Consistency is the point
2. **Include [[Thrumming Stone]]** — Chain into many rats
3. **Add anthems** — Mass pump for the swarm
4. **Pack removal** — Mono-black has options
