# Rogues

## Overview

Rogues are a Dimir tribe focused on evasion, mill, and stealing from opponents. They became highly competitive with Zendikar Rising's support cards.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Blue, Black (Dimir)
**Secondary:** Mono-black or Grixis variants

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Deploy evasive rogues that mill opponents, then benefit from cards in opponents' graveyards. Control the game while chipping in damage.

## Essential Cards

### Mill Rogues
- [[Thieves' Guild Enforcer]] — Flash, mills on rogue ETB
- [[Soaring Thought-Thief]] — Flash, lord, mills on attack
- [[Zareth San, the Trickster]] — Steals from graveyards
- [[Oona's Blackguard]] — Discard on damage

### Graveyard Payoffs
- [[Dauthi Voidwalker]] — Exiles graveyards, casts for free
- [[Nighthawk Scavenger]] — Power scales with graveyards
- [[Consuming Aberration]] — Massive with mill

### Support
- [[Bitterblossom]] — Faerie Rogue tokens
- [[Notorious Throng]] — Extra turn with prowl
- [[Knowledge Exploitation]] — Cast opponent's instant/sorcery
- [[Moritte of the Frost]] — Changeling clone

## Common Commanders

- **[[Anowon, the Ruin Thief]]** — Mill + draw on rogue damage
- **[[Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow]]** — Ninja/rogue overlap
- **[[Zareth San, the Trickster]]** — Steal from graveyards

## Building Tips

1. **Prioritize evasion** — Rogues need to connect
2. **Include mill payoffs** — Not just incidental mill
3. **Run flash creatures** — Control-friendly tribe
4. **Consider ninja overlap** — Many ninjas are rogues
