# Saprolings

## Overview

Saprolings are green-black fungal tokens that accumulate over time. Unlike most token strategies that create tokens all at once, saproling decks generate tokens incrementally through spore counters and fungus creatures, then sacrifice them for value.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Black, White
- **Common Pairs**: Golgari (BG), Selesnya (GW), Abzan (WBG)

## Key Mechanics

- **Spore Counters**: Fungus creatures accumulate counters, remove to make saprolings
- **Token Generation**: Steady stream of 1/1 tokens
- **Sacrifice Synergy**: Tokens fuel aristocrats effects
- **Fungus Tribal**: Underlying creature type

## Signature Cards

### Token Generators
- Sporemound — Saproling on landfall
- Tendershoot Dryad — Saproling each upkeep, lord effect
- Mycoloth — Devour, makes saprolings each upkeep
- Verdant Force — Saproling each upkeep

### Payoffs
- Slimefoot, the Stowaway — Drain on saproling death
- Sporecrown Thallid — +1/+1 to saprolings
- Life and Limb — Saprolings are forests (combo piece)

### Fungus Support
- Sporesower Thallid — Spore counter acceleration
- Utopia Mycon — Sacrifice saproling for mana
- Pallid Mycoderm — Sacrifice for +1/+1

## Common Commanders

- **Slimefoot, the Stowaway** — Aristocrats saproling drain
- **Ghave, Guru of Spores** — Abzan counter/token combo
- **Thelon of Havenwood** — Fungus/spore counter synergy

## Synergy Partners

Saprolings pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice fodder
- **Tokens** — Token doublers
- **Counters** — Spore counter manipulation
- **Landfall** — Sporemound synergy

## Combo Potential

Life and Limb + Sporemound creates infinite saprolings (each saproling is a forest, triggering another saproling). Add a drain effect to win.
