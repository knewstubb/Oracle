# Shamans

## Overview

Shamans are a creature type spread across all colors, representing spiritual leaders and elemental channelers. In Commander, they're most notable in Gruul colors where they synergize with lands, creatures, and primal magic.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green, Red
- **Secondary**: Black, Blue
- **Common Pairs**: Gruul (RG), Jund (BRG), Temur (GUR)

## Key Mechanics

- **Mana Production**: Many shamans are mana dorks
- **Elemental Synergy**: Overlap with elementals
- **Land Matters**: Landfall and land sacrifice
- **Activated Abilities**: Tap abilities are common

## Signature Cards

### Mana Shamans
- Elvish Archdruid — Counts elves (many are shamans)
- Mul Daya Channelers — Conditional 5/5 that taps for 2
- Shaman of Forgotten Ways — Formidable win condition

### Lords and Payoffs
- Sachi, Daughter of Seshiro — Shamans tap for GG
- Rage Forger — Counters and damage
- Wolf-Skull Shaman — Kinship token generation

### Notable Shamans
- Oracle of Mul Daya — Extra land drops, play from top
- Eternal Witness — Recursion
- Reclamation Sage — Artifact/enchantment removal

## Common Commanders

- **Sek'Kuar, Deathkeeper** — Jund tokens on nontoken death
- **Radha, Heir to Keld** — Mana on attack
- **Wort, the Raidmother** — Conspire spellslinger

## Synergy Partners

Shamans pair well with:
- **Elves** — Significant type overlap
- **Elementals** — Thematic connection
- **Lands Matter** — Many shamans care about lands
- **Tokens** — Several make tokens

## Notes

Shaman tribal lacks dedicated lords compared to other tribes. Most shaman decks use the type incidentally rather than building around it specifically.
