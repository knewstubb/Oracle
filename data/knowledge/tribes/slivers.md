# Slivers

## Overview

Slivers are Magic's most synergistic tribe — each Sliver shares its abilities with all other Slivers, creating exponentially scaling boards. Sliver tribal in Commander builds critical mass where every creature becomes a multi-keyword threat.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Exponential synergy scaling
- Every creature improves every other creature
- Multiple keyword combinations create threats
- Strong tutoring support

**Weaknesses:**
- Telegraphed and draws hate
- 5-color mana requirements
- Very dependent on board state
- Vulnerable to board wipes

**Typical Colors:** WUBRG (5-color is standard), though mono/two-color builds exist

## Unique Mechanics

### Ability Sharing
The defining sliver mechanic:
- All slivers you control gain the abilities of all other slivers
- Keywords stack multiplicatively
- One flying sliver + one hexproof sliver = all slivers have flying and hexproof
- Lords affect all slivers, including opponents' (in most cases)

### Play Patterns

Sliver tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Fix mana, deploy utility slivers
2. **Mid game** — Establish critical slivers (lords, keywords)
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming board with keyword soup creatures
4. **Win** — Alpha strike or combo finish

## Building Tips

1. **Mana base priority** — 5-color needs excellent fixing
2. **Include haste** — [[Heart Sliver]], [[Blur Sliver]] enable immediate attacks
3. **Protection slivers** — [[Sliver Hivelord]], [[Crystalline Sliver]] prevent blowouts
4. **Balance keyword and lord density** — Need both to function
5. **Tutors** — [[Sliver Overlord]] or cards like [[Worldly Tutor]]

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Poor mana base** — 5-color demands investment in lands
2. **No protection** — Board wipes destroy sliver armies
3. **Overcommitting** — Don't dump hand when wrath is likely
4. **Helping opponents** — Some slivers affect ALL slivers; watch for opponent changelings
5. **No removal** — Need interaction; can't just race everything

## Synergy Partners

Slivers pair well with:
- **Changelings** — Gain sliver abilities, expand synergies
- **Blink** — Re-trigger ETB slivers like [[Harmonic Sliver]]
- **Token strategies** — [[Sliver Queen]] makes tokens
- **Combo** — Multiple infinite combo lines

## Meta Considerations

- **Board wipes** — Slivers are very vulnerable
- **Sliver hate** — [[Plague Sliver]] (silver bullet)
- **Color hate** — [[Blood Moon]] destroys 5-color mana
- **Creature removal** — Key slivers draw removal
- **Political attention** — Slivers are scary; expect focus fire

## Sliver Combos

### Sliver Queen + Mana Sliver + Basal Sliver
1. Have [[Sliver Queen]], [[Gemhide Sliver]], [[Basal Sliver]]
2. Create token, tap for mana, sacrifice for 2 mana
3. Net 1 mana per cycle = infinite mana and tokens

### Add a Win Condition
- [[Lavabelly Sliver]] — Infinite ETB damage
- [[Psionic Sliver]] — Infinite direct damage
- [[Blood Artist]] effect — Infinite drain

### The First Sliver Cascade Chain
With low-curve slivers and [[The First Sliver]]:
1. Cast a sliver, cascade
2. Hit another sliver, cascade again
3. Chain through deck, potentially finding combo pieces
