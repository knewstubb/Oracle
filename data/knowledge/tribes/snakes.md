# Snakes

## Overview

Snakes are a green-black-blue tribe with deathtouch synergies and counter manipulation. Kamigawa's snake shamans provide the backbone for dedicated snake tribal builds.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Black, Blue
- **Common Pairs**: Simic (UG), Golgari (BG), Sultai (UBG)

## Key Mechanics

- **Deathtouch**: Common keyword
- **Counter Manipulation**: Kamigawa snakes move counters
- **Ramp**: Several snakes produce mana
- **Ninjutsu Support**: Snake ninjas exist

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Seshiro the Anointed — Lord and card draw
- Sosuke, Son of Seshiro — Deathtouch for snakes
- Kaseto, Orochi Archmage — Unblockable and pump

### Counter Synergy
- Patagia Viper — Flying snake with counters
- Orochi Hatchery — X snakes each turn
- Ohran Frostfang — Deathtouch and draw

### Mana Snakes
- Sakura-Tribe Elder — Classic ramp
- Coiling Oracle — Draw/ramp
- Lotus Cobra — Landfall mana

## Common Commanders

- **Kaseto, Orochi Archmage** — Simic snake tribal
- **Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons** — -1/-1 counter snakes
- **Xyris, the Writhing Storm** — Wheel snakes

## Synergy Partners

Snakes pair well with:
- **Counters** — Both +1/+1 and -1/-1
- **Deathtouch** — Defensive and fight
- **Landfall** — Lotus Cobra
- **Wheels** — Xyris build
