# Soldiers

## Overview

Soldiers are a primarily white creature type focused on combat efficiency, team synergy, and military discipline. They excel at going wide with token generation while buffing each other through lord effects and formation-based abilities.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: White
- **Secondary**: Red, Blue
- **Common Pairs**: Boros (WR), Azorius (WU), Mardu (WBR)

## Key Mechanics

- **Token Generation**: Mass soldier token production
- **Anthem Effects**: Lords that pump all soldiers
- **First Strike/Vigilance**: Combat keyword density
- **Battalion**: Rewards for attacking with 3+ creatures

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Anthems
- Field Marshal — First strike to all soldiers
- Daru Warchief — Cost reduction and +1/+2
- Captain of the Watch — Creates tokens and buffs
- Preeminent Captain — Cheats soldiers into play attacking

### Token Generators
- Decree of Justice — Scalable soldier tokens
- Increasing Devotion — 5 tokens, flashback for 10
- Martial Coup — Board wipe that leaves soldiers
- Deploy to the Front — Massive token generation

### Payoffs
- Catapult Master — Tap 5 soldiers to exile
- Mobilization — Makes soldiers, gives vigilance
- Militia's Pride — Token on attack

## Common Commanders

- **Darien, King of Kjeldor** — Damage to you makes soldiers
- **Myrel, Shield of Argive** — No opponent plays during your turn, makes soldiers
- **Jirina Kudro** — Humans/soldiers matter, Mardu colors

## Synergy Partners

Soldiers pair well with:
- **Tokens** — Go-wide strategies
- **Equipment** — Combat enhancement
- **Humans** — Significant type overlap
- **Aggro** — Fast combat damage

## Budget Considerations

Soldier tribal is relatively budget-friendly as most lords and token makers are affordable commons/uncommons from various sets.
