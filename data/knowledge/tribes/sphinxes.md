# Sphinxes

## Overview

Sphinxes are blue's iconic flying creatures representing wisdom and knowledge. They typically have high stats, flying, and abilities related to card draw, scrying, or library manipulation.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Blue
- **Secondary**: White
- **Common Pairs**: Azorius (WU), Esper (WUB)

## Key Mechanics

- **Flying**: Universal evasion
- **Card Advantage**: Draw, scry, or reveal
- **High CMC**: Typically 4-6 mana
- **Riddles/Choices**: Opponent choice effects

## Signature Cards

### Card Advantage
- Consecrated Sphinx — Draw on opponent draws
- Sphinx's Revelation — Lifegain and draw
- Sphinx of the Steel Wind — Protection suite
- Sphinx of Uthuun — Fact or Fiction on ETB

### Lords
- Unesh, Criosphinx Sovereign — Cost reduction, FoF trigger
- Isperia, Supreme Judge — Draw on attacks

### Others
- Sphinx Ambassador — Tutor on combat damage
- Sphinx of the Second Sun — Extra beginning phase
- Sphinx of Lost Truths — Kicker draw/discard

## Common Commanders

- **Unesh, Criosphinx Sovereign** — Sphinx tribal payoffs
- **Isperia the Inscrutable** — Tutor on combat
- **Yennett, Cryptic Sovereign** — Odd CMC matters

## Synergy Partners

Sphinxes pair well with:
- **Control** — High-end finishers
- **Blink** — ETB abilities
- **Big Mana** — Expensive creatures
- **Flying** — Evasion theme

## Budget Notes

Consecrated Sphinx is expensive but not required. Unesh provides value without it.
