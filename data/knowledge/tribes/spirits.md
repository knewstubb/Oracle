# Spirits

## Overview

Spirits are Magic's premier flying tribal deck with a strong tempo identity, combining evasive creatures with flash and tribal synergies. Spirit tribal in Commander excels at controlling the pace of the game while building incremental advantages through ETB effects and sacrifice synergies.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Inherent flying on most creatures
- Many spirits have flash
- Strong ETB and death triggers
- Excellent tempo and disruption
- Sacrifice synergies (Arcane/Soulshift)

**Weaknesses:**
- Relatively low power/toughness
- Requires specific lords for power
- Some synergies are Kamigawa-specific
- Less raw power than other tribes

**Typical Colors:** White (primary), Blue (control), Black (death triggers)

## Unique Mechanics

### Spirit-Specific Themes
- **Soulshift** — Return smaller spirits from graveyard
- **Arcane synergy** — Splice onto Arcane (Kamigawa)
- **Flash** — Many spirits can be cast at instant speed
- **ETB effects** — Spirits often have strong enter-the-battlefield abilities

### Play Patterns

Spirit tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Deploy value spirits, hold up mana
2. **Mid game** — Flash in responses, build board
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming air force with lords
4. **Win** — Evasive damage or combo finish

## Building Tips

1. **Maximize flash** — [[Rattlechains]] enables instant-speed spirits
2. **Protect with hexproof** — [[Drogskol Captain]] is essential
3. **Include sacrifice synergies** — [[Selfless Spirit]], [[Karmic Guide]]
4. **ETB exploitation** — Blink effects maximize value
5. **Don't overload lords** — Need spirits to boost

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too few spirits** — Lords without bodies do nothing
2. **Ignoring flash** — Spirits' strength is tempo
3. **No recursion** — Spirits have great death/recursion synergies
4. **Missing removal** — Need answers beyond just creatures
5. **Overcommitting** — Board wipes hurt; keep threats in hand

## Synergy Partners

Spirits pair well with:
- **Blink** — ETB spirits are great blink targets
- **Aristocrats** — Death trigger spirits
- **Enchantress** — Many spirits care about enchantments
- **Control** — Flash spirits fit control shell

## Meta Considerations

- **Flying hate** — Spirits are almost all flyers
- **Board wipes** — Can be devastating; run protection
- **Targeted removal** — [[Drogskol Captain]] prevents this
- **Fast combo** — Spirits can apply pressure + disrupt
- **Creature removal** — Flash lets you respond

## Kamigawa Spirit Builds

Kamigawa spirits have unique mechanics:

### Soulshift
When a spirit with Soulshift dies, return a spirit with lower CMC:
- [[Kodama of the Center Tree]] — Big soulshift
- [[Karmic Guide]] — Pairs with soulshift chains
- Chain recursion through deaths

### Arcane Synergy
Splice onto Arcane allows copying effects:
- [[Evermind]] — Draw + splice target
- [[Kodama's Reach]] — Ramp arcane spell
- [[Tallowisp]] — Tutor on arcane cast

### Play Pattern
Use soulshift to create recursion loops, keeping spirits flowing even through removal.
