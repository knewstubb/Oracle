# Squirrels

## Overview

Squirrels are Magic's most beloved joke tribe turned legitimate strategy. What started as silver-bordered fun became a real tribe with Chatterfang's printing. Squirrel tribal is a token-based aristocrats strategy that generates absurd numbers of creatures.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Black (Golgari)
- **Common Pairs**: Golgari (BG), Mono-green

## Key Mechanics

- **Token Generation**: Create many small tokens
- **Token Doubling**: Chatterfang doubles all tokens as squirrels
- **Sacrifice Synergy**: Small tokens fuel aristocrats
- **Go-Wide**: Overwhelm with numbers

## Signature Cards

### Token Generators
- Squirrel Nest — Repeatable squirrel production
- Chatterstorm — Storm count squirrels
- Scurry Oak — Infinite with counters
- Chitterspitter — Sacrifice food for squirrels

### Payoffs
- Squirrel Mob — Gets huge with squirrel count
- Nut Collector — Threshold for mass tokens
- Ravenous Squirrel — Grows on sacrifice

### Combo Pieces
- Ivy Lane Denizen — Infinite with Scurry Oak
- Pitiless Plunderer — Treasure on death

## Common Commanders

- **Chatterfang, Squirrel General** — The definitive squirrel commander, doubles tokens and has a built-in sacrifice outlet
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** — Indestructible card draw engine

## Synergy Partners

Squirrels pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Sacrifice fodder
- **Tokens** — Token doubling effects
- **Counters** — Scurry Oak combo
- **Food** — Flavor and mechanical synergy

## Power Level Notes

Chatterfang is surprisingly powerful — the token doubling applies to ALL token creation, not just creatures. Treasure, Food, Clue tokens all make squirrels too.
