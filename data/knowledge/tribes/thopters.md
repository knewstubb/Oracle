# Thopters

## Overview

Thopters are artifact creature tokens with flying, typically 1/1s. While not a traditional tribe, thopter strategies focus on generating many flying artifact tokens and benefiting from artifact/token synergies.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Blue
- **Secondary**: White, Red (Jeskai)
- **Common Pairs**: Izzet (UR), Esper (WUB), Jeskai (WUR)

## Key Mechanics

- **Flying Tokens**: Evasive attackers
- **Artifact Tokens**: Artifact count matters
- **Sacrifice Fodder**: Feed to artifact costs
- **Go-Wide**: Swarm strategies

## Signature Cards

### Token Generators
- Thopter Assembly — 5 thopters if alone
- Whirler Rogue — 2 thopters and unblockable
- Efficient Construction — Thopter on artifact cast
- Thopter Spy Network — Thopters and card draw

### Payoffs
- Sharding Sphinx — Thopters make more thopters
- Thopter Engineer — Haste for artifact creatures
- Ornithopter of Paradise — Mana dork thopter
- Chief of the Foundry — Artifact creature anthem

### Combos
- Thopter Foundry + Sword of the Meek — Infinite tokens with mana
- Time Sieve + Thopter Assembly — Infinite turns

## Common Commanders

- **Breya, Etherium Shaper** — Artifact tokens matter
- **Alela, Artful Provocateur** — Faeries and thopters
- **Saheeli, the Gifted** — Artifact cost reduction

## Synergy Partners

Thopters pair well with:
- **Artifacts-matter** — Core synergy
- **Tokens** — Doubling effects
- **Affinity** — Artifact count
- **Improvise** — Tap artifacts for mana
