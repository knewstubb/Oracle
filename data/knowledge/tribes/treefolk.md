# Treefolk

## Overview

Treefolk are green's ancient forest creatures — massive, durable, and patient. They typically have high toughness, and many treefolk strategies involve turning that toughness into damage through effects like assault formation.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: White, Black
- **Common Pairs**: Mono-green, Selesnya (GW), Abzan (WBG)

## Key Mechanics

- **High Toughness**: Often toughness > power
- **Toughness Matters**: Deal damage equal to toughness
- **Defender/Reach**: Some start as walls
- **Forest Synergy**: Many care about forests

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Verdeloth the Ancient — Lord and token maker
- Timber Protector — Indestructibility for treefolk and forests
- Bosk Banneret — Cost reduction
- Orchard Warden — Lifegain on treefolk ETB

### Toughness Matters
- Doran, the Siege Tower — Creatures deal toughness damage
- Assault Formation — Same effect, plus pump
- Kin-Tree Invocation — Token with P/T = highest toughness

### Notable Treefolk
- Treefolk Harbinger — Tutor for treefolk or forest
- Unstoppable Ash — Champion, pump on attack
- Wickerbough Elder — Artifact/enchantment removal

## Common Commanders

- **Doran, the Siege Tower** — Toughness matters tribal
- **Colfenor, the Last Yew** — Toughness-based card advantage
- **Verdeloth the Ancient** — Treefolk and saproling tokens

## Synergy Partners

Treefolk pair well with:
- **Toughness Matters** — Doran builds
- **Defenders** — High toughness overlap
- **Forests** — Land synergies
- **Saprolings** — Verdeloth connection

## Build Notes

Most treefolk decks are actually "toughness matters" decks that happen to include treefolk. Doran enables defenders and high-toughness creatures of any type.
