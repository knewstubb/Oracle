# Vampires

## Overview

Vampires are Magic's premier aggressive aristocrats tribe, combining life manipulation, sacrifice synergies, and incremental growth. Vampire tribal in Commander often blends go-wide token strategies with drain effects and +1/+1 counter accumulation.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Strong sacrifice and lifedrain synergy
- Many vampires have lifelink
- Excellent token generation with Edgar Markov
- Multiple viable build paths (aggro, aristocrats, madness)

**Weaknesses:**
- Relies on commander for token generation
- Few evasive creatures
- Dependent on life total for some effects
- Can be mana-intensive

**Typical Colors:** Black (primary), Red (aggressive), White (tokens/lifegain)

## Unique Mechanics

### Vampire-Specific Themes
- **Blood tokens** — Looting resources ([[Voldaren Bloodcaster]], [[Bloodtithe Harvester]])
- **Eminence** — [[Edgar Markov]]'s token generation from command zone
- **+1/+1 counter growth** — Many vampires grow when dealing damage
- **Madness** — Discard synergy vampires ([[Anje Falkenrath]])

### Life Manipulation
Vampires interact with life totals uniquely:
- Lifelink is common
- Drain effects hit all opponents
- Some grow based on life gained/lost
- Life payment costs are thematic

### Play Patterns

Vampire tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Deploy cheap vampires, trigger Edgar tokens
2. **Mid game** — Establish drain effects, grow with counters
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming board with drain effects for lethal

## Key Synergies

### Edgar Markov Synergy
- Every vampire cast creates a 1/1 token
- Eminence works from command zone
- Enables aristocrats, go-wide, and +1/+1 counter strategies
- Best with low-CMC vampires for maximum triggers

### Lords and Anthems
- [[Captivating Vampire]] — Lord + steal creatures
- [[Legion Lieutenant]] — +1/+1 to vampires
- [[Bloodline Keeper]] — Lord + token generation
- [[Stromkirk Captain]] — First strike lord
- [[Vampire Nocturnus]] — Conditional +2/+1 and flying

### Drain Effects
- [[Blood Artist]] — Drain on any creature death
- [[Sanctum Seeker]] — Drain on each attacking vampire
- [[Drana's Emissary]] — Drain each upkeep
- [[Malakir Bloodwitch]] — ETB drain based on vampires
- [[Exquisite Blood]] — Combo piece + ongoing drain

## Building Tips

1. **Low curve with Edgar** — Maximize eminence triggers with 1-2 CMC vampires
2. **Include sacrifice outlets** — Vampires + aristocrats is natural synergy
3. **Protect your board** — [[Teferi's Protection]], [[Boros Charm]], [[Rootborn Defenses]]
4. **Don't skip drain effects** — Sanctum Seeker can close games quickly
5. **Balance lords and vampires** — Too many lords with no army does nothing

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too many high-CMC vampires** — Misses Edgar value; keep curve low
2. **No sacrifice outlets** — Can't convert creatures to value
3. **Overextending** — Board wipes hurt go-wide strategies
4. **Ignoring life total** — Some effects require high life; don't neglect lifegain
5. **Cutting non-vampires** — Critical support cards ([[Blood Artist]]) matter

## Synergy Partners

Vampires pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Natural overlap with drain/sacrifice
- **+1/+1 Counters** — Many vampires grow with counters
- **Lifegain** — Vampires have built-in lifegain
- **Madness** — [[Anje]] builds specifically

## Meta Considerations

- **Flying** — Most vampires are groundbound; can be flown over
- **Lifegain hate** — [[Erebos]], [[Sulfuric Vortex]] shut down drain strategies
- **Board wipes** — Devastating to go-wide vampire strategies
- **Graveyard hate** — Less impactful unless running aristocrats heavily
- **Creature removal** — Targeted removal is less effective against swarm

## Edgar Markov Builds

Edgar enables several build paths:

### Aristocrats
Focus on drain effects and sacrifice:
- [[Blood Artist]], [[Zulaport Cutthroat]], [[Cruel Celebrant]]
- [[Viscera Seer]], [[Yahenni]], [[Indulgent Aristocrat]]
- Low curve for maximum tokens to sacrifice

### Aggro/Go-Wide
Focus on overwhelming with attackers:
- [[Sanctum Seeker]], [[Shared Animosity]], [[Coat of Arms]]
- Lords: [[Captivating Vampire]], [[Legion Lieutenant]]
- Very low curve, maximum token generation

### +1/+1 Counters
Focus on growing vampires:
- [[Drana, Liberator of Malakir]], [[Cordial Vampire]]
- [[Cathars' Crusade]], [[Feast of Sanity]]
- Balance cheap vampires with counter synergies
