# Warriors

## Overview

Warriors are an aggressive creature type spread across all colors but concentrated in Mardu (WBR). They emphasize attacking, combat tricks, and overwhelming opponents through relentless aggression. Warriors care about the attack step more than any other tribe.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Red, White, Black
- **Secondary**: Green
- **Common Pairs**: Mardu (WBR), Gruul (RG), Rakdos (BR)

## Key Mechanics

- **Attack Triggers**: Rewards for declaring attackers
- **Raid**: Bonus if you attacked this turn
- **Combat Keywords**: First strike, menace, haste
- **Equipment Synergy**: Many warriors care about being equipped

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Enablers
- Boldwyr Intimidator — Makes creatures cowards or warriors
- Lovisa Coldeyes — Haste and +2/+2 to warriors/berserkers/barbarians
- Mindblade Render — Draw on warrior damage
- Blood-Chin Fanatic — Sacrifice warrior for damage/life

### Payoffs
- Raiders' Spoils — Draw when warriors deal damage
- Shared Animosity — Massive pump on attack
- Rushblade Commander — All warriors have haste

### Combat Tricks
- Rush of Battle — Lifelink and +2/+1 to attackers
- Coordinated Assault — First strike and +1/+0

## Common Commanders

- **Najeela, the Blade-Blossom** — 5-color warrior combo, infinite combats
- **Lovisa Coldeyes** — Mono-red warrior aggro
- **Alesha, Who Smiles at Death** — Mardu warrior reanimation

## Synergy Partners

Warriors pair well with:
- **Aggro** — Core aggressive strategy
- **Equipment** — Combat enhancement
- **Aristocrats** — Many black warriors
- **Tokens** — Najeela creates warrior tokens

## Power Level Notes

Najeela is a cEDH-viable commander due to infinite combat potential. Most other warrior builds are casual aggro.
