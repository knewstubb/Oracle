# Werewolves

## Overview

Werewolves are transforming creatures from Innistrad. They flip between weaker human forms and powerful wolf forms based on spells cast. Midnight Hunt introduced daybound/nightbound for more reliable transformation.

## Color Identity

**Primary:** Red, Green (Gruul)
**Secondary:** Pure green options exist

## Core Strategy

**Philosophy:** Control the day/night cycle to keep werewolves transformed. Attack aggressively when in wolf form, and use the threat of transformation to discourage opponents from casting multiple spells.

**Key Elements:**
- Day/night manipulation
- Transformation payoffs
- Wolf tribal overlap
- Combat tricks
- Cards that benefit from not casting spells

## Essential Cards

### Key Werewolves
- [[Huntmaster of the Fells]] — Value on each transform
- [[Mayor of Avabruck]] — Lord, generates wolves
- [[Arlinn, the Pack's Hope]] — Planeswalker support
- [[Tovolar, Dire Overlord]] — Reliable transformation
- [[Hollowhenge Overlord]] — Flash, wolf tokens

### Day/Night Control
- [[Tovolar, Dire Overlord]] — Forces night at 3+ wolves
- [[Unnatural Moonrise]] — Sets to night, draws
- [[The Celestus]] — Manual day/night toggle
- [[Wandering Mind]] — Flash to skip spells

### Wolf Overlap
- [[Howlpack Piper]] — Cheats creatures in
- [[Nightpack Ambusher]] — Flash, wolf tokens
- [[Master of the Wild Hunt]] — Wolf token + removal
- [[Silverfur Partisan]] — Wolf tokens on targeting

## Common Commanders

- **[[Tovolar, Dire Overlord]]** — Premier werewolf commander
- **[[Ulrich of the Krallenhorde]]** — Fight on transform
- **[[Ruric Thar, the Unbowed]]** — Punishes noncreature spells

## Building Tips

1. **Run [[Tovolar]] — Makes werewolves consistently viable
2. **Include flash creatures** — Skip your spells, still develop board
3. **Overlap with wolves** — Many synergies work for both
4. **Combat tricks** — Attack-focused tribe
