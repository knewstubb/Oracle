# Wizards

## Overview

Wizards are Magic's spellcaster tribe, combining creature bodies with spell synergies. Wizard tribal in Commander leverages instants and sorceries alongside creature-based strategies, often featuring cost reduction, spell copying, and card advantage through cast triggers.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Natural synergy with instant/sorcery strategies
- Excellent card advantage
- Many cost reduction options
- Strong ETB abilities
- Overlaps with spellslinger

**Weaknesses:**
- Generally fragile bodies
- Relies on keeping wizards alive
- Spell-heavy metas can outpace creature builds
- Some synergies require critical mass

**Typical Colors:** Blue (primary), Red (spellslinger), Black (reanimation)

## Unique Mechanics

### Wizard-Specific Themes
- **Spell copying** — Wizards often copy instants/sorceries
- **Cost reduction** — Cheaper spells with wizards in play
- **Draw triggers** — Many wizards draw on spell cast
- **Creature-spell hybrid** — Bridge creature and spell strategies

### Play Patterns

Wizard tribal typically follows:
1. **Early game** — Deploy value wizards, establish draw
2. **Mid game** — Chain spells with cost reduction/copying
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming value or combo finish
4. **Win** — Spell payoffs or creature combat

## Building Tips

1. **Balance creatures and spells** — Too many of either weakens the deck
2. **Include tap synergies** — [[Azami]] wants untap effects
3. **Protect key wizards** — [[Riptide Laboratory]] is excellent
4. **Draw engines** — Wizards excel at card advantage
5. **Instant speed** — Hold up mana, respond with wizards

## Common Pitfalls

1. **All spells, no creatures** — Wizard synergies need wizards
2. **All creatures, no spells** — Spellslinger synergies need spells
3. **Missing protection** — Key wizards get removed
4. **No finisher** — Card advantage without a closer
5. **Overcommitting** — Board wipes hurt; protect or rebuild

## Synergy Partners

Wizards pair well with:
- **Spellslinger** — Natural synergy
- **Blink** — ETB wizards are excellent targets
- **Copy effects** — [[Dualcaster Mage]] combos
- **Artifacts** — Many wizards care about artifacts

## Meta Considerations

- **Creature removal** — Key wizards are targets
- **Counterspells** — Wizard decks often have counterspell access
- **Board wipes** — Devastating; need protection/recursion
- **Fast aggro** — Wizards can struggle with early aggression
- **Stax** — Tax effects hurt spell-heavy strategies

## Inalla Builds

[[Inalla, Archmage Ritualist]] enables powerful wizard strategies:

### Eminence Copies
Pay 1 mana when a wizard ETBs to create a haste copy:
- Token has haste and is sacrificed at end step
- Double ETB effects like [[Naru Meha]]
- Creates combo potential

### Key Combos
- **Wanderwine Prophets** — Infinite turns with eminence
- **Dualcaster Mage + Ghostly Flicker** — Infinite ETBs
- **Bloodline Necromancer** — Self-reanimation loop

### Play Pattern
1. Deploy wizards with powerful ETBs
2. Pay eminence cost for token copies
3. Build toward combo or value overwhelming
