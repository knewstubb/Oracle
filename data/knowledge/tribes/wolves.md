# Wolves

## Overview

Wolves are a green creature type focused on pack tactics and combat. They synergize with werewolves and often appear in Gruul or Selesnya builds emphasizing creature combat.

## Color Identity

- **Primary**: Green
- **Secondary**: Red, White
- **Common Pairs**: Gruul (RG), Selesnya (GW), Naya (WRG)

## Key Mechanics

- **Pack Tactics**: Bonuses when attacking with total power 6+
- **Wolf/Werewolf Synergy**: Tribal overlap
- **Token Generation**: Many create wolf tokens
- **Combat Focus**: Attack-oriented abilities

## Signature Cards

### Lords and Payoffs
- Master of the Wild Hunt — Creates wolves, fight ability
- Tolsimir, Friend to Wolves — ETB fight, lifegain
- Nightpack Ambusher — Flash, wolf each end step

### Token Generators
- Howl of the Night Pack — Wolf per forest
- Wolfbriar Elemental — Multikicker tokens
- Kessig Cagebreakers — Wolves on attack

### Support
- Howlpack Resurgence — Flash and trample
- Raised by Wolves — Aura that makes tokens

## Common Commanders

- **Tolsimir, Friend to Wolves** — Selesnya wolf tribal
- **Tovolar, Dire Overlord** — Gruul wolves/werewolves
- **Rin and Seri, Inseparable** — Cats and dogs (wolf adjacent)

## Synergy Partners

Wolves pair well with:
- **Werewolves** — Tribal overlap with Tovolar
- **Tokens** — Go-wide strategies
- **Aggro** — Combat-focused
- **Fight** — Tolsimir theme
