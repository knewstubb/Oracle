# Zombies

## Overview

Zombies are Magic's premier recursive tribe, designed to come back from the dead over and over. Zombie tribal in Commander excels at grinding value through sacrifice loops, graveyard recursion, and inevitability — opponents kill your zombies, and they just return.

## Tribal Identity

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional recursion and graveyard synergy
- Strong sacrifice payoffs
- Multiple lords available
- Great overlap with aristocrats
- Resilient to removal

**Weaknesses:**
- Vulnerable to graveyard hate
- Generally slower than other tribes
- Most zombies are groundbound
- Can be mana-intensive to recur

**Typical Colors:** Black (primary), Blue (mill synergy), White (tokens)

## Unique Mechanics

### Recursion Themes
Zombies have unique "undying" flavor:
- [[Gravecrawler]] — Recast from graveyard infinitely
- [[Relentless Dead]] — Bring back other zombies
- [[Prized Amalgam]] — Returns automatically
- [[Undead Augur]] — Draw on zombie death

### Zombie Token Generation
Many zombies create more zombies:
- [[Army of the Damned]] — Thirteen zombies
- [[Grave Titan]] — Two zombies on ETB/attack
- [[Liliana, Dreadhorde General]] — Zombies on creature death
- [[Ghoulcaller Gisa]] — Sacrifice for zombie swarm

### Play Patterns

Zombie tribal is grind-oriented:
1. **Early game** — Deploy value zombies, fill graveyard
2. **Mid game** — Sacrifice loops, recursive threats
3. **Late game** — Overwhelming board through inevitability

## Building Tips

1. **Include sacrifice outlets** — Gravecrawler loops need free sac outlets
2. **Graveyard filling** — Self-mill helps recursion plans
3. **Graveyard protection** — [[Perpetual Timepiece]], [[Elixir of Immortality]]
4. **Don't overload on lords** — 4-5 lords is plenty
5. **Include finishers** — Gray Merchant, Torment of Hailfire close games

## Common Pitfalls

1. **No sacrifice outlets** — Recursion without sac outlets is slow
2. **Ignoring graveyard hate** — [[Rest in Peace]] shuts down the deck
3. **Too many lords** — Need zombies to boost, not just boosters
4. **No card advantage** — Zombies need to keep flowing
5. **Missing combo pieces** — Gravecrawler loops are powerful; include them

## Synergy Partners

Zombies pair well with:
- **Aristocrats** — Natural sacrifice synergy
- **Mill** — Fill graveyard for recursion
- **Reanimator** — Already in graveyard colors
- **+1/+1 Counters** — [[Mikaeus]] synergy

## Meta Considerations

- **Graveyard hate** — Devastating; run protection or recursion
- **Exile removal** — Worse than regular removal for zombies
- **Lifegain** — Counteracts drain strategies
- **Flying** — Most zombies are ground-bound
- **Board wipes** — Less impactful due to recursion

## Gravecrawler Combos

[[Gravecrawler]] enables infinite loops with:
- **Phyrexian Altar** — Infinite mana (need another zombie)
- **Carrion Feeder + Blood Artist** — Infinite drain
- **Altar of Dementia** — Infinite mill
- **Diregraf Captain** — Infinite damage

Key: Need a free sacrifice outlet and a death/cast trigger payoff.
