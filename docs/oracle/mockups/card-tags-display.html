How to review. The card window is not part of this mockup: Tags in it moved to the card details redesign. Use "Collection filter state" to open the Tag filter: pick Tags, switch between "all of them" and "any of them", and combine with colour and Unplaced. "Empty result" shows what you see when nothing matches. "Phone" narrows the frame (the filter becomes a bottom sheet). "Greyscale" proves nothing depends on colour. Rows show no Tags on purpose. The 40 Tags and their order are the real list; the cards, colours and statuses are samples.
