Sample data only. Hover and the open rows are shown at rest.

3 · Printing picker (reusable for any card on the review screen)

Opens from “Choose art” (generic), “Choose printing” or “Change printing”. Newest first; each row shows set, number, year and which finishes exist. A finish the printing lacks is struck through; picking it keeps the row’s finish if available, otherwise switches and shows the ↻ marker. Image only: you don’t need to own the printing. “Default art” is a printing from your collection, picked once and kept.

4 · Deck page: generic line (sketch)

No swap feature. To trade a generic for a specific land, remove the specific line and raise the generic count, or the reverse. A generic line has: count − / +, a Sleeved / Unsleeved control for the whole line, Remove, and “Add generic basic”. Sleeving is all or nothing.
