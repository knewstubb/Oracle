Deck health (new, HC-01 and HC-02). The rail's Deck health section now shows six rows with the Command Zone ranges, using the real counts from the audit for two decks: Yedora the Explorer and A rot to process (pick one under "Health numbers for"). Select any row, or the headline, to open the details panel (a side drawer on desktop, a bottom sheet on a phone). The other switches show states the real decks do not have today: cards without text, double-faced land cards, loading and failed checks, and, as a future state only, a proposed range from Claude. The rest of the rail (cards, art, mana curve) is still the Yedora sample. Sample card names in the panel are placeholders.

How to review the Strategy window and card Tags. Pick a Strategy state, then open the window from the Strategy block in the left rail. Loading, Failed and Offline open the window for you. Tab, Shift+Tab and Escape work as in the app. "Phone" narrows the frame so the rail stacks and collapses; "Greyscale" proves nothing depends on colour alone. All names and numbers are sample data for Yedora, the Explorer. The mockup's calculated bracket is fixed at 4 (4 Game Changers), so a target of 3 shows the warning and a target of 4 or 5 shows nothing.
