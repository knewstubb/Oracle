Oracle · Shared words, colours and badges

How to read this page

Each card or deck fact has one word, one icon and one colour, used on every screen. Colour is only used where you may need to act (proxy, in another deck, not owned, a check that failed). Everything normal stays grey. A solid badge means a real copy is sleeved in the deck. An outlined badge means the deck still needs a card in that slot. Use Today (before) at the top to compare with the app today, and Greyscale check to confirm every state can still be told apart without colour.

7 · Questions for you

1. Should a sleeved card's badge just say Original or Proxy?Recommended: yes. The solid badge and the "Sleeved" heading already show it's in the deck (see section 3).
2. Until the app can work out Draft, Assembling and Playable, should deck tiles drop Active/Inactive and just show "87/100 sleeved"?Recommended: yes. Later, Playable decks sort first, then Assembling, then Draft.
3. Should Playable stay calm (a white tick, no colour)?Recommended: yes. Colour is saved for things you need to act on.
4. Does the teal Proxy colour ever look like a blue card?Recommended: keep teal, always with the mask icon. If it blurs, make proxies grey with the mask icon instead.
5. Should normal cards show no finish tag in busy lists?Recommended: yes. Only Foil and Etched are tagged.
6. Should the collection say "Short 2" (like Reconcile) instead of "Over-allocated"?Recommended: yes.
