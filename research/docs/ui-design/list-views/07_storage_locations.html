Storage Locations View

Layout: Card grid for locations, table/list for contents

Design Rationale: Storage locations are "as real as a deck" — they represent physical containers (binders, boxes, folders). Card grid for browsing locations (like Decks list). When you click into a location, you see its contents as a searchable table. This answers "What's in my Staples Binder?" and "Where should I put this card?"

Key Interactions

  • • Location icons: User-selectable or auto-assigned based on type (binder, box, folder)

  • • Kebab menu: Move to deck, Move to different storage, Change printing, Remove
  • • Drag & drop: Future enhancement — drag cards between storage locations
  • • Quick add: "Add cards to this location" button for intake workflow
