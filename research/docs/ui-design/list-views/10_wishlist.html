Key Behaviors

Auto-removal

When a wishlist card is added to Collection (via "Mark Owned" or import), it's automatically removed from Wishlist.

Deck links

"Decks Wanting" column links to each deck. "View Picklist" jumps directly to that deck's action queue.

Source tracking

Cards are tagged by how they arrived: Deck need, Unowned proxy, Manual add, or Imported. Helps prioritize purchases.

Export for purchase

"Export Buy List" generates TCGPlayer/CardKingdom compatible format for easy checkout.
